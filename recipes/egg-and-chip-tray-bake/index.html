Egg and chip tray bake

Simple comfort food made easier by cooking the chips and eggs together in one tray.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • Low-calorie cooking spray
  • 1kg floury potatoes,
  • such as Maris Piper or King Edward,
  • sliced into chips
  • 4 eggs
  • Chopped fresh parsley,
  • to serve

Method

  1. Preheat your oven to 220°C/fan 200°C/gas 7 and spray a non-stick baking tray with low-calorie cooking spray.
  2. Cook the chips in a saucepan of boiling water over a high heat for 5 minutes.
  3. Drain well, return to the pan and cover.
  4. Leave to cool slightly, then shake the pan to roughen the edges.
  5. Spread the chips out on the baking tray and spray with low-calorie cooking spray.
  6. Cook at the top of the oven for 25 minutes or until the chips are golden, turning occasionally.
  7. Take the tray out of the oven, make 4 wells among the chips and break an egg into each.
  8. Return to the oven for another 5 minutes or until everything is cooked to your liking.
  9. Season to taste, scatter over the parsley and serve with plenty of salad.
