Syn Free One-Pot Cheeseburger Pasta

A hearty one-pot pasta with lean beef mince, tomato, melted mature Cheddar and tangy chopped pickles for cheeseburger-inspired flavour.

Ingredients

  • 455g extra-lean beef mince
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 medium tomatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp mustard powder
  • Pinch of salt and black pepper
  • ½ tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp granulated sweetener or brown sugar alternative
  • 300g dried penne pasta
  • 960ml chicken stock
  • 2–3 tbsp pickles or gherkins, finely chopped
  • 120g mature Cheddar cheese, grated
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Low-calorie cooking spray

Method

  1. 1. In a small bowl, mix together the paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, mustard powder, salt and black pepper.
  2. 2. Lightly spray a large, deep pan with cooking spray and place over a medium-high heat. Add the beef mince and onion and cook until the mince is browned and the onion softened, breaking up the mince with a spoon.
  3. 3. Add the seasoning, tomatoes, tomato purée, Worcestershire sauce and sweetener. Stir well to coat the beef.
  4. 4. Stir in the dried penne, then pour in the chicken stock. Bring to the boil, cover and reduce to a simmer for 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the pasta is nearly tender. Add a little extra hot water or stock if needed.
  5. 5. Remove the lid and stir in the chopped pickles and most of the grated Cheddar. Cook and stir until the cheese has melted and the sauce coats the pasta. Check the beef is thoroughly cooked and the pasta tender.
  6. 6. Sprinkle with fresh parsley and the reserved Cheddar. Serve immediately, with a side salad if desired.
