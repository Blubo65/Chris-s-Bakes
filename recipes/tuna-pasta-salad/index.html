Tuna pasta salad

Tasty tuna, crunchy carrot and sweetcorn seasoned with herbs and black pepper.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 350g dried pasta shells
  • 200g can sweetcorn,
  • drained
  • 200g can tuna in brine,
  • drained
  • 1 medium carrot,
  • peeled and coarsely grated
  • 1 small red onion,
  • finely chopped
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • For the dressing:
  • 1 tsp mustard powder
  • 300g fat-free natural yogurt
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • ¼ tsp turmeric

Method

  1. Cook the pasta according to the pack instructions and drain.
  2. Cool under cold running water, drain again and tip the pasta into a large bowl.
  3. Meanwhile, mix all the dressing ingredients in a bowl.
  4. Add the sweetcorn, tuna, carrot and red onion to the pasta.
  5. Pour over the dressing, season to taste and mix well.
