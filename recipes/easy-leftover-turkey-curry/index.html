Easy Leftover Turkey Curry

A quick mild curry that turns cooked leftover turkey into a warming meal with tomatoes and yoghurt.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g cooked turkey,
  • diced or shredded
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 150 ml chicken or turkey stock
  • 100 g natural yoghurt
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh coriander,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a saucepan and cook the onion for 5 minutes until softened.
  2. Add the garlic, ginger and curry powder and stir for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the chopped tomatoes and stock.
  4. Simmer for 10 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  5. Stir in the cooked turkey and simmer gently for 5 minutes until piping hot throughout.
  6. Remove from the heat and stir in the yoghurt.
  7. Season to taste and garnish with coriander if desired.
  8. Serve with rice or flatbreads.
  9. Reheat leftover turkey only once.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Easy Leftover Turkey Curry recipe. Use safely stored cooked turkey and reheat until piping hot. Source: https://www.easypeasyfoodie.com/easy-leftover-turkey-curry
