Cottage Pie with Swede Mash Topping

A hearty, lighter cottage pie with savoury lean beef and vegetables under a smooth swede mash topping. Serve with green beans and broccoli.

Serves: 4 | Prep: 10 minutes | Cook: 55 minutes | Total: 1 hour 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1kg swede, peeled and chopped
  • Low-calorie cooking spray
  • 1 red onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 celery sticks, finely chopped
  • 2 carrots, peeled and finely chopped
  • 500g lean beef mince (5% fat or less)
  • 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp vegetable stock
  • 1 level tsp sweetener
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 egg, lightly beaten
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Cook the swede in boiling water for 12–15 minutes, or until tender. Drain well, return to the pan and mash until smooth. Season lightly and set aside.
  2. Spray a large non-stick frying pan with low-calorie cooking spray. Add the onion, garlic, celery and carrots and stir-fry for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the lean beef mince and cook for about 5 minutes, breaking it up with a spoon, until browned.
  4. Stir in the chopped tomatoes, stock, sweetener and oregano. Bring to the boil, then remove from the heat. Season to taste.
  5. Preheat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan/gas mark 6). Transfer the beef mixture to an ovenproof dish and spread the mashed swede over the top. Roughen with a fork if desired.
  6. Brush the topping with beaten egg. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until lightly golden and bubbling. Serve with green beans and broccoli.

Tips

  • Drain the swede thoroughly to avoid a watery topping.
  • Make sure the centre is piping hot before serving.
