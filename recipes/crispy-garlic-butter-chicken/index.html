Crispy Garlic Butter Chicken

Golden chicken pieces finished in a buttery garlic sauce with herbs.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 30 g butter
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with cornflour, paprika, garlic granules, salt and pepper until lightly coated.
  2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan.
  3. Cook the chicken in batches for 5–7 minutes, turning, until golden and cooked through.
  4. Reduce the heat and add the butter and garlic.
  5. Cook for 30–45 seconds, stirring so the garlic does not burn.
  6. Add the lemon juice and parsley, then toss the chicken in the garlic butter until coated.
  7. Check the chicken reaches 75°C internally and serve immediately with potatoes or vegetables.
