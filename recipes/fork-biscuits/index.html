Fork Biscuits

Old-fashioned, buttery fork biscuits made with just three basic ingredients.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 12

Ingredients

  • 115 g softened butter or margarine
  • 55 g caster sugar
  • 140 g self-raising flour

Method

  1. Beat the softened butter until smooth, then gradually beat in the caster sugar.
  2. Stir in the self-raising flour until the mixture comes together into a soft dough.
  3. If needed, finish bringing it together with your hands.
  4. Roll into walnut-sized balls and place on a small piece of baking parchment suitable for your air fryer, leaving room for spreading.
  5. Dip a fork in water and gently press each ball to flatten it.
  6. Air fry at 140°C for about 12–16 minutes, checking early as air fryers vary.
  7. The biscuits should be pale golden and feel firmer at the edges.
  8. Leave on the tray for a few minutes to firm up, then transfer carefully to a rack to cool completely.

Recipe notes

Adapted from the saved video recipe. The original excerpt suggests 140°C for 16 minutes; check early because air fryer models and biscuit size vary. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rNAbNx5p_yA
