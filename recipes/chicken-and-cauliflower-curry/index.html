Chicken & Cauliflower Curry

A lighter curry with chicken and cauliflower in a warmly spiced tomato sauce.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast or thighs,
  • diced
  • 1 medium cauliflower,
  • cut into small florets
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 200 ml chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh coriander,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan.
  2. Cook the onion for 5 minutes until soft.
  3. Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder and cumin and stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the chicken and stir for 3–4 minutes until the outside is no longer pink.
  5. Add the tomatoes, stock and cauliflower.
  6. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  7. Cover and cook for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the cauliflower is tender and chicken is cooked through.
  8. Add a splash of water if the sauce gets too thick.
  9. Season to taste and garnish with coriander if desired.
  10. Serve with rice.

Recipe notes
