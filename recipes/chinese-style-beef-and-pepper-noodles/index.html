Chinese-Style Beef & Pepper Noodles

A quick noodle dish with beef, peppers and a savoury Chinese-inspired sauce.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 250 g egg noodles
  • 400 g lean beef steak,
  • thinly sliced
  • 1 red pepper, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce, optional,
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Cook the noodles according to the packet, drain and set aside.
  2. Toss the beef with 1 tbsp soy sauce and the cornflour.
  3. Heat the oil in a wok.
  4. Stir-fry the beef in batches until browned and cooked through, then set aside.
  5. Add the pepper and onion and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes.
  6. Add garlic and ginger and cook for 30 seconds.
  7. Return the beef to the wok.
  8. Add the remaining soy sauce, oyster sauce if using and a splash of water.
  9. Toss with the noodles until hot and evenly coated.
  10. Season with black pepper and serve.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Chinese Pasta with Beef and Pepper recipe. Source: https://cookingwithlei.com/chinese-pasta
