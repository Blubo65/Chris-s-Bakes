Apple Pie

A small golden apple pie filled with cinnamon-scented apples, baked in the air fryer.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 6

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet ready-rolled shortcrust pastry,
  • about 320 g
  • 3 medium apples, peeled,
  • cored and diced
  • 40 g caster sugar,
  • to taste
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 egg, beaten, for glazing

Method

  1. Mix the diced apples with sugar, cinnamon, lemon juice and cornflour in a saucepan.
  2. Cook gently for 5–8 minutes until just beginning to soften and the juices thicken.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 160°C.
  5. Line a small air-fryer-safe pie tin with pastry, leaving enough for a lid.
  6. Spoon the cooled apple filling into the pastry case.
  7. Cover with pastry, press and seal the edges, then cut a small steam vent in the top.
  8. Brush with beaten egg.
  9. Bake for 25–35 minutes, checking from 20 minutes, until the pastry is cooked and golden.
  10. Cover loosely if the top browns too quickly.
  11. Leave to stand for 10–15 minutes before slicing.
  12. Make sure the tin fits with space for air to circulate.

Recipe notes

Practical air fryer adaptation inspired by the saved apple pie videos. Use a tin suitable for your appliance and check the pastry base is fully cooked. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eBDjLQt5oa8
