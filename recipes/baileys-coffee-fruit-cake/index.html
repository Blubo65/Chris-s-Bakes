Baileys Coffee fruit cake

This moist fruit cake combines bold coffee, creamy Baileys, and warm spices for a deep, comforting flavor. Perfect for cozy evenings or festive gatherings.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (250 ml) strong black coffee
  • 1/2 cup (125 ml) Baileys Irish Cream
  • 500 g mixed dried fruit
  • 1/2 cup (110 g) brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup (125 g) unsalted butter, chopped
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups (225 g) self-raising flour
  • 1 teaspoon mixed spice
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Method

  1. Grease and line two 1 lb loaf tins with baking paper, leaving a little paper over the ends to make the cakes easier to lift out.
  2. Grease and line two 1 lb loaf tins with baking paper, leaving a little paper over the ends to make the cakes easier to lift out.
  3. In a saucepan, combine coffee, Baileys, dried fruit, brown sugar, and butter. Heat gently until the butter melts and the mixture begins to simmer. Let it simmer for 5 minutes, then remove from heat and allow to cool completely.
  4. Once the fruit mixture is completely cool, transfer it to a large mixing bowl.
  5. Add the eggs and vanilla extract.
  6. Mix well until thoroughly combined.
  7. Sift in the self-raising flour, mixed spice, cinnamon and nutmeg.
  8. Fold gently until all the flour has been incorporated and you have a thick fruit-filled batter. DO NOT OVERMIX.
  9. Divide the batter evenly between the two prepared 1 lb loaf tins.
  10. The tins should be approximately ⅔–¾ full.
  11. Smooth the tops.
  12. Preheat your air fryer to 150°C.
  13. Place one loaf tin in the air fryer basket.
  14. Bake at:
  15. 150°C for 20 minutes
  16. Then reduce the temperature to:
  17. 140°C for a further 30–45 minutes
  18. After about 25 minutes at 140°C, loosely cover the top with foil if it is becoming too brown.
  19. Insert a skewer into the centre of the cake.
  20. It should come out clean or with a few moist crumbs attached.
  21. If there is wet batter on the skewer, continue cooking at 140°C in 5-minute intervals until cooked through.
  22. Leave each cake in its tin for 15–20 minutes.
  23. Carefully lift the cake out using the baking-paper lining and place it on a wire rack.
  24. While the cakes are still warm, lightly brush the tops with 2–3 tablespoons of Baileys.
  25. Let the Baileys soak into the cakes.
  26. Don't pour it over all at once—brush it on gradually.
  27. Allow the cakes to cool completely.
  28. Wrap them well and leave them overnight before slicing.
  29. The cakes will become more moist and the Baileys flavour will develop as they rest.
