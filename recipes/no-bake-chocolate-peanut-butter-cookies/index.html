No-Bake Chocolate Peanut Butter Cookies

Chocolatey no-bake oat cookies with peanut butter, made on the stovetop and left to set.

Time: 20 mins plus setting · Makes 12

Ingredients

  • 100 g butter
  • 150 g caster sugar
  • 50 ml milk
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 100 g smooth peanut butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200 g rolled oats
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Line a tray with baking parchment.
  2. Put the butter, sugar, milk and cocoa powder in a saucepan.
  3. Heat gently, stirring, until the butter melts and the mixture comes to a full boil.
  4. Boil for 1 minute while stirring, then remove from the heat.
  5. Stir in the peanut butter, vanilla, salt and oats until evenly combined.
  6. Drop spoonfuls onto the lined tray and flatten slightly.
  7. Leave at room temperature for 30–60 minutes until set, or chill to speed up the process.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved no-bake chocolate peanut butter cookies bookmark. Keep in an airtight container once set. Source: https://grandmasthing.com/chocolate-peanut-butter-no-bake-cookies/2
