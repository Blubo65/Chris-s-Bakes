Orzo with Peas and Roasted Butternut Squash

A comforting, creamy orzo pasta dish. A simple, delicious vegetarian meal packed with flavour and perfect for a midweek dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbs garlic-infused olive oil (or regular olive oil and 1 fat clove of garlic)
  • 1 butternut squash
  • 150g frozen petit pois
  • 250g orzo pasta
  • 1 tbs soft unsalted butter
  • 2 tbs grated Parmesan
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cut the butternut squash into small cubes. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Roast for around 30 minutes or until tender.
  2. Meanwhile, warm the oil in a large pan.
  3. Cook the garlic gently for one minute, then add the peas and stir for a couple of minutes.
  4. Add the orzo pasta and stir. Pour in 625ml (2½ cups) boiling water.
  5. Turn down the heat and simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking. Add a little more water if needed.
  6. Once the pasta is soft and the water absorbed, beat in the butter and Parmesan.
  7. Add the roasted butternut squash, check the seasoning and serve immediately in warm bowls.
