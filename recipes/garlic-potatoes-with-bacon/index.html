Garlic Potatoes with Bacon

Crispy potato cubes tossed with garlic, smoky bacon and herbs.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 800 g potatoes,
  • cut into 2 cm cubes
  • 150 g smoked bacon, chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves,
  • finely chopped
  • 1 tsp dried mixed herbs
  • Black pepper
  • Chopped parsley,
  • optional

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  2. Pat the potato cubes dry and toss with olive oil, herbs and black pepper.
  3. Air fry for 15 minutes, shaking the basket halfway.
  4. Add the chopped bacon and continue cooking for 7–10 minutes, shaking once, until the potatoes are crisp and bacon is cooked.
  5. Add the garlic for the final 2 minutes, tossing carefully so it does not burn.
  6. Check the potatoes are tender, garnish with parsley if desired and serve hot.

Recipe notes

Home adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Garlic Potatoes with Bacon bookmark. Adjust timing to potato size and your air fryer. Source: https://recipethis.com/air-fryer-garlic-potatoes-with-bacon/
