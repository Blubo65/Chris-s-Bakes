Garlic Potatoes with Bacon

Golden air fryer potato chunks tossed with garlic, olive oil and crispy bacon—a hearty side dish with plenty of flavour.

Ingredients

  • 4 large potatoes (or 6 medium), peeled and cubed
  • 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 6 bacon slices, chopped
  • 6 whole garlic cloves, peeled
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Peel and cube the potatoes. Slice the bacon and peel the garlic cloves. Place them all in a mixing bowl.
  2. Add the extra-virgin olive oil, salt and pepper. Mix well with your hands so the potatoes are evenly coated.
  3. Place the mixture in the air fryer basket and cook at 180°C for 12 minutes.
  4. Shake the basket, increase the temperature to 200°C and cook for a further 5 minutes, or until the potatoes are golden, tender and cooked through and the bacon is crisp. Cooking times can vary by air fryer, so check the potatoes before serving.
