Rock Cakes

Traditional fruit-studded rock cakes with a lightly crisp outside and soft crumb.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 225 g self-raising flour
  • 75 g cold butter,
  • cubed
  • 75 g caster sugar
  • 100 g mixed dried fruit
  • 1 tsp mixed spice
  • 1 medium egg,
  • beaten
  • 1–2 tbsp milk,
  • as needed

Method

  1. Rub the cold butter into the flour until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs.
  2. Stir in the sugar, dried fruit and mixed spice.
  3. Add the beaten egg and enough milk to make a stiff, rough dough.
  4. Divide into 8 rough heaps and place on a small piece of baking parchment suitable for the air fryer, leaving space between them.
  5. Air fry at 160°C for 12–16 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  6. Check early as models vary; cook in batches if necessary.
  7. Leave for a few minutes to firm up, then cool on a rack.

Recipe notes

Practical air fryer adaptation inspired by the saved rock cakes video. Times may vary with the size of the cakes and your appliance. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jxpOR_yF39w
