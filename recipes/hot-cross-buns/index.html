Hot Cross Buns

Soft, warmly spiced hot cross buns with optional raisins or currants, flour crosses and a golden syrup glaze.

Time: Approx. 20–25 mins cooking, plus proving · Makes 8 small buns

Ingredients

  • 300g plain flour (about 2½ cups)
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 75g brown sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp mixed spice
  • 5g dried yeast
  • 35g softened butter
  • 1 tbsp vanilla essence
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 120ml warm milk
  • 60ml warm water,
  • approximately
  • 1 egg
  • 40g raisins or currants (optional)
  • For the crosses:
  • ¼ tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • Water,
  • a little at a time
  • For the glaze:
  • 1 tbsp hot water
  • 1 tbsp golden syrup

Method

  1. Mix the flour, baking powder, brown sugar, salt, mixed spice, yeast and cinnamon in a bowl.
  2. Rub or mix in the softened butter.
  3. Add the vanilla, warm milk and egg, then add enough warm water to make a soft dough.
  4. Knead for about 8–10 minutes until smooth, then knead in the raisins or currants if using.
  5. Cover and leave in a warm place until doubled in size, usually about 1–1½ hours.
  6. Divide into 8 pieces, shape into buns and place spaced apart in a lined air-fryer-safe tin.
  7. Cover and prove again until puffy, about 30–45 minutes.
  8. Mix the cross ingredients with just enough water to make a pipeable paste.
  9. Pipe crosses over the buns.
  10. Air fry on the BAKE setting at 160°C, checking from about 15 minutes; cook until golden and the centres are baked through.
  11. Depending on your air fryer and tin, this may take around 15–25 minutes.
  12. If the tops brown too quickly, cover loosely with foil.
  13. Do not overcrowd; bake in batches if necessary.
  14. Mix the hot water and golden syrup and brush over the buns as soon as they come out.
  15. Cool slightly before serving.
