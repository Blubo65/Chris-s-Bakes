Paneer Tikki

Spiced paneer and potato patties with a crisp outside, cooked in the air fryer.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 200 g paneer,
  • grated or crumbled
  • 250 g boiled potato,
  • mashed
  • 1 small onion,
  • finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp chilli powder, optional,
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Oil spray

Method

  1. Mix the paneer, mashed potato, onion, coriander, cumin, garam masala, chilli powder, cornflour, salt and pepper.
  2. Shape into 8 small patties and chill for 10 minutes if time allows.
  3. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  4. Lightly spray the patties with oil and place in a single layer.
  5. Air fry for 10–14 minutes, turning carefully halfway, until golden and crisp.
  6. Serve hot with mint yoghurt or chutney.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Paneer Tikki video. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U-DXKEoc18g
