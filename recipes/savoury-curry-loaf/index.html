Savoury Curry Loaf

A firm, savoury loaf flavoured with curry spices, useful sliced for lunches or snacks.

Time: 1 hr · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 200 g red lentils,
  • rinsed
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 carrot,
  • grated
  • 2 eggs
  • 100 g grated mature cheddar
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Salt and black pepper
  • A little oil for greasing

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Grease and line a 2 lb loaf tin.
  3. Simmer the lentils in water for 12–15 minutes until tender, then drain very well and allow to cool slightly.
  4. Mix the cooked lentils with onion, grated carrot, eggs, cheese, curry powder, baking powder, salt and pepper.
  5. Spoon into the tin and level the top.
  6. Bake for 35–45 minutes until firm and golden and set in the centre.
  7. Cool in the tin for 15 minutes before turning out and slicing.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved savoury curry loaf bookmarks. This version uses lentils and is a practical adaptation, not a verbatim source recipe. Sources: https://www.justaveragejen.com/curry-loaf.html and https://slimmingsurvival.com/blog/curry-loaf
