Curried Cauliflower Soup

A smooth, warming cauliflower soup flavoured with curry spices and finished with milk or yoghurt.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower,
  • cut into florets
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 750 ml vegetable stock
  • 150 ml milk or light coconut milk
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Lemon juice,
  • to taste

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan.
  2. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the garlic and curry powder and stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the cauliflower and vegetable stock.
  5. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 18–22 minutes until the cauliflower is very tender.
  6. Blend until smooth using a stick blender, taking care with the hot liquid.
  7. Stir in the milk or coconut milk and warm gently without boiling.
  8. Season with salt, pepper and a squeeze of lemon juice.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Curried Cauliflower Soup recipe. Source: https://www.slimmingeats.com/blog/curried-cauliflower-soup
