Egg, chip and pepper bake

A comforting twist on egg and chips with crispy potatoes and colourful peppers.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1kg Desiree potatoes
  • Low-calorie cooking spray
  • 1 red pepper, deseeded and thinly sliced
  • 1 yellow pepper, deseeded and thinly sliced
  • 4 eggs
  • Chopped fresh parsley,
  • to serve

Method

  1. Preheat your oven to 240°C/fan 220°C/gas 9. Peel the potatoes and cut them into 1cm-thick, long chips.
  2. Put them in a saucepan of boiling water and cook for 4–5 minutes.
  3. Drain carefully and spread on kitchen paper to dry.
  4. Put the chips in a shallow medium-size ovenproof dish or 4 individual gratin dishes.
  5. Spray with low-calorie cooking spray and bake for 10 minutes.
  6. Mix the pepper slices together and add to the potatoes, then continue to bake for another 15 minutes, turning occasionally.
  7. Remove the dish or dishes from the oven and make 4 wells in the chip mixture.
  8. Break an egg into each well and return to the oven for 5–6 minutes or until the eggs are cooked to your liking.
  9. Serve hot, seasoned and sprinkled with chopped parsley.
