School Cake

You can easily bake a nostalgic retro school cake right in your air fryer

Ingredients

  • 200g softened butter (or Stork)
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 200g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2–3 tbsp milk (if needed for a dropping consistency)
  • For the Icing: 200g icing sugar,
  • 2–3 tbsp water or milk, and a generous cup of multi-colored sprinkles (hundreds and thousands).

Method

  1. Mix the Batter: Beat the softened butter and caster sugar together in a large bowl until pale and fluffy. Add the eggs, vanilla extract, and self-raising flour, mixing gently until smooth. Add a splash of milk if the batter feels too thick.
  2. Prepare the Tins: Grease 2 air fryer-safe small cake tins or line it with baking parchment (ensure it fits comfortably inside your air fryer basket). Pour the batter in and smooth the top.
  3. Air Fry: Preheat your air fryer to 160°C. Bake at 160°C for 20 to 25 minutes (or up to 30 minutes depending on your air fryer model). Check with a skewer or toothpick inserted into the center—it should come out clean.
  4. Allow the sponge to cool completely on a wire rack.
  5. Mix the icing sugar with water or milk to form a thick, spreadable paste, spread it over the cake, and top immediately with sprinkles.
  6. Let it set before slicing into squares.
