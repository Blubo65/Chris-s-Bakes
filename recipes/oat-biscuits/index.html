Oat Biscuits

Crisp-edged oat biscuits with a gentle gingerbread-spice flavour, cooked in the air fryer.

Ingredients

  • 125g butter, softened
  • 1 free-range egg
  • 75g brown sugar
  • 125g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 100g rolled oats
  • 1 tsp gingerbread spice
  • A pinch of salt

Method

  1. Beat the softened butter, egg and brown sugar until creamy.
  2. Mix the plain flour with the baking powder, then add to the butter mixture with the rolled oats, gingerbread spice and salt. Mix until a dough forms.
  3. Divide the dough into 20–25 portions and roll into balls.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 160°C. Line the air fryer baking tray with suitable baking paper, ensuring it cannot lift into the heating element.
  5. Arrange 5–6 dough balls on the tray with plenty of space between them. Flatten each slightly.
  6. Air fry at 160°C for 8–10 minutes until golden brown. Check early as air fryers vary.
  7. Allow the biscuits to cool briefly, then transfer to a wire rack. Repeat with the remaining dough, working in batches.
  8. Cool completely before serving
  9. the biscuits will firm as they cool.
