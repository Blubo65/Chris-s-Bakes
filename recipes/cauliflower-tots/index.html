Cauliflower Tots

Small baked cauliflower bites with cheese and breadcrumbs, crisp outside and soft inside.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 400 g cauliflower florets
  • 1 egg
  • 75 g grated cheddar
  • 50 g breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Oil spray

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Steam or boil the cauliflower until tender, then drain very well and chop finely.
  3. Mix the cauliflower with the egg, cheddar, breadcrumbs, flour, garlic granules, salt and pepper.
  4. Shape into small cylinders or nuggets and place on a lined baking tray.
  5. Spray lightly with oil.
  6. Bake for 20–25 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and set.
  7. For an air fryer, cook at 190°C for about 12–16 minutes, turning carefully.
  8. Serve warm with a dip.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Cauliflower Tots bookmark. Drain the cauliflower thoroughly to help the tots hold together. Source: https://ketoox.com/cauliflower-tots/
