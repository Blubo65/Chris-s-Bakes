Crispy Gnocchi

Pillowy gnocchi tossed with oil and herbs, air fried until crisp outside and tender inside.

Time: 15 mins · Makes 3

Ingredients

  • 500 g shelf-stable or refrigerated potato gnocchi
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic granules
  • 1 tsp Italian herbs
  • 30 g grated Parmesan, optional,
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 200°C.
  2. Toss the gnocchi with olive oil, garlic granules, herbs, salt and pepper.
  3. There is no need to boil shelf-stable gnocchi first.
  4. Spread in a single layer in the basket.
  5. Air fry for 10–14 minutes, shaking the basket halfway, until golden and crisp outside.
  6. Toss with Parmesan if using and serve immediately with a dip or tomato sauce.

Recipe notes

Home adaptation inspired by the saved viral air fryer gnocchi video. If using a different type of gnocchi, follow the packet guidance and check early. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4RFkfFsECxo
