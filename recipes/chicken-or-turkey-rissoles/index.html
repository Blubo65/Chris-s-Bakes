Chicken or Turkey Rissoles

Juicy, flavourful rissoles for lunch or dinner.

Makes Makes 12

Ingredients

  • 500g minced chicken or turkey
  • 1 small onion,
  • finely grated
  • 1 small carrot,
  • finely grated
  • 2 cloves garlic,
  • minced
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 large egg
  • ½ cup (60g) breadcrumbs,
  • plus extra for coating
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Mix the mince, grated onion and carrot, garlic, parsley, Worcestershire sauce, mustard, egg and breadcrumbs.
  2. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Shape into 12 even rissoles.
  4. Coat lightly in extra breadcrumbs.
  5. Cook until golden and cooked through; ensure the centre reaches a safe temperature for poultry.
