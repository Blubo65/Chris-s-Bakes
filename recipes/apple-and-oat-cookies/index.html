Apple & Oat Cookies

Naturally sweet apple and oat cookies with honey, walnuts and flaxseeds, baked without flour or refined sugar.

Ingredients

  • 100 g rolled oats (about 1 cup)
  • 2 medium apples, peeled and grated
  • 1 tbsp honey (15 ml)
  • 2 tbsp chopped walnuts (suggested optional amount)
  • 1 tbsp flaxseeds (suggested optional amount)

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and line a large baking tray with baking parchment.
  2. Combine the oats, chopped apples and honey. Stir in walnuts and flaxseeds if using.
  3. Spoon the mixture into approximately 9 separate mounds on the tray and flatten each slightly into a cookie shape.
  4. Bake for 35–40 minutes at 180°C until golden and set.
  5. Leave to cool on the baking tray before transferring to a wire rack.
