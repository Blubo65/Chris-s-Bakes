Cheeseburger Wraps

A quick cheeseburger-inspired wrap filled with seasoned beef, cheese, pickles and burger sauce.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 400 g lean beef mince
  • 4 large tortilla wraps
  • 1 small onion,
  • finely chopped
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • 100 g grated cheddar
  • 4 gherkins,
  • sliced
  • 4 tbsp burger sauce or mayonnaise mixed with ketchup
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Cook the beef mince and onion in a frying pan over medium-high heat until browned and cooked through.
  2. Drain excess fat, then season with paprika, garlic granules, salt and pepper.
  3. Lay out the wraps and divide the beef between them.
  4. Add cheese, sliced gherkins and a little burger sauce.
  5. Fold the sides in and roll tightly into wraps.
  6. Place seam-side down in the air fryer basket; cook in batches if necessary.
  7. Air fry at 190°C for 5–8 minutes, turning once, until crisp and golden.
  8. Make sure the filling is piping hot.
  9. Serve with salad or air fryer chips.
