Cauliflower Cheese Spaghetti

A comforting pasta dish combining cauliflower, a creamy cheese sauce and spaghetti.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g spaghetti
  • 1 medium cauliflower,
  • cut into small florets
  • 30 g butter
  • 30 g plain flour
  • 500 ml milk
  • 120 g mature cheddar,
  • grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard, optional,
  • Salt and black pepper
  • A pinch of nutmeg,
  • optional

Method

  1. Cook the cauliflower in boiling water for 6–8 minutes until just tender.
  2. Lift out with a slotted spoon and reserve the water for the spaghetti if desired.
  3. Cook the spaghetti according to the packet instructions, then drain.
  4. Melt the butter in a saucepan.
  5. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  6. Gradually whisk in the milk until smooth.
  7. Simmer, stirring, for 4–6 minutes until thickened.
  8. Remove from the heat and stir in most of the cheese, mustard if using, pepper and nutmeg.
  9. Fold the cauliflower and spaghetti into the sauce.
  10. Transfer to an ovenproof dish, scatter with the remaining cheese and grill for 3–5 minutes until golden, or serve straight from the pan.

Recipe notes

Home adaptation inspired by the saved Jamie Oliver Cauliflower Cheese Spaghetti recipe; not a verbatim reproduction. Source: https://www.jamieoliver.com/recipes/pasta/cauliflower-cheese-spaghetti
