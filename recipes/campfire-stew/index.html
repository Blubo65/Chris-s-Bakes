Campfire Stew

A hearty smoky stew with gammon, beans, peppers and tomatoes, ideal for slow cooking.

Time: 6–8 hrs slow cooker · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g gammon joint,
  • visible fat trimmed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 x 400 g tins chopped tomatoes
  • 1 x 400 g tin baked beans
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp chilli flakes, optional,
  • 150 ml water or stock
  • Black pepper,
  • to taste

Method

  1. Put the onion, pepper, garlic, chopped tomatoes, baked beans, tomato purée, smoked paprika, chilli flakes if using and water or stock into the slow cooker.
  2. Stir together.
  3. Nestle the gammon joint into the sauce and spoon some over the top.
  4. Cover and cook on LOW for 6–8 hours or HIGH for 4–5 hours, until the gammon is tender and cooked through.
  5. Lift out the gammon and shred or chop it, removing any rind or excess fat.
  6. Return the meat to the stew and stir well.
  7. Taste and season with black pepper.
  8. Serve on its own or with rice or potatoes.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Campfire Stew recipe. Cooking time depends on joint size and slow cooker; ensure the gammon is fully cooked. Source: https://www.slimmingworld.co.uk/blog/eat-campfire-stew
