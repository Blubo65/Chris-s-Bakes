Jerk Pork & Pineapple Skewers

Juicy pork and sweet pineapple skewers coated in spicy Caribbean jerk seasoning.

Time: 30 mins plus marinating · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g pork tenderloin,
  • cut into 3 cm cubes
  • 250 g fresh pineapple,
  • cubed
  • 1 red pepper,
  • cut into chunks
  • 1 red onion,
  • cut into chunks
  • 1–2 tbsp jerk seasoning
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Juice of ½ lime

Method

  1. Toss the pork with jerk seasoning, oil and lime juice.
  2. Cover and marinate for at least 20 minutes, or longer in the refrigerator.
  3. Thread the pork, pineapple, pepper and onion onto skewers, alternating the ingredients.
  4. Heat a grill or barbecue to medium-high.
  5. Cook the skewers for 10–15 minutes, turning regularly, until the pork is cooked through and the vegetables are lightly charred.
  6. Check the pork is fully cooked, then rest for 2 minutes before serving with rice or salad.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved jerk pork and pineapple skewers recipe. Cooking time depends on pork cube size; ensure the pork is cooked through. Source: https://twochubbycubs.com/2018/06/01/pork-and-pineapple-skewers
