Turkish-Style Flatbread

Soft, quick flatbreads cooked in the air fryer, ideal for dipping or serving with curry and kebabs.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 250 g self-raising flour
  • 200 g thick natural yoghurt
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, optional,
  • Garlic butter or herbs,
  • optional

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yoghurt and salt in a bowl until a soft dough forms.
  2. Add a little extra flour if very sticky.
  3. Knead gently for 1 minute on a floured surface, then divide into 4 portions.
  4. Roll each portion into a flat round about 5 mm thick.
  5. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  6. Place one or two breads in the basket, allowing space for air circulation.
  7. Air fry for 5–8 minutes, turning once if needed, until puffed in places and cooked through.
  8. Brush with garlic butter or herbs if desired.

Recipe notes

Home adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Turkish Bread bookmark. Flour and yoghurt moisture vary; adjust the dough to a soft, workable consistency. Source: https://keto.cookingpoint.net/air-fryer-turkish-bread/
