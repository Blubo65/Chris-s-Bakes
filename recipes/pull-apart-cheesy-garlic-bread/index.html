Pull-Apart Cheesy Garlic Bread

Soft bread pieces coated with garlic butter and melted cheese, perfect for sharing.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 6

Ingredients

  • 1 small crusty loaf or 1 round sourdough loaf
  • 60 g butter,
  • softened
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 150 g grated mozzarella
  • 50 g grated mature cheddar
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Cut a crosshatch pattern into the loaf without cutting all the way through the base.
  3. Mix the butter with garlic, parsley and black pepper.
  4. Gently open the cuts and spread garlic butter into the gaps.
  5. Push the grated cheeses into the cuts.
  6. Wrap loosely in foil and bake for 10 minutes, then open the foil and bake for a further 8–10 minutes until the cheese is melted and the top is crisp.
  7. Serve warm for pulling apart at the table.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved pull-apart cheesy garlic bread bookmark. Source: http://www.slimmingeats.com/blog/syn-free-pull-apart-cheesy-garlic-bread
