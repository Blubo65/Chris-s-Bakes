Garlic Parmesan Potatoes

Crispy potato chunks tossed with garlic, onion seasoning and Parmesan.

Time: 20–25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 700–900g potatoes,
  • cut into 2.5cm chunks
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1½ tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • About 50g grated Parmesan

Method

  1. Cut the potatoes into 2.5cm chunks.
  2. Rinse to remove excess starch, then dry very thoroughly with a clean tea towel.
  3. Toss the potatoes with the salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, olive oil and Parmesan until evenly coated.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  5. Cook for 10 minutes, then shake the basket and stir the potatoes to redistribute the seasoning.
  6. Increase the temperature to 200°C and cook for a further 8–12 minutes, shaking once halfway through, until golden and crisp and tender in the centre.
  7. Check early as air fryers vary.
  8. Serve immediately.
