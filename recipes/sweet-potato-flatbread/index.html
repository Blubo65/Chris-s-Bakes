Sweet Potato Flatbread

Soft flatbreads made with mashed sweet potato and flour, ideal alongside curry or soup.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 250 g cooked sweet potato,
  • mashed and cooled
  • 200–250 g plain flour,
  • plus extra for dusting
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp ground cumin,
  • optional

Method

  1. Mix the mashed sweet potato with salt and cumin if using.
  2. Gradually add flour until a soft dough forms that can be handled without sticking excessively.
  3. The amount depends on how moist the potato is.
  4. Divide into 6–8 balls and roll each out thinly on a floured surface.
  5. Heat a dry frying pan over medium-high heat.
  6. Cook each flatbread for 1–2 minutes per side until browned in spots and cooked through.
  7. Keep the cooked flatbreads wrapped in a clean tea towel to stay soft.
  8. Serve warm.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Sweet Potato Flatbread bookmark. Add flour gradually as sweet potato moisture varies. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WRj0tPEjcBY
