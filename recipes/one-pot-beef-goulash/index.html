One-Pot Beef Goulash

A warming paprika-rich beef stew with peppers, tomatoes and tender chunks of beef.

Time: 1 hr 15 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g stewing beef,
  • cubed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds, optional,
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 500 ml beef stock
  • 2 potatoes,
  • cubed (optional)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a heavy casserole.
  2. Brown the beef in batches and set aside.
  3. Add the onion and pepper and cook for 5 minutes until softened.
  4. Stir in the garlic, paprika and caraway if using for 30 seconds.
  5. Return the beef to the pan and add the chopped tomatoes and stock.
  6. Bring to a simmer.
  7. Cover and cook gently on the hob for 60–75 minutes, or in the oven at 160°C (fan 140°C), until the beef is tender.
  8. Add the potatoes for the final 25–30 minutes if using.
  9. Season to taste and serve with rice, noodles or crusty bread.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved One Pan Goulash bookmark. Cooking time depends on the cut of beef; simmer until tender. Source: https://www.facebook.com/reel/2256244015125788/
