Mincemeat Loaf Cake (2 x 1 lb tins)

A moist, warmly spiced mincemeat loaf cake topped with crunchy almonds, baked in the air fryer in two 1 lb loaf tins.

Time: 45–60 mins · Makes 2 loaves · approx. 10–12 slices per loaf

Ingredients

  • 225g self-raising flour
  • 150g soft light brown sugar
  • 150g unsalted butter,
  • softened
  • 425g good-quality mincemeat
  • 2 medium eggs
  • 50g blanched almonds,
  • halved
  • Optional decoration:
  • 3 tbsp smooth apricot jam
  • Halved glacé cherries,
  • if desired

Method

  1. Grease and line two 1 lb loaf tins (approximately 20 x 11cm).
  2. Preheat the air fryer to 150°C for 3 minutes if your model requires preheating.
  3. Put the flour and brown sugar in a large mixing bowl.
  4. Add the softened butter, mincemeat and eggs.
  5. Beat with an electric hand mixer for 2–3 minutes until thoroughly combined.
  6. Divide the mixture equally between the tins and level the tops.
  7. Scatter the halved almonds over both cakes.
  8. Place the tins in the air fryer with space around them for air to circulate.
  9. Air fry at 150°C for 45–60 minutes.
  10. Start checking after 40–45 minutes.
  11. A skewer inserted into the centre should come out clean or with a few moist crumbs, but no wet batter.
  12. If the tops brown too quickly, loosely cover with foil, secured so it cannot lift into the heating element.
  13. If both tins do not fit with space around them, cook one at a time.
  14. The second batch may cook slightly faster in a preheated air fryer.
  15. Continue in 5-minute increments if the centre is still wet.
  16. Leave the cakes in their tins for 10 minutes, then turn out carefully onto a wire rack to cool completely.
  17. Optional: warm the apricot jam and brush over the cooled cakes.
  18. Decorate with halved glacé cherries if desired.

Recipe notes

Air fryer temperatures vary, so use the cooking time as a guide and check that the centres are fully cooked. Store completely cooled cakes in an airtight container; the flavour often improves the following day.
