Pizza Chicken

Chicken breasts topped with tomato sauce, mozzarella and Italian herbs for a pizza-inspired dinner.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 small chicken breasts
  • 150 g passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 garlic clove,
  • crushed
  • 100 g grated mozzarella
  • 20 g grated Parmesan, optional,
  • 8 slices pepperoni, optional,
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Place the chicken breasts in a lightly greased ovenproof dish and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Mix the passata with the garlic and oregano, then spoon over the chicken.
  4. Bake for 15–20 minutes depending on thickness, until the chicken is nearly cooked through.
  5. Top with mozzarella, Parmesan and pepperoni if using.
  6. Return to the oven for 5–8 minutes until the cheese melts and the chicken reaches 75°C internally.
  7. Rest for 3 minutes and serve with salad or vegetables.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Pizza Chicken recipe. Source: http://www.slimmingeats.com/blog/pizza-chicken
