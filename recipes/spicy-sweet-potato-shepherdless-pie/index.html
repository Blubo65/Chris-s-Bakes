Spicy Sweet Potato Shepherdless Pie

A warming vegetarian pie with a spiced lentil and vegetable filling under a sweet potato mash topping.

Time: 55 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 800 g sweet potatoes,
  • peeled and cubed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 carrot,
  • diced
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 1 x 400 g tin lentils,
  • drained
  • 1 x 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp chilli powder,
  • or to taste
  • 150 ml vegetable stock
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Boil the sweet potatoes for 15–18 minutes until tender, then drain and mash with salt and pepper.
  3. Meanwhile, soften the onion, carrot and pepper in a non-stick pan with a splash of water or a little oil if preferred.
  4. Stir in the cumin, paprika, chilli powder and tomato purée.
  5. Add the lentils, chopped tomatoes and stock.
  6. Simmer for 12–15 minutes until thickened, then season to taste.
  7. Spoon the filling into an ovenproof dish and spread the sweet potato mash over the top.
  8. Bake for 20–25 minutes until bubbling and lightly browned on top.
  9. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.
