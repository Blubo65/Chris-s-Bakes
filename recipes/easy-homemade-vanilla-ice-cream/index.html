Easy Homemade Vanilla Ice Cream

A simple creamy vanilla ice cream made without an ice-cream machine.

Time: 15 mins plus freezing · Makes 6

Ingredients

  • 600 ml double cream,
  • chilled
  • 1 x 397 g tin sweetened condensed milk,
  • chilled
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract

Method

  1. Pour the cold double cream into a large bowl and whip until it forms soft peaks; do not overwhip.
  2. In another bowl, stir the condensed milk with the vanilla extract.
  3. Fold the condensed milk mixture gently into the whipped cream until smooth and evenly combined.
  4. Spoon into a freezer-safe container and smooth the top.
  5. Cover and freeze for at least 6 hours, preferably overnight, until firm.
  6. Allow to stand at room temperature for 5–10 minutes before scooping.

Recipe notes

Simple home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved free ice cream bookmark; this version uses cream and sweetened condensed milk and is not a Slimming World free-food recipe. Source: https://www.slimmingworld.co.uk/recipes/free-ice-cream
