Carrot & Date Cake (two 1 lb loaf tins)

Rich sticky carrot and date loaf version.

Time: 30–35 mins · Makes 2 small loaves

Ingredients

  • 150g pitted dates
  • 150ml boiling water
  • 200g finely grated carrots
  • 125g melted butter
  • 2 medium eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ½ tsp ground ginger (optional)
  • 100g light brown sugar
  • 75g chopped walnuts
  • 1–2 tbsp milk,
  • if needed

Method

  1. Soak chopped dates in boiling water for 10 minutes; drain and mash.
  2. Finely grate the carrots.
  3. Mix date paste, melted butter, eggs and vanilla, then stir in carrots.
  4. Combine flour, raising agents, spices and sugar; fold into wet mixture with walnuts.
  5. Add milk only if needed.
  6. Divide between two greased and lined 1 lb loaf tins.
  7. Air fry at 160°C for 30–35 minutes; check from 30 minutes with a skewer.
  8. Cool in tins for 10 minutes before turning out.

Recipe notes

Optional cream-cheese frosting as in the round-cake version. Based on your two-loaf-tin adaptation; bake one tin at a time if needed for airflow.
