Broccoli & Cheddar Pasta

A quick, creamy pasta with tender broccoli and a cheddar cheese sauce.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g pasta
  • 1 medium broccoli,
  • cut into small florets
  • 30 g butter
  • 30 g plain flour
  • 450 ml milk
  • 120 g mature cheddar,
  • grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard, optional,
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Cook the pasta in boiling salted water according to the packet.
  2. Add the broccoli for the final 4–5 minutes of cooking, then drain and reserve a little pasta water.
  3. Melt the butter in a saucepan.
  4. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  5. Gradually whisk in the milk and simmer for 4–6 minutes, stirring until thickened.
  6. Remove from the heat and stir in the cheddar and mustard if using.
  7. Season with black pepper.
  8. Toss the cheese sauce with the pasta and broccoli.
  9. Loosen with a splash of reserved pasta water if needed and serve hot.

Recipe notes
