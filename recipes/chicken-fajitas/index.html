Chicken Fajitas

Juicy strips of seasoned chicken with sweet peppers and onion, ready for warm tortillas.

Time: 15–20 mins · Makes 2–3

Ingredients

  • 400g chicken breast,
  • cut into strips
  • 2 peppers,
  • deseeded and sliced
  • 1 red onion,
  • sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • ½ tsp ground coriander (optional)
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp chilli powder,
  • or to taste
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Tortillas,
  • salsa and yoghurt or sour cream,
  • to serve

Method

  1. Put the chicken, peppers and onion in a bowl.
  2. Add the oil, paprika, cumin, garlic, coriander if using, oregano, chilli powder, salt and pepper.
  3. Toss until everything is evenly coated.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C for 3 minutes if your model recommends preheating.
  5. Arrange the mixture in the basket in a fairly even layer; cook in batches if crowded.
  6. Air fry at 190°C for 12–16 minutes, shaking or stirring halfway through, until the vegetables are softened and the chicken is cooked through.
  7. Check the thickest pieces reach 75°C internally; cook a little longer if needed.
  8. Serve in warm tortillas with salsa and yoghurt or sour cream.

Recipe notes

Source saved in Raindrop: https://www.slimmingeats.com/blog/air-fryer-chicken-fajitas . Seasoning quantities and timing here are a practical adaptation; air fryers and strip thickness vary. Not yet tested by Chris.
