Carrot & Coconut Dhal

A comforting lentil dhal with sweet carrots, coconut milk and warming spices.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 200 g red lentils,
  • rinsed
  • 2 carrots,
  • diced small
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 400 ml light coconut milk
  • 400 ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Lemon juice,
  • to taste

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a saucepan and cook the onion and carrots for 5 minutes until starting to soften.
  2. Add the garlic, ginger, cumin and curry powder and stir for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the rinsed lentils, coconut milk and vegetable stock.
  4. Bring to a simmer.
  5. Cook gently for 20–25 minutes, stirring often, until the lentils break down and the carrots are tender.
  6. Add a splash of water if it becomes too thick.
  7. Season to taste and finish with a squeeze of lemon juice.
  8. Serve with rice or flatbreads.

Recipe notes
