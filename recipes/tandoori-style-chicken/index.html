Tandoori-Style Chicken

Yoghurt-marinated chicken flavoured with tandoori spices, cooked until lightly charred and juicy.

Time: 40 mins plus marinating · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 700 g boneless chicken thighs or breast,
  • in large pieces
  • 150 g natural yoghurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp chilli powder,
  • or to taste
  • Salt,
  • to taste

Method

  1. Mix the yoghurt, lemon juice, garlic, ginger, paprika, cumin, coriander, turmeric, garam masala, chilli and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add the chicken and coat thoroughly.
  3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, ideally 2–4 hours.
  4. Heat the oven to 220°C (fan 200°C), or preheat the air fryer to 190°C for smaller batches.
  5. Arrange the chicken on a lined tray or in the air fryer basket, allowing space between pieces.
  6. Roast for 20–30 minutes in the oven or air fry for about 15–20 minutes, turning halfway, until lightly charred and the thickest piece reaches 75°C internally.
  7. Rest for 5 minutes and serve with lemon wedges, salad and rice.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Tandoori Chicken link. Cooking times vary with piece size and appliance; ensure chicken reaches 75°C internally. Source: https://www.easypeasy-lemonsqueezy.co.uk/tandoori-chicken-slimming-style
