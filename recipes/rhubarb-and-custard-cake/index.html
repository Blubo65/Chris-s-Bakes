Rhubarb & Custard Cake

Soft custard-powder sponge with tangy rhubarb, sandwiched with custard buttercream.

Ingredients

  • 200–250g rhubarb, chopped
  • 25g caster sugar
  • 125g salted butter, softened
  • 125g caster sugar
  • 2 medium eggs
  • 100g self-raising flour
  • 25g custard powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2½ tbsp milk
  • 100g salted butter for buttercream
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp custard powder
  • 1–2 tbsp milk

Method

  1. Chop the rhubarb and toss with 25g caster sugar. Set aside.
  2. Cream 125g butter and 125g caster sugar until light. Beat in the eggs, then fold in the flour, custard powder, vanilla and milk.
  3. Divide the mixture between two greased and lined 5-inch round tins. Add the prepared rhubarb evenly.
  4. Air fry at 160°C for 18–22 minutes, checking from 16–18 minutes. Cover loosely with foil if the tops brown too quickly. At 150°C, allow about 22–28 minutes. Check the centre is cooked through.
  5. Cool completely. Beat the buttercream butter until soft, gradually beat in the icing sugar and custard powder, then add enough milk to make a spreadable filling.
  6. Sandwich the cakes together with the custard buttercream.

Recipe notes

Designed for two 5-inch round tins in the Ninja air fryer. Check the centres carefully because air fryer models and tin depth vary.
