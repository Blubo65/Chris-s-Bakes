Chip Shop Curry Sauce

A smooth, lightly spiced chip-shop-style curry sauce made with butternut squash, leek, onion and warming curry spices. Perfect over chips.

Ingredients

  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1 leek, finely chopped
  • 250 g butternut squash, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1–2 tbsp curry powder, to taste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce (or tamari for a gluten-free version)
  • 840 ml vegetable stock
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Low-calorie cooking spray

Method

  1. Lightly spray a large saucepan with low-calorie cooking spray and heat over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the chopped onion and leek and cook until softened.
  3. Stir in the garlic, ginger, diced butternut squash, curry powder and soy sauce to coat the vegetables.
  4. Pour in the vegetable stock and bring to the boil.
  5. Reduce the heat and simmer for about 30 minutes, until the squash is soft.
  6. Carefully blend the mixture until smooth, using a stick blender or a heat-safe blender.
  7. Return to the saucepan over medium heat, season with salt and black pepper, and simmer until thickened to your liking.
  8. If the sauce becomes too thick, loosen with a little extra stock.
  9. Serve hot over chips or alongside your favourite dishes.
