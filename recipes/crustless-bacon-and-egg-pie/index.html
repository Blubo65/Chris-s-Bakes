Crustless Bacon & Egg Pie

A simple baked egg pie with smoky bacon and cheese, without a pastry crust.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 6 medium eggs
  • 150 g smoked bacon, chopped
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 100 g grated cheddar
  • 150 ml milk
  • 1 tsp dried mixed herbs
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Lightly grease a medium ovenproof dish.
  3. Cook the bacon in a frying pan until browned.
  4. Add the onion and cook for 4 minutes until softened; cool slightly.
  5. Whisk the eggs with the milk, cheese, herbs and black pepper.
  6. Spread the bacon and onion in the dish and pour over the egg mixture.
  7. Bake for 25–35 minutes until set in the centre and golden.
  8. Rest for 5 minutes before slicing.

Recipe notes
