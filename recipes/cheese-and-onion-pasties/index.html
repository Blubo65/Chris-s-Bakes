Cheese & Onion Pasties

Golden pastry pasties filled with softened onion and mature cheese.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet ready-rolled shortcrust pastry,
  • about 320 g
  • 1 large onion,
  • finely chopped
  • 150 g mature cheddar,
  • grated
  • 1 medium potato,
  • peeled and diced small (optional)
  • 1 tsp mustard, optional,
  • Black pepper
  • 1 egg, beaten, for glazing

Method

  1. If using potato, boil the small dice for 6–8 minutes until just tender, then drain and cool.
  2. Cook the onion gently in a little oil or water for 8–10 minutes until soft but not browned.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. Mix the onion with the cheese, potato if using, mustard if desired and black pepper.
  5. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  6. Cut the pastry into 4 circles or squares.
  7. Divide the filling between them, leaving an edge clear.
  8. Brush the edges with beaten egg, fold over and crimp firmly to seal.
  9. Cut a small steam vent in each pasty and brush with egg.
  10. Bake for 25–30 minutes until golden and crisp.
  11. Allow to cool slightly before serving.
