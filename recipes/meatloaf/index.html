Meatloaf

A compact, juicy meatloaf with herbs and a tangy tomato glaze, cooked in the air fryer.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g lean beef mince
  • 1 small onion,
  • finely chopped
  • 1 egg
  • 50 g breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp garlic granules
  • 1 tsp dried mixed herbs
  • 3 tbsp ketchup,
  • divided
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Mix the mince, onion, egg, breadcrumbs, Worcestershire sauce, garlic granules, herbs, 1 tbsp ketchup, salt and pepper until combined without overworking.
  2. Shape into a compact loaf that fits your air fryer basket and place in a small lined air-fryer-safe tin or on perforated parchment.
  3. Preheat the air fryer to 160°C.
  4. Cook for 25–35 minutes, depending on thickness.
  5. Spread the remaining ketchup over the top for the final 8–10 minutes of cooking.
  6. Check the centre reaches at least 70°C for 2 minutes or follow current food-safety guidance for minced beef.
  7. Rest for 5–10 minutes before slicing.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved air fryer meatloaf bookmarks. Shape and tin size affect cooking time; confirm safe internal temperature. Source: https://cookathomemom.com/air-fryer-bbq-meatloaf
