Layered Mashed Potato Casserole

Layers of creamy white and sweet potato mash, topped with melted cheddar, crispy bacon and fresh chives.

Ingredients

  • 800 g peeled white potatoes, cubed
  • 600 g peeled sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 2 slices back bacon, visible fat removed, finely chopped
  • 90 g cheddar, grated
  • 2 large eggs
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Chicken or vegetable stock, enough to cover potatoes for boiling
  • Fresh chives, chopped, to serve
  • Low-calorie cooking spray, for frying

Method

  1. Put the cubed white potatoes into a saucepan and pour in enough stock to just cover. Bring to the boil, reduce to a simmer and cook until fork-tender.
  2. Drain well, season with salt and pepper, then mash until smooth with the eggs. Set aside.
  3. Put the cubed sweet potatoes into a separate saucepan and cover with stock. Bring to the boil, reduce to a simmer and cook until fork-tender.
  4. Drain well, season with salt and pepper, and mash until smooth.
  5. Preheat the oven to 180°C fan / 200°C conventional (Gas Mark 6).
  6. Spread the sweet potato mash evenly over the base of an ovenproof dish. Top with the white potato mash, smoothing each layer with the back of a spoon.
  7. Bake for 20 minutes.
  8. Meanwhile, spray a frying pan with low-calorie cooking spray and heat over medium-high heat. Fry the chopped bacon until crisp and golden, then set aside.
  9. Remove the casserole from the oven, sprinkle grated cheddar evenly over the top and return to the oven for another 10 minutes, or until the cheese has melted and is lightly golden.
  10. Scatter over the crispy bacon and freshly chopped chives before serving.
