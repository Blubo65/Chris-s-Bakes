McVitie’s-Style Ginger Nuts

Thin, crisp and spicy ginger biscuits with a classic ginger-nut snap, made in the Ninja AF410.

Time: 11–13 mins · Makes About 18 biscuits

Ingredients

  • 150g plain flour
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • ¼ tsp mixed spice
  • ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 50g butter,
  • softened
  • 3 tbsp golden syrup
  • Pinch of salt (omit if using salted butter)

Method

  1. Mix the softened butter and caster sugar until well combined; it does not need to be fluffy.
  2. Mix in the golden syrup.
  3. Stir in the flour, ground ginger, mixed spice, bicarbonate of soda and salt to make a firm dough.
  4. If the mixture is too dry to form a ball, add 1 tsp extra golden syrup at a time until it just comes together.
  5. Chill the dough for 30 minutes.
  6. Roll into small balls about 2–2.5 cm in diameter (roughly 12–15g each).
  7. Lightly roll the balls in caster sugar, then flatten to about 2.5–3mm thick.
  8. Place on the crisper plate lined with perforated baking parchment, leaving plenty of space between biscuits.
  9. Air fry in the Ninja AF410 at 150°C for 11–13 minutes.
  10. No flipping is needed.
  11. Check from 11 minutes; they should be deep golden brown.
  12. Turn the air fryer off and leave the biscuits inside for 3–5 minutes with the drawer closed to help them dry and crisp.
  13. Transfer to a rack and cool completely.
  14. They will become much firmer and develop their characteristic snap as they cool.

Recipe notes

For the best ginger-nut texture, keep the biscuits thin and evenly shaped. If your AF410 browns quickly, check from 10–11 minutes. Makes approximately 18 biscuits depending on size.
