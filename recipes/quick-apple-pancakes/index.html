Quick Apple Pancakes

Soft little pancakes with grated apple and cinnamon, perfect for breakfast or a snack.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 1 medium apple,
  • grated
  • 1 medium egg
  • 100 g plain flour
  • 120 ml milk
  • 1 tbsp sugar or sweetener, optional,
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • A little oil for the pan

Method

  1. Whisk the egg and milk together in a bowl.
  2. Stir in the flour, baking powder, cinnamon and sugar or sweetener if using until just combined.
  3. Fold in the grated apple.
  4. If the batter seems very thick, add a splash more milk.
  5. Heat a lightly oiled non-stick frying pan over medium-low heat.
  6. Spoon small rounds of batter into the pan.
  7. Cook for 2–3 minutes until bubbles appear and the edges set, then flip and cook for another 1–2 minutes until cooked through.
  8. Serve warm with yoghurt, fruit or a little syrup.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved quick apple pancakes video. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CZZ0xJHctQM
