Rhubarb Custard Bars

Sweet-tart rhubarb baked over a buttery base with a creamy custard-style filling.

Time: 1 hr plus cooling · Makes 12

Ingredients

  • 200 g plain flour
  • 75 g caster sugar
  • 125 g cold butter,
  • cubed
  • 300 g rhubarb,
  • diced
  • 150 g caster sugar,
  • for filling
  • 2 medium eggs
  • 150 g natural yoghurt or sour cream
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Line a 20 cm square tin with baking parchment.
  3. Rub 200 g flour, 75 g sugar and the cold butter together until crumbly.
  4. Press firmly into the tin and bake for 12–15 minutes until just beginning to colour.
  5. Mix the diced rhubarb with the filling sugar and spread over the warm base.
  6. Whisk the eggs with the yoghurt or sour cream, 2 tbsp flour and vanilla until smooth.
  7. Pour evenly over the rhubarb.
  8. Bake for 30–35 minutes until the custard is set in the centre.
  9. Cool completely, then chill before cutting into bars.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Rhubarb Custard Bars bookmark. Allow to cool and chill fully before slicing. Source: https://www.heathershomemadehiekitchen.com/rhubarb-custard-bars
