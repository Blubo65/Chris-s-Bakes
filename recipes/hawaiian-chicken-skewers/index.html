Hawaiian Chicken Skewers

Sweet-and-savoury chicken skewers with pineapple and peppers in a tangy soy glaze.

Time: 30 mins plus marinating · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • cut into chunks
  • 250 g fresh pineapple,
  • cubed
  • 1 red pepper,
  • cut into chunks
  • 1 red onion,
  • cut into chunks
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 garlic clove,
  • crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Mix the soy sauce, honey, lime juice, garlic, ginger and oil in a bowl.
  2. Add the chicken and marinate in the refrigerator for at least 20 minutes.
  3. Thread the chicken, pineapple, pepper and onion onto skewers.
  4. If using wooden skewers, soak them first.
  5. Heat a grill or barbecue to medium-high.
  6. Cook for 10–15 minutes, turning regularly and brushing with remaining marinade only early in cooking.
  7. Ensure the chicken reaches 75°C internally and the vegetables are lightly charred.
  8. Serve with rice or salad.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Hawaiian Chicken Skewers recipe. Discard any marinade that has touched raw chicken. Source: https://simply-delicious-food.com/hawaiian-chicken-skewers/
