Homemade Macaroni Cheese

Classic macaroni in a smooth cheddar cheese sauce, with an optional golden baked topping.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g macaroni
  • 40 g butter
  • 40 g plain flour
  • 600 ml milk
  • 200 g mature cheddar,
  • grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard, optional,
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 30 g breadcrumbs,
  • optional

Method

  1. Cook the macaroni in boiling salted water until just tender, then drain.
  2. Melt the butter in a saucepan.
  3. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Gradually whisk in the milk until smooth.
  5. Simmer for 5–7 minutes, stirring, until thickened.
  6. Remove from the heat and stir in the cheddar and mustard if using.
  7. Season to taste.
  8. Mix the sauce with the macaroni.
  9. Serve as it is, or transfer to an ovenproof dish, top with breadcrumbs and grill for 3–5 minutes until golden.
