Pinch of Nom Corned Beef Wrap Pasties

Lighter corned beef, potato and onion pasties made with tortilla wraps instead of pastry.

Ingredients

  • 3 Weight Watchers white tortilla wraps
  • 1 small egg, beaten
  • 1 tin reduced-fat corned beef
  • 2 boiled potatoes
  • ½ onion, finely chopped
  • 1 dash Worcestershire sauce
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1 pinch mustard powder
  • Low-calorie cooking spray (Frylight)

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix the corned beef, cooked potatoes, onion, Worcestershire sauce, salt, pepper and mustard powder together.
  3. Divide the filling into three portions and spoon onto the wraps.
  4. Fold the wraps over, brushing the inside edges with beaten egg to help seal.
  5. Crimp the edges firmly with a fork.
  6. Place on a baking tray coated with low-calorie cooking spray and brush the tops with the remaining beaten egg.
  7. Bake for about 10–15 minutes until golden, then turn the pasties over and cook the other side for a further 5–10 minutes, until golden and heated through.
