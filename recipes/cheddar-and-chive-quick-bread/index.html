Cheddar & Chive Quick Bread

A savoury quick bread with mature cheddar and fresh chives, excellent alongside soup.

Time: 1 hr · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 350 g plain flour
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp mustard powder, optional,
  • 250 g mature cheddar,
  • grated
  • 3 tbsp chopped fresh chives
  • 330 ml stout or dark beer
  • 1 tbsp melted butter,
  • for the top

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Grease and line a 2 lb loaf tin.
  3. Mix the flour, baking powder, salt and mustard powder in a large bowl.
  4. Stir in most of the grated cheddar and the chives.
  5. Pour in the stout or beer and mix just until there are no dry pockets of flour; do not overmix.
  6. Spoon into the tin and scatter the remaining cheese over the top.
  7. Brush with melted butter.
  8. Bake for 40–50 minutes until golden and a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean.
  9. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then transfer to a rack.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Cheddar & Chive Guinness Bread recipe. Alcohol flavour mellows during baking, but the bread is not guaranteed alcohol-free. Source: https://www.thekitchn.com/recipe-cheddar-guinness-beer-bread-166877
