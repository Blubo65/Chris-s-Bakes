Lemon Drizzle Cake

A classic lemon drizzle cake with fresh lemon zest and a tangy lemon-sugar topping.

Ingredients

  • 170g self-raising flour
  • 170g caster sugar
  • 170g softened butter or margarine
  • 4 large eggs
  • Zest of 2 lemons
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 70g granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice for topping
  • Zest of ¼ lemon

Method

  1. Grease and line a suitable cake tin. Preheat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan).
  2. Beat the softened butter and caster sugar until light and fluffy.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, adding a little flour if needed.
  4. Fold in the self-raising flour, lemon zest, vanilla and 1 tbsp lemon juice until just combined.
  5. Spoon into the tin and bake for about 40–50 minutes, until golden and a skewer comes out clean. Check early as tins and ovens vary.
  6. Mix the granulated sugar, 2 tbsp lemon juice and ¼ lemon zest. Prick the warm cake and spoon the drizzle over the top. Cool in the tin.

Recipe notes

Add the lemon drizzle while the cake is still warm so the syrup soaks in.
