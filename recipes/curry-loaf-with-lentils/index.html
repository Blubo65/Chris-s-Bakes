Curry Loaf with Lentils

A firm savoury loaf of lentils, eggs and vegetables seasoned with curry spices, good for lunchboxes.

Time: 1 hr · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 200 g red lentils,
  • rinsed
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 carrot,
  • grated
  • 2 medium eggs
  • 100 g grated cheddar, optional,
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Grease and line a 2 lb loaf tin.
  3. Simmer the lentils in water for 12–15 minutes until soft.
  4. Drain thoroughly and cool slightly.
  5. Mix the lentils with the onion, carrot, eggs, cheddar if using, curry powder, baking powder, salt and pepper.
  6. Spoon into the tin and press down firmly.
  7. Bake for 35–45 minutes until firm and set in the centre.
  8. Cool in the tin for 15 minutes before turning out and slicing.

Recipe notes

A second practical version inspired by the saved curry loaf bookmarks. This is a lentil-based adaptation rather than a verbatim reproduction.
