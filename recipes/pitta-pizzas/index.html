Pitta Pizzas

These customisable pizzas mean everyone can have exactly what they want topping their personal pizza.

Ingredients

  • 4 white pitta breads​
  • Margherita topping
  • 4 tbsp tomato purée with herbs
  • 80g grated mozzarella
  • BBQ chicken topping
  • 1 tbsp BBQ sauce
  • 20g grated mozzarella
  • 20g roast sliced chicken breast, torn into small pieces
  • 15g sweetcorn, defrosted if frozen
  • 15g sliced mushrooms
  • Classic Pepperoni
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 20g grated mozzarella
  • 15g pizza pepperoni
  • 15g frozen sliced mixed peppers, defrosted
  • pinch dried oregano
  • Pesto topping
  • 1 tbsp green pesto
  • 20g grated mozzarella
  • 1 sweet vine-ripened tomato
  • pinch dried oregano

Method

  1. Preheat the air-fryer to 200°C.
  2. Assemble the pitta pizzas by spreading your chosen sauce over the top of the breads and sprinkling with cheese.
  3. Add your chosen toppings, pressing down so that they don’t fall off. Carefully lift the assembled pizza into the basket of the air-fryer and cook for 4-6mins until the cheese is golden brown and bubbling.
  4. Lift out and slice into pieces. Serve while warm.
