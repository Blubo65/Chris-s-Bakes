Roasted Cauliflower

Tender cauliflower florets with caramelised edges, seasoned with garlic and paprika.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower,
  • cut into florets
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic granules
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice,
  • optional

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  2. Pat the cauliflower dry.
  3. Toss with olive oil, garlic granules, paprika, salt and pepper.
  4. Arrange in a single layer in the basket, working in batches if needed.
  5. Air fry for 12–16 minutes, shaking the basket halfway, until tender and browned at the edges.
  6. Finish with lemon juice if desired and serve immediately.

Recipe notes

Adapted from the saved air fryer roasted cauliflower bookmark. Check early as florets and air fryers vary. Source: https://allguiderecipes.info/air-fryer-roasted-cauliflower/
