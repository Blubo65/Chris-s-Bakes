Wholewheat Bread (No Knead)

A simple no-knead wholewheat loaf made with just flour, water, yeast, sugar and salt.

Time: 2 hrs 15 mins · Makes 1

Ingredients

  • 250 g wholewheat flour
  • 250 ml warm water
  • 1 tsp active dry yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Mix the warm water, yeast and sugar in a bowl and leave for 5–10 minutes until the yeast begins to foam, if using active dry yeast that needs activating.
  2. Stir in the wholewheat flour and salt until you have a sticky, evenly mixed dough.
  3. Cover and leave in a warm place until noticeably risen, about 60–90 minutes depending on room temperature.
  4. Line a 6-inch round tin that fits your air fryer and lightly grease it.
  5. Transfer the dough into the tin and smooth the top with wet fingers.
  6. Leave to rise again for 20–30 minutes while preheating the air fryer on Bake to 160°C.
  7. Bake for about 25–35 minutes, checking from 25 minutes.
  8. If the top browns too quickly, loosely cover with foil if safe for your appliance.
  9. The loaf should sound hollow when tapped and be cooked through in the centre.
  10. Remove from the tin and cool on a rack before slicing.
