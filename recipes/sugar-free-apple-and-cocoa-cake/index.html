Sugar-Free Apple & Cocoa Cake

A simple sugar-free apple and cocoa cake, blended until smooth and baked in the air fryer.

Time: 25–30 mins · Makes 6–8

Ingredients

  • 4 medium sweet apples
  • 4 large eggs
  • 5 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Olive oil,
  • for greasing the mould

Method

  1. Preheat your air fryer to 160°C for 3 minutes.
  2. Peel, core and chop the apples into small pieces.
  3. Place the apples, eggs, cocoa powder and baking powder into a blender or food processor.
  4. Blend until completely smooth.
  5. Lightly grease a 6-inch (15cm) cake tin or suitable ovenproof mould with olive oil.
  6. Make sure it fits comfortably in your air fryer basket with room for air to circulate.
  7. Pour the mixture into the prepared tin and smooth the top.
  8. Air fry at 160°C for 25–30 minutes, checking from 25 minutes.
  9. The cake is ready when the centre is set and a skewer inserted into the middle comes out clean or with only a few moist crumbs.
  10. Leave the cake in the tin for 10 minutes, then carefully turn it out onto a wire rack and allow it to cool before serving.

Recipe notes

Air-fryer version adapted from the original oven recipe. Ninja-style air fryer: 160°C is recommended; if the top browns too quickly, loosely cover with foil for the final part of cooking. Do not use foil unless it is securely held down by the cake tin.
