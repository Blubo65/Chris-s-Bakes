Sticky BBQ Chicken Mac and Cheese

Creamy, lower-fat macaroni cheese baked until bubbly and topped with sticky homemade BBQ chicken.

Ingredients

  • For the BBQ chicken:
  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 5 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp mustard powder
  • 500 g passata
  • 3 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 tbsp sweetener
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • About 140 ml Coke Zero (1/4 imperial pint)
  • For the macaroni cheese:
  • 200 g macaroni or other pasta
  • 240 ml chicken stock
  • 100 ml skimmed milk
  • 200 g low-fat cream cheese, softened
  • 200 g grated low-fat cheddar
  • 60 g grated low-fat mozzarella
  • 1 tsp English mustard
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Cook the pasta according to the packet until al dente. Drain, reserving some cooking water.
  2. In a large saucepan, combine the chicken stock, milk and softened cream cheese. Heat over medium-low heat, stirring until smooth.
  3. Stir in half the grated cheddar and mozzarella until melted. Season with salt and pepper and stir in the English mustard.
  4. Mix together the Worcestershire sauce, mustard powder, passata, balsamic vinegar, garlic, sweetener, smoked paprika and Coke Zero to make the BBQ sauce.
  5. Preheat a grill or stovetop grill pan over medium-high heat. Season the chicken breasts with salt and pepper, then brush both sides with BBQ sauce.
  6. Grill the chicken for about 6–8 minutes per side, or until thoroughly cooked through, basting with additional BBQ sauce if desired.
  7. Stir the cooked pasta into the cheese sauce, adding a few tablespoons of reserved pasta water for a glossy consistency.
  8. Preheat the oven to 175°C. Transfer the macaroni cheese to a baking dish.
  9. Top with the remaining cheese and drizzle over some BBQ sauce.
  10. Bake for about 15 minutes until bubbling and lightly browned.
  11. Slice the cooked chicken and serve on top of the macaroni cheese.
