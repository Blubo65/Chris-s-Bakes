Chicken Peanut Butter Curry

A creamy Thai-inspired one-pan chicken curry with peanut butter, coconut milk, chickpeas, broccoli and spinach. Ready in 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 450 g boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 3 large garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 cups fresh broccoli florets
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp cayenne pepper
  • ½ tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 can (400 ml) unsweetened coconut milk
  • ⅔–1 cup low-sodium vegetable or chicken stock
  • 1 can (425 g) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3–4 tbsp creamy unsweetened peanut butter
  • Juice of 1 large lime
  • ¼ cup fresh coriander, chopped
  • 3 cups baby spinach (optional)
  • Rice or quinoa, to serve
  • Peanuts and extra coriander, to garnish (optional)

Method

  1. Cut the chicken thighs into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a deep frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and sear for about 3 minutes, then turn and cook for another minute.
  3. Add the onion, garlic and broccoli. Cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring, until the onion softens.
  4. Stir in the paprika, cumin, cayenne, ginger, salt and pepper. Cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  5. Add the coconut milk, ⅔ cup stock, chickpeas, tomato paste, peanut butter and lime juice. Stir well and simmer over medium-low heat for 5 minutes.
  6. Stir in the coriander and baby spinach. Simmer for another 5 minutes, adding the remaining stock if you prefer a thinner sauce. Check that the chicken is fully cooked and adjust the seasoning.
  7. Garnish with extra coriander and peanuts, if using. Serve with jasmine rice, brown rice or quinoa.
