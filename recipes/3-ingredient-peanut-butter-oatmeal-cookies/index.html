3-Ingredient Peanut Butter Oatmeal Cookies

Soft, simple peanut butter oat cookies made with just three pantry ingredients — no flour or eggs.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup old-fashioned rolled oats

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F) and line a baking tray with baking parchment.
  2. Mix the peanut butter and brown sugar together until smooth.
  3. Fold in the rolled oats until fully combined.
  4. Scoop portions of dough, roll into balls and gently press flat on the prepared tray.
  5. Bake for 10–12 minutes.
  6. Leave on the baking tray for 5–10 minutes to firm up before moving to a wire rack.
