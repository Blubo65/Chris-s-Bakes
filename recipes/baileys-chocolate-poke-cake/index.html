Baileys Chocolate Poke Cake

Rich chocolate poke cake soaked with Baileys chocolate cream filling, finished with fluffy Baileys chocolate buttercream and chocolate curls.

Ingredients

  • For the cake:
  • Cooking spray, for the tin
  • 1 box chocolate cake mix, plus the ingredients required on the packet
  • For the filling:
  • 1 x 14 oz (397 g) tin sweetened condensed milk
  • ⅓ cup (80 ml) double cream
  • ⅓ cup (about 30 g) cocoa powder
  • ¼ cup (60 ml) Baileys Irish Cream
  • For the frosting:
  • 1 cup (about 225 g) butter, softened
  • 5 cups (about 600 g) icing sugar
  • ½ cup (120 ml) Baileys Irish Cream
  • ¼ cup (about 25 g) cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Chocolate curls, to decorate

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan
  2. 350°F). Line a 9×13-inch (23×33 cm) baking tin with baking parchment and lightly grease. Prepare the chocolate cake mix according to the packet directions, pour into the tin and bake for about 25 minutes, or until a skewer comes out clean. Cool for 10 minutes.
  3. Use the rounded end of a wooden spoon to poke holes evenly across the warm cake.
  4. Whisk together the condensed milk, double cream, cocoa powder and ¼ cup Baileys until smooth. Pour over the cake, working the mixture into the holes, and spread evenly. Refrigerate for 1 hour.
  5. To make the frosting, beat the softened butter with about half the icing sugar, the ½ cup Baileys, cocoa powder, vanilla and salt. Gradually add the remaining icing sugar and beat until light and fluffy.
  6. Spread the frosting over the chilled cake, decorate with chocolate curls and serve.
