Golden Mac ’n’ Cheese

A lighter, golden baked macaroni cheese with broccoli, carrots, creamy quark and reduced-fat Cheddar.

Ingredients

  • 250g dried macaroni
  • 2 carrots, peeled and thinly sliced
  • 200g broccoli florets
  • 4 spring onions, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 300ml vegetable stock
  • 4 tbsp tomato purée
  • 200g plain quark
  • 1 tsp English mustard powder
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 160g reduced-fat Cheddar, coarsely grated
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • Small handful fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
  • Mixed salad leaves and lemon juice, to serve

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan/Gas 6). Cook the macaroni according to the packet instructions, drain well and transfer to a large bowl.
  2. Steam the carrots and broccoli for 3–4 minutes until just tender. Drain and add to the macaroni with the spring onions and garlic.
  3. Blend the vegetable stock, tomato purée, quark, mustard powder and beaten eggs until smooth. Pour over the macaroni, add three-quarters of the grated Cheddar, season and mix well.
  4. Spoon into a shallow ovenproof dish, sprinkle with the remaining Cheddar and bake for 15–20 minutes until golden and bubbling. Garnish with parsley and black pepper. Serve with salad leaves dressed with lemon juice.
