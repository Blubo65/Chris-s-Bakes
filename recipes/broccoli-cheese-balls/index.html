Broccoli Cheese Balls

Bite-sized broccoli and cheese balls with a crisp coating, ideal as a snack or side dish.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 250 g broccoli florets
  • 100 g grated cheddar
  • 1 egg
  • 50 g breadcrumbs,
  • plus extra for coating
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Oil spray

Method

  1. Steam or boil the broccoli until tender, then drain very well and chop finely.
  2. Allow to cool.
  3. Mix the broccoli with cheddar, egg, breadcrumbs, flour, garlic granules, salt and pepper.
  4. Shape into small balls.
  5. Roll lightly in extra breadcrumbs and chill for 10 minutes if possible.
  6. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  7. Place the balls in a single layer and spray lightly with oil.
  8. Air fry for 8–12 minutes, turning carefully once, until golden and heated through.
  9. Serve warm.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Broccoli Balls video. Drain the broccoli thoroughly so the mixture holds together. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vf1LHpf1-Z0
