Chocolate Brunch Bars (Fridge Cake)

Easy no-bake chocolate fridge cake packed with crushed digestive biscuits, sultanas and raisins, finished with a smooth chocolate topping.

Ingredients

  • 110g butter
  • 3 tbsp golden syrup
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 57g caster sugar
  • 300g digestive biscuits, crushed
  • 85g sultanas
  • 57g raisins
  • 300g chocolate, for the topping

Method

  1. Line a 9-inch square tin with baking paper.
  2. In a saucepan over a low heat, melt together the butter, golden syrup, cocoa powder and caster sugar, stirring until everything has melted. Do not allow the mixture to boil.
  3. Add the crushed digestive biscuits, sultanas and raisins. Mix thoroughly so the biscuits and fruit are coated.
  4. Press the mixture firmly into the lined tin and flatten the surface with the back of a spoon.
  5. Break the chocolate into pieces and melt it in the microwave in short bursts, stirring between each burst until smooth. Spread the melted chocolate over the top of the biscuit mixture.
  6. Place in the fridge until completely set. Lift out of the tin and cut into squares to serve.
