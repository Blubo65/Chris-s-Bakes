Full Indian breakfast bake

A spicy Indian twist on a cooked breakfast with sausages, beans, mushrooms, spinach and eggs.

Time: 1 hour · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 2 large onions,
  • sliced
  • 200g mushrooms,
  • halved or quartered
  • 3 garlic cloves,
  • grated
  • 3cm piece fresh root ginger,
  • peeled and grated
  • 2 tbsp curry powder,
  • plus extra to serve
  • Low-calorie cooking spray
  • 4 Slimming World sausages (or other low-fat sausages,
  • with Syns counted)
  • 200g baby spinach,
  • chopped
  • 400g can chopped tomatoes
  • 4 medium eggs
  • 400g can baked beans
  • Fresh coriander sprigs,
  • to serve

Method

  1. Heat your oven to 200°C/fan 180°C/gas 6.
  2. Put the onions and mushrooms in a large non-stick ovenproof dish or baking tray.
  3. Scatter over the garlic, ginger and curry powder, season lightly and spray with low-calorie cooking spray.
  4. Toss well and bake for 15–20 minutes.
  5. Add the sausages, spinach and tomatoes and return to the oven for another 15 minutes.
  6. Make 4 dips in the mixture and crack in the eggs.
  7. Spoon in the baked beans and cook for 12–15 minutes or until the sausages are cooked and the eggs are done how you like them.
  8. Chop the sausages into chunks and return them to the dish.
  9. Grind over freshly ground black pepper and the extra curry powder, if using, and scatter over coriander sprigs to serve.
