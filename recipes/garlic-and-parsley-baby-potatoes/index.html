Garlic & Parsley Baby Potatoes

Crispy-skinned baby potatoes tossed with garlic, parsley and olive oil.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g baby potatoes,
  • halved if large
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves,
  • finely chopped or grated
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
  • Salt and black pepper
  • ½ tsp paprika,
  • optional

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  2. Wash and dry the potatoes; halve any larger ones so they cook evenly.
  3. Toss the potatoes with olive oil, salt, pepper and paprika if using.
  4. Air fry for 18–22 minutes, shaking the basket every 5–6 minutes, until golden outside and tender inside.
  5. Mix the garlic with the parsley.
  6. Toss through the hot potatoes for the final 1–2 minutes of cooking, taking care not to burn the garlic, or stir through just after cooking.
  7. Serve hot.

Recipe notes
