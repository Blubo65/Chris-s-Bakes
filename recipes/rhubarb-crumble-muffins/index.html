Rhubarb Crumble Muffins

Light muffins studded with tangy rhubarb and topped with a buttery cinnamon crumble.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 12

Ingredients

  • 250 g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 120 g caster sugar
  • 1 medium egg
  • 180 ml milk
  • 80 ml vegetable oil
  • 200 g rhubarb,
  • diced
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • For crumble: 40 g flour,
  • 25 g butter,
  • 25 g brown sugar,
  • ½ tsp cinnamon

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C) and line a 12-hole muffin tin with paper cases.
  2. Mix the flour, baking powder and caster sugar in a bowl.
  3. In a jug, whisk the egg, milk, oil and vanilla.
  4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and fold together just until combined.
  5. Fold in the diced rhubarb.
  6. Rub the crumble flour, butter, brown sugar and cinnamon together until crumbly.
  7. Divide the muffin mixture between the cases and sprinkle the crumble topping over each.
  8. Bake for 18–23 minutes until risen and a skewer comes out clean.
  9. Cool for 5 minutes in the tin, then transfer to a rack.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Rhubarb Crumble Muffins bookmark. Source: https://www.curlys cooking.co.uk/rhubarb-crumble-muffins
