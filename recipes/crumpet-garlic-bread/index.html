Crumpet Garlic Bread

A quick, easy recipe turning simple crumpets into golden pockets of garlicky, cheesy delight.

Time: 8 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300g crumpets,
  • halved
  • ¼ cup finely chopped fresh continental parsley
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 cup pre-grated 3 cheese blend
  • 150g salted butter,
  • at room temperature
  • 1 tbsp sea salt flakes

Method

  1. Combine butter, parsley, garlic and salt in a bowl.
  2. Spread generous teaspoonfuls over each crumpet half.
  3. Arrange the remaining crumpet halves over the cheese to make half-moon crumpet sandwiches.
  4. Transfer the sandwiches, cut side down, to an air fryer basket lined with foil.
  5. Sprinkle over the remaining cheese.
  6. Air fry for 8 minutes at 180°C until golden and the cheese is melted.
  7. To serve, brush over the remaining garlic butter.

Recipe notes

Prep time 10 mins · Total time 18 mins · 490 kcal per serving. Source recipe specifies 180°C and 8 minutes.
