Homemade Doner Kebab

A homemade spiced doner-style kebab loaf, thinly sliced and served in flatbreads with salad and sauce.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g lamb mince or a lamb-and-beef mince mix
  • 1 small onion,
  • very finely grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp onion granules
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Flatbreads,
  • salad and yoghurt-garlic sauce,
  • to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Mix the mince with the grated onion, garlic, cumin, coriander, paprika, oregano, onion granules, salt and pepper.
  3. Knead the mixture for 2–3 minutes until sticky and well combined.
  4. Shape into a compact loaf and place in a small roasting dish.
  5. Bake for 30–40 minutes, until cooked through.
  6. Drain off any excess fat and rest for 5 minutes.
  7. Slice very thinly.
  8. For crisp edges, spread the slices on a tray and grill briefly, turning once.
  9. Serve in warm flatbreads with salad, sliced onion and yoghurt-garlic sauce.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved homemade doner kebab video; not a verbatim reproduction. Ensure minced meat is cooked through. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEF354SpSxg
