Muffins

Easy plain vanilla muffins baked in the air fryer, ready to customise with fruit or chocolate chips.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 200 g self-raising flour
  • 100 g caster sugar
  • 1 medium egg
  • 120 ml milk
  • 60 ml vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp baking powder, optional,
  • 50 g chocolate chips or blueberries,
  • optional

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 160°C.
  2. Place paper muffin cases in silicone cups or small tins that fit the basket.
  3. Mix the flour, sugar and baking powder if using in a bowl.
  4. Whisk the egg, milk, oil and vanilla in a separate jug.
  5. Pour into the dry ingredients and fold together just until combined; stir in chocolate chips or blueberries if using.
  6. Divide between 8 muffin cases, filling each about two-thirds full.
  7. Bake in batches if necessary.
  8. Air fry for 12–16 minutes, checking from 10 minutes, until risen and a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean.
  9. Cool for 5 minutes before transferring to a rack.

Recipe notes

Practical air fryer adaptation inspired by the saved BBC Good Food Air Fryer Muffins recipe. Small air fryer baskets and muffin sizes affect timing. Source: https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/air-fryer-muffins
