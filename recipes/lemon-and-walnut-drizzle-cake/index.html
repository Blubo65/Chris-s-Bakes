Lemon and Walnut Drizzle Cake

A light lemon cake with chopped walnuts and a simple lemon icing drizzle.

Time: 35–40 mins · Makes 9

Ingredients

  • 175g butter,
  • softened
  • Finely grated zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • 175g caster sugar
  • 3 large free-range eggs
  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 50g California walnuts,
  • finely chopped
  • 25g California walnuts,
  • roughly chopped
  • 50g icing sugar,
  • sifted

Method

  1. Grease and line the base and sides of an air fryer tray with baking parchment.
  2. Whisk together the butter, lemon zest and caster sugar until pale and fluffy.
  3. Whisk in the eggs, one at a time.
  4. Stir the flour and 50g finely chopped walnuts into the cake mixture with the juice of half a lemon.
  5. Spoon into the prepared tray, level the surface and cook in the air fryer at 180°C for 35–40 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
  6. Cover with foil towards the end if it looks a little dark.
  7. Allow to cool.
  8. Blend the icing sugar with 1–2 tsp lemon juice to give a smooth, thick but pipeable consistency, then drizzle or pipe over the cake.
  9. Sprinkle with the remaining walnuts and cut into nine pieces.

Recipe notes

Tip from the supplied recipe: try adding a little ground cinnamon to the flour for an extra Christmassy flavour. Source shown in the image: California Walnuts / www.californiawalnuts.co.uk.
