BBQ Cola Steak

Steak marinated with cola, barbecue sauce and spices for a sweet smoky glaze.

Time: 25 mins plus marinating · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 beef steaks,
  • about 150–200 g each
  • 100 ml cola
  • 3 tbsp barbecue sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 garlic clove,
  • crushed
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • Black pepper
  • 1 tsp oil

Method

  1. Mix the cola, barbecue sauce, soy sauce, garlic and paprika in a bowl.
  2. Add the steaks and marinate in the refrigerator for 30 minutes to 2 hours.
  3. Reserve the marinade separately before cooking if you want to use it as a sauce.
  4. Heat a grill or frying pan until hot.
  5. Remove the steaks from the marinade and pat lightly dry, then rub with a little oil and black pepper.
  6. Cook to your preferred doneness, turning once.
  7. Rest the steaks for 5 minutes before serving.
  8. If using the reserved marinade as a sauce, boil it thoroughly in a small pan until reduced and syrupy; never serve marinade that has touched raw meat without boiling.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved BBQ Cola Steak video. Do not use raw-meat marinade as a sauce unless thoroughly boiled. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YMIQ2gbP7vA
