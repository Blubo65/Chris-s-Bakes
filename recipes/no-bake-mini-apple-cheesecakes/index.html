No-Bake Mini Apple Cheesecakes

Individual no-bake cheesecakes with a buttery biscuit base, creamy filling and cinnamon apples.

Time: 25 mins plus chilling · Makes 6

Ingredients

  • 120 g digestive biscuits,
  • crushed
  • 45 g butter,
  • melted
  • 200 g cream cheese,
  • softened
  • 150 ml double cream
  • 50 g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 apple,
  • peeled and finely diced
  • 1 tsp butter
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon

Method

  1. Mix the crushed biscuits with melted butter and divide between 6 small glasses or ramekins.
  2. Press down lightly and chill.
  3. Cook the diced apple in a small pan with 1 tsp butter, brown sugar and cinnamon for 4–5 minutes until just tender.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Beat the cream cheese, icing sugar and vanilla until smooth.
  6. Whip the double cream to soft peaks and fold into the cream cheese mixture.
  7. Spoon the filling over the biscuit bases and top with the cooled cinnamon apple.
  8. Chill for at least 4 hours until set.
  9. Keep refrigerated until serving.

Recipe notes
