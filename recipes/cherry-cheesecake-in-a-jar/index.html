Cherry cheesecake in a jar

Creamy, crunchy and nutty — a fruity layered cheesecake-style dessert.

Time: 30 mins plus 30 mins cooling/chilling · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1½ level tsp arrowroot
  • Juice of 1 orange
  • 1 level tbsp sweetener granules
  • 250g cherries,
  • stoned and destalked,
  • plus whole cherries to decorate
  • 5 crunchy amaretti biscuits,
  • roughly crushed
  • 300g plain quark
  • 200g fat-free natural Greek yogurt
  • 4 whole almonds,
  • roughly chopped

Method

  1. Measure out 100ml water and mix 1 tbsp with the arrowroot to make a paste.
  2. Set aside.
  3. Put the rest of the water in a small non-stick pan with the orange juice and 1 level tsp sweetener.
  4. Bring to the boil over a high heat, then add the arrowroot paste and stir until thickened.
  5. Return to the boil, add the cherries and simmer for 3–4 minutes or until the cherries have softened slightly.
  6. Set aside to cool.
  7. Divide the amaretti biscuits between 4 x 200ml jars or dessert glasses, followed by a third of the cherries in syrup.
  8. Put 250g quark, the yogurt and the remaining sweetener in a mixing bowl and whisk until smooth.
  9. Stir in most of what's left of the cherries in syrup, divide between the jars or glasses and smooth the tops.
  10. Chill for at least 15 minutes.
  11. Dry-fry the almonds in a pan over a high heat for 1–2 minutes, or until just golden.
  12. Top each jar or glass with a dollop of the remaining quark and spoon over the rest of the cherries in syrup.
  13. Scatter over the almonds and decorate with whole cherries.
