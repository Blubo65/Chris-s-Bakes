Rhubarb Crunch

A warm rhubarb dessert with a buttery oat crumble topping, delicious with custard.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 500 g rhubarb,
  • chopped
  • 100 g caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract, optional,
  • 120 g rolled oats
  • 100 g plain flour
  • 100 g light brown sugar
  • 100 g butter,
  • melted
  • ½ tsp cinnamon,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Put the rhubarb in an ovenproof dish and toss with caster sugar, cornflour and vanilla if using.
  3. Mix the oats, flour, brown sugar and cinnamon in a bowl.
  4. Stir in the melted butter until clumps form.
  5. Scatter the crumble evenly over the rhubarb.
  6. Bake for 30–35 minutes until the topping is golden and the rhubarb is bubbling and tender.
  7. Rest for 10 minutes before serving warm with custard or ice cream.

Recipe notes
