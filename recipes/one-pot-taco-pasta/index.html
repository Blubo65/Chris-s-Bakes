One-Pot Taco Pasta

A family-friendly pasta with beef mince, tomato, taco spices and melted cheese, all cooked in one pot.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 400 g lean beef mince
  • 300 g dried pasta
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 2 tbsp taco seasoning
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 600 ml beef stock,
  • hot
  • 100 g grated cheddar
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper,
  • to taste

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large deep pan.
  2. Add the mince and onion and cook until the mince is browned.
  3. Stir in the pepper and taco seasoning and cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the tomato purée, chopped tomatoes, stock and dried pasta.
  5. Stir well and bring to a simmer.
  6. Cover partially and cook for 12–16 minutes, stirring frequently, until the pasta is tender.
  7. Add a splash of hot water if needed.
  8. Stir in the grated cheese until melted.
  9. Check the mince is fully cooked and the dish is piping hot, then serve.
