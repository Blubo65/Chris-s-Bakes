Lemon Cookies (standard ingredients)

Traditional lemon cookies made with butter, caster sugar, lemon zest and fresh lemon juice.

Ingredients

  • 100g plain flour
  • 50g salted butter, softened
  • 60g caster sugar
  • 1 medium egg yolk
  • Zest of 1 large lemon
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • Optional: 1 tbsp icing sugar

Method

  1. Rub the lemon zest into the caster sugar.
  2. Cream the softened butter and lemon sugar together until well combined.
  3. Mix in the egg yolk, lemon juice and vanilla.
  4. Stir in the flour and baking powder to form a soft dough. Chill for 20–30 minutes.
  5. Shape into small cookies and flatten slightly.
  6. For the Ninja AF410, place on perforated parchment and air fry at 160°C for about 7–8 minutes, checking early. Leave to cool and firm up before moving.

Recipe notes

Traditional full-sugar version; not the low-calorie sweetener version.
