Festive Spiced Latte Cake (2 x 7-inch round tins)

A moist, warmly spiced coffee cake soaked with coffee and rum syrup, finished with a creamy vanilla custard topping. Adapted for two 7-inch round tins in a Ninja air fryer.

Ingredients

  • For the cakes:
  • 375g unsalted butter, melted
  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 1½ tsp baking powder
  • 375g light brown soft sugar
  • ¾ tsp ground nutmeg, plus a pinch
  • ⅜ tsp salt (a generous pinch)
  • 2 tbsp instant coffee powder
  • 112ml whole milk
  • 260g low-fat fresh vanilla custard (use 110g in the cake and reserve 150g for the topping)
  • 5 medium eggs, beaten
  • For the coffee and rum syrup:
  • 1½ tsp instant coffee powder
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1½ tbsp hot water
  • 3 tbsp dark rum
  • For the custard topping:
  • 300ml whipping cream
  • 3 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1½ tbsp dark rum
  • 150g low-fat fresh vanilla custard
  • ¾ tsp ground nutmeg, for dusting

Method

  1. 1. Prepare the tins: Grease and line the bases of two 7-inch (18cm) round cake tins with baking parchment. Preheat the Ninja air fryer to 150°C for 3 minutes.
  2. 2. Make the mixture: In a large bowl, mix the flour, baking powder, light brown sugar, nutmeg and salt, breaking up any lumps. Dissolve the 2 tbsp instant coffee in a little hot water. In another bowl or jug, mix the melted butter, milk, 110g custard, beaten eggs and dissolved coffee. Add the wet mixture to the dry ingredients and mix until smooth. Divide evenly between the two tins.
  3. 3. Air fry the cakes: Cook one tin at a time at 150°C for approximately 25–35 minutes. Check from 25 minutes with a skewer
  4. it should come out clean. If the top browns too quickly, loosely cover with foil, making sure it is secured and cannot touch the heating element. Air fryers vary, so allow extra time if needed. Cool in the tins for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack.
  5. 4. Make the syrup: Mix the 1½ tsp coffee powder with the hot water, then stir in the caster sugar and dark rum until dissolved. When the cakes are cool enough to handle, prick the tops all over with a skewer and brush the syrup evenly over both cakes. Leave to cool completely.
  6. 5. Make the topping: Whip the cream with the caster sugar and rum until thick. Gently fold in the reserved 150g custard. Place one cake on a serving plate, spread with some topping, then place the second cake on top and spread the remaining topping over it. Dust with nutmeg.
  7. 6. Chill before serving: Refrigerate until ready to serve so the cream and custard topping stays firm.
