Dirty Fried Rice

A quick savoury fried rice with mince, vegetables and Cajun-style seasoning.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g cold cooked rice
  • 300 g lean beef or turkey mince
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 100 g frozen peas
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Black pepper
  • 2 spring onions,
  • sliced

Method

  1. Use rice that has been cooked and cooled promptly.
  2. Heat the oil in a large wok or frying pan.
  3. Add the mince and cook until browned and fully cooked, breaking it up as it cooks.
  4. Add the onion and pepper and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes.
  5. Stir in the garlic and Cajun seasoning for 30 seconds.
  6. Add the cold rice and peas.
  7. Stir-fry over high heat for 4–5 minutes until piping hot throughout.
  8. Add the soy sauce and toss well.
  9. Garnish with spring onions and serve immediately.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Dirty Fried Rice bookmark. Cool cooked rice promptly and reheat only once until piping hot. Source: http://pinchofnom.com/recipes/syn-free-dirty-fried-rice-slimming-world
