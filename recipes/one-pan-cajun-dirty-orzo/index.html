One-Pan Cajun Dirty Orzo

A hearty one-pan orzo dish with Cajun spices, peppers and sausage, cooked until rich and savoury.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g orzo pasta
  • 250 g smoked sausage,
  • sliced
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 2 celery sticks,
  • diced (optional)
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning,
  • or to taste
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 700 ml chicken stock,
  • hot
  • 100 g sweetcorn (optional)
  • 1 tbsp oil,
  • if needed
  • Black pepper,
  • to taste
  • Sliced spring onions,
  • to garnish (optional)

Method

  1. Heat a large deep frying pan or casserole over medium heat.
  2. Add the sausage and brown for 3–4 minutes, using a little oil only if needed.
  3. Add the onion, pepper and celery if using.
  4. Cook for 4–5 minutes until beginning to soften.
  5. Stir in the garlic, Cajun seasoning and tomato purée and cook for 30 seconds.
  6. Add the dry orzo and hot stock.
  7. Stir well and bring to a gentle simmer.
  8. Cook uncovered for 10–14 minutes, stirring often so the orzo does not stick, until tender and most of the liquid has been absorbed.
  9. Add a splash of hot water or stock if needed; stir in sweetcorn for the final few minutes if using.
  10. Taste, adjust the seasoning and make sure the dish is piping hot.
  11. Garnish with spring onions if desired and serve.
