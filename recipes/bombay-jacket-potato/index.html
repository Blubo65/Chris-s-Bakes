Bombay Jacket Potato

Crispy jacket potatoes filled with a spicy Bombay-style potato mixture and finished with yoghurt or chutney.

Time: 1 hr · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 2 large baking potatoes
  • 250 g potatoes,
  • peeled and diced small
  • 1 small onion,
  • chopped
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 100 g peas, optional,
  • 2 tbsp chopped tomatoes
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Natural yoghurt or chutney,
  • to serve

Method

  1. Bake the jacket potatoes at 200°C (fan 180°C) for 50–60 minutes until the skins are crisp and centres tender, or use your preferred air fryer jacket potato method.
  2. Meanwhile, boil the diced potatoes for 8–10 minutes until just tender, then drain.
  3. Heat the oil in a pan and cook the onion for 4–5 minutes.
  4. Add the curry powder, cumin and turmeric and stir for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the diced potatoes, peas if using and chopped tomatoes.
  6. Cook for 5–7 minutes, stirring gently until coated and hot.
  7. Season to taste.
  8. Split the baked potatoes and fluff the centres.
  9. Spoon over the Bombay potato mixture and serve with yoghurt or chutney.

Recipe notes
