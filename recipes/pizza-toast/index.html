Pizza Toast

Quick pizza-style toast topped with tomato sauce, cheese and your favourite toppings.

Time: 10 mins · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 2 thick slices bread
  • 3 tbsp passata or pizza sauce
  • 60 g grated mozzarella or cheddar
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Toppings such as sliced pepper,
  • mushrooms or cooked ham,
  • optional

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  2. Spread the passata or pizza sauce over the bread slices, leaving a small border around the edge.
  3. Sprinkle with oregano and cheese, then add any toppings you like, making sure they are already cooked if needed.
  4. Place carefully in the air fryer basket and cook for 4–6 minutes until the bread is crisp and the cheese melted.
  5. Serve immediately.

Recipe notes

Home adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Pizza Toast bookmark. Use toppings sparingly so they do not fall off the bread. Source: https://www.airfryeryum.com/pizza-toast-air-fryer/
