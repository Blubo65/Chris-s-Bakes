Hunter’s Chicken

Chicken breasts topped with smoky barbecue sauce, bacon and melted cheese, with an easy air fryer method.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 small chicken breasts,
  • about 150–180 g each
  • 4 rashers smoked back bacon
  • 4 tbsp barbecue sauce
  • 80 g grated mature cheddar
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  2. Pat the chicken dry and season with paprika, garlic granules, pepper and a little salt.
  3. Wrap one bacon rasher around each chicken breast or lay it over the top.
  4. Place in the air fryer basket in a single layer.
  5. Cook for 15–20 minutes, turning carefully if needed, until nearly cooked through.
  6. Spoon barbecue sauce over each breast and sprinkle with cheese.
  7. Return to the air fryer for 3–5 minutes until the cheese melts and the chicken reaches 75°C internally.
  8. Rest for 2 minutes and serve with vegetables or potatoes.

Recipe notes

Adapted home version inspired by the saved Bored of Lunch Air Fryer Hunter’s Chicken recipe. Timing depends on chicken thickness; confirm 75°C in the centre. Source: https://boredoflunch.com/recipes/air-fryer-hunters-chicken
