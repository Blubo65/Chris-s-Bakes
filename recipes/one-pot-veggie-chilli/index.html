One-Pot Veggie Chilli

A comforting, hearty meat-free chilli packed with vegetables, kidney beans and warming spices.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1–2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 2 red peppers, chopped
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped
  • 100g chestnut mushrooms, chopped
  • 2–4 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 (400g) tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 (400g) tin kidney beans, undrained
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • Salt and pepper
  • Fresh coriander, optional
  • Crusty bread, optional

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat, then cook the onion, carrots and garlic for 4 minutes or until tender.
  2. Stir in the peppers, celery and chilli powder and cook for 5 minutes or until tender.
  3. Stir in the mushrooms and cook for about 4 minutes.
  4. Stir in the chopped tomatoes and kidney beans, including their liquid. Add the cumin, smoked paprika and cinnamon stick, and season with salt and pepper. Bring to the boil, reduce the heat, cover and simmer gently for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Remove the cinnamon stick, then serve topped with fresh coriander and crusty bread if desired.
