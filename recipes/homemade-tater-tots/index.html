Homemade Tater Tots

Crisp little potato bites with a soft centre, made from grated potato and seasoning.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g potatoes,
  • peeled
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • ½ tsp onion granules
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Oil spray

Method

  1. Grate the potatoes coarsely and squeeze out as much moisture as possible using a clean tea towel.
  2. Place in a bowl and mix with the cornflour, garlic granules, onion granules, salt and pepper.
  3. Shape into small cylinders or nuggets, pressing firmly so they hold together.
  4. Chill for 10 minutes if time allows.
  5. Preheat the air fryer to 200°C.
  6. Lightly spray the tots with oil and arrange in a single layer.
  7. Air fry for 15–20 minutes, shaking or turning halfway, until crisp and golden.
  8. Cook in batches if needed.

Recipe notes

Home adaptation inspired by the saved Homemade Air Fryer Tater Tots recipe. Squeezing out moisture helps them crisp and hold their shape. Source: https://simply-delicious-food.com/homemade-air-fryer-tater-tots
