Mongolian Beef & Noodles

Tender beef and noodles tossed in a sweet-savoury soy, garlic and ginger sauce.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 400 g beef steak,
  • thinly sliced
  • 250 g egg noodles
  • 1 red pepper, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar or honey
  • 150 ml beef stock or water
  • 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp cold water
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 spring onions,
  • sliced

Method

  1. Cook the noodles according to the packet, drain and set aside.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok or large frying pan.
  3. Stir-fry the beef in batches until browned, then set aside.
  4. Add the pepper and cook for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 30 seconds.
  6. Stir in the soy sauce, sugar or honey and stock.
  7. Bring to a simmer, then add the cornflour slurry and cook until glossy.
  8. Return the beef to the pan, add the noodles and toss until piping hot and coated in sauce.
  9. Garnish with spring onions and serve immediately.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Mongolian Beef and Noodle recipe. Source: https://thebestblogrecipes.com/mongolian-beef-and-noodle-recipe
