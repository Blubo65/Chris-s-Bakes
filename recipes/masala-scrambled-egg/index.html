Masala Scrambled Egg

Indian-style scrambled eggs with onion, tomato, green chillies, fresh coriander and warming spices. Delicious with toast or paratha.

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • ½ medium onion, chopped
  • 2 green chillies, chopped
  • 1 handful fresh coriander, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • ¼ tsp chilli powder
  • ¼ tsp ground coriander
  • ¼ tsp panch phoron (five whole spices)
  • ¼ tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tsp tomato paste or ketchup
  • Salt, to taste
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a frying pan over a high heat. Add the panch phoron and fry for a few seconds until fragrant.
  2. Add the chopped onion and green chillies. Reduce the heat to medium and cook until the onion softens.
  3. Add the chopped tomato and cook until combined and softened.
  4. Add the turmeric, chilli powder, ground coriander, ginger-garlic paste, tomato paste or ketchup, fresh coriander and salt. Stir well.
  5. Crack in the eggs and stir continuously until scrambled and cooked to your liking. Serve with paratha, toast or bread.
