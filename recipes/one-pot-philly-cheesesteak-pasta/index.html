One-Pot Philly Cheesesteak Pasta

A creamy one-pot pasta inspired by Philly cheesesteak, with beef, peppers, mushrooms and melted cheese.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 400 g lean beef mince
  • 300 g dried pasta shapes
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 green pepper, sliced
  • 150 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 700 ml beef stock,
  • hot
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 100 g light cream cheese
  • 100 g grated mature cheddar or mozzarella
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Black pepper,
  • to taste

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large deep pan.
  2. Add the beef mince and cook until browned, breaking it up as it cooks.
  3. Add the onion, pepper and mushrooms and cook for 4–5 minutes until beginning to soften.
  4. Stir in the garlic and paprika for 30 seconds, then add the dried pasta, hot beef stock and Worcestershire sauce.
  5. Stir well.
  6. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover partially and cook for 12–16 minutes, stirring frequently, until the pasta is tender.
  7. Add a splash of hot water if the pan gets too dry before the pasta is ready.
  8. Turn the heat to low.
  9. Stir in the cream cheese and grated cheese until melted and creamy.
  10. Check the beef is fully cooked and the dish is piping hot.
  11. Season with black pepper and serve straight away.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved One Pot Philly Cheesesteak Pasta link; not a verbatim reproduction. Pasta cooking time and liquid absorption vary by shape, so stir often and adjust the liquid as needed. Source: https://www.slimmingeats.com/blog/one-pot-philly-cheesesteak-pasta
