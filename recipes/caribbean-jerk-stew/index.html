Caribbean Jerk Stew

A warming Caribbean-inspired stew with chicken, peppers, beans and jerk seasoning.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless chicken thighs,
  • cut into chunks
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1–2 tbsp jerk seasoning,
  • to taste
  • 1 x 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 x 400 g tin kidney beans,
  • drained
  • 200 ml chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Juice of ½ lime
  • Salt,
  • if needed

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large casserole.
  2. Add the chicken and brown for 4–5 minutes.
  3. Add the onion and pepper and cook for 4 minutes until beginning to soften.
  4. Stir in the garlic and jerk seasoning.
  5. Add the tomatoes, kidney beans and stock.
  6. Bring to a simmer, cover and cook gently for 25–30 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and tender.
  7. Remove the lid for the final few minutes if the sauce needs thickening.
  8. Finish with lime juice and adjust seasoning.
  9. Serve with rice.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Caribbean Jerk Stew recipe. Jerk seasonings vary in heat and salt; start with less and adjust. Source: https://pinchofnom.com/recipes/caribbean-jerk-stew
