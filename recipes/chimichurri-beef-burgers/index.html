Chimichurri Beef Burgers

Juicy beef burgers served with a fresh parsley, garlic and vinegar chimichurri sauce.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g lean beef mince
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 4 burger buns,
  • to serve
  • 1 small bunch parsley,
  • finely chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp chilli flakes,
  • optional

Method

  1. Mix the beef mince with salt and pepper and shape into 4 patties.
  2. Chill briefly if time allows.
  3. Make the chimichurri by mixing parsley, garlic, vinegar, olive oil and chilli flakes if using.
  4. Set aside for the flavours to mingle.
  5. Grill or pan-fry the burgers for about 4–6 minutes per side, depending on thickness, until cooked through.
  6. Rest for 2 minutes, then serve in buns with a generous spoonful of chimichurri.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Chimichurri Beef Quarter Pounders bookmark. Ensure minced beef is cooked safely through. Source: https://member.slimmingworld.co.uk/recipes/chimichurri-beef-quarter-pounders
