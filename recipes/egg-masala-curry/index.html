Egg Masala Curry

Hard-boiled eggs served in a spiced onion and tomato curry sauce.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 6 medium eggs
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp chilli powder,
  • or to taste
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 100 ml water
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh coriander,
  • optional

Method

  1. Boil the eggs for 9–10 minutes, cool in cold water, peel and set aside.
  2. Heat the oil in a saucepan.
  3. Cook the onion for 6–8 minutes until soft and lightly golden.
  4. Add the garlic, ginger, cumin, coriander, turmeric and chilli powder.
  5. Stir for 30 seconds.
  6. Add the tomatoes and water.
  7. Simmer for 10–12 minutes until the sauce thickens, then season to taste.
  8. Halve the eggs and nestle them into the sauce.
  9. Simmer gently for 3–4 minutes to warm through.
  10. Garnish with coriander if desired and serve with rice or flatbreads.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved egg masala curry bookmark. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=476SeIqP3ac
