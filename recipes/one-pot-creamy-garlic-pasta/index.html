One-Pot Creamy Garlic Pasta

Simple creamy garlic pasta cooked in one pan with stock, milk and a little cheese.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g dried pasta
  • 4 garlic cloves,
  • finely chopped
  • 700 ml vegetable stock,
  • hot
  • 250 ml milk
  • 100 g light cream cheese
  • 40 g grated Parmesan or mature cheddar
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Black pepper
  • Chopped parsley,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large deep pan.
  2. Add the garlic and cook gently for 30 seconds without browning.
  3. Add the pasta and hot stock.
  4. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered, stirring frequently, for 8–10 minutes or according to the pasta packet, adding a splash of water if needed.
  5. Stir in the milk and continue cooking until the pasta is tender and the sauce has thickened.
  6. Lower the heat and mix in the cream cheese and grated cheese until smooth.
  7. Season with black pepper.
  8. Serve immediately, topped with parsley if desired.
