Apple Crumble Cake

A soft apple cake topped with a buttery crumble, delicious served with warm custard.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 150 g self-raising flour
  • 100 g caster sugar
  • 1 medium egg
  • 75 g melted butter
  • 75 ml milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 apples, peeled and diced
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • For crumble: 40 g plain flour,
  • 25 g butter,
  • 25 g brown sugar

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 150°C.
  2. Grease and line a small round tin that fits comfortably in your basket.
  3. Mix the self-raising flour and caster sugar in a bowl.
  4. Whisk the egg with melted butter, milk and vanilla, then fold into the dry ingredients until just combined.
  5. Fold in the diced apples and cinnamon.
  6. Spoon the batter into the prepared tin.
  7. Rub the crumble flour, butter and brown sugar together until crumbly, then scatter over the cake batter.
  8. Air fry for 25–35 minutes, checking from 20 minutes.
  9. If the top browns too quickly, reduce the temperature slightly or cover loosely with foil if safe for your appliance.
  10. Check a skewer inserted into the cake part comes out clean.
  11. Cool for 10 minutes before removing from the tin.
  12. Serve with custard.

Recipe notes

Practical air fryer adaptation inspired by the saved apple crumble cake video. Tin size and air fryer model affect cooking time; check the centre is cooked. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pwux8unwuo8
