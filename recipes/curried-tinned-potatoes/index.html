Curried Tinned Potatoes

A quick budget side made with tinned potatoes tossed in curry spices and air fried until golden.

Time: 15 mins · Makes 3

Ingredients

  • 1 x 400 g tin potatoes,
  • drained and patted dry
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Chopped coriander,
  • optional

Method

  1. Drain the tinned potatoes and pat them very dry.
  2. Halve any large potatoes.
  3. Toss with oil, curry powder, cumin, paprika, salt and pepper.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 200°C.
  5. Arrange the potatoes in a single layer.
  6. Air fry for 10–14 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp at the edges.
  7. Garnish with coriander if desired and serve hot.

Recipe notes

Home adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Tinned Potatoes video. Dry the potatoes well for best browning. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZOQAHamloTY
