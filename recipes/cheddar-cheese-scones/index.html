Cheddar Cheese Scones

Golden savoury cheddar scones with a hint of mustard, delicious served warm with cheese, ham and chutney.

Ingredients

  • 225g self-raising flour, plus extra for dusting
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 3 tbsp + 2 tsp cold butter, diced
  • 100g cheddar cheese, grated, plus a little extra for the tops
  • 1 free-range egg
  • 1 tsp mustard (wholegrain, Dijon or English)
  • 5–6 tbsp milk, plus extra for glazing
  • To serve: sliced ham, sliced cheese and tomato or caramelised onion chutney

Method

  1. Sift the self-raising flour into a large bowl. Add the baking powder, salt and pepper and mix well. Using your fingertips, rub in the cold butter until the mixture resembles fine breadcrumbs. Stir in most of the grated cheddar, reserving a little for the tops.
  2. In a small bowl, beat together the egg and mustard. Stir into the flour mixture, adding enough milk to make a soft dough.
  3. Turn out onto a lightly floured surface, knead lightly and roll or pat out to about 2.5cm thick. Cut into 5 rounds with a fluted 6cm cutter, re-rolling the trimmings gently to make more if needed.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C. Place the scones on a piece of pierced non-stick baking paper suitable for your air fryer. Brush the tops with a little extra milk and sprinkle with the reserved cheese.
  5. Air fry for about 6 minutes, then carefully turn over and cook for a further 4 minutes, or until well risen and golden brown. Air fryers vary, so check early and adjust the time as needed.
  6. Transfer to a wire rack to cool. Serve sliced in half with ham, cheese and chutney, if desired.
