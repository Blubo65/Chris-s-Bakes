Thai-Inspired Peanut Butter Chicken Curry

A creamy, gently spicy chicken curry with peanut butter, coconut milk, lime and warming aromatics.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp smooth peanut butter
  • 400 ml light coconut milk
  • 150 ml chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp red curry paste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Fresh coriander, optional,
  • Cooked rice,
  • to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan.
  2. Add the onion and cook for 4–5 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the garlic, ginger and curry paste and cook for 30 seconds, stirring.
  4. Add the chicken and cook for 4–5 minutes until the outside is no longer pink.
  5. Whisk the peanut butter with a little coconut milk until smooth, then add to the pan with the remaining coconut milk, stock and soy sauce.
  6. Simmer gently for 12–15 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened.
  7. Stir occasionally and avoid a rapid boil.
  8. Stir in the lime juice, taste and adjust seasoning.
  9. Garnish with coriander if desired and serve with rice.
