Potato & Chicken Casserole

Individual potato and chicken casseroles with tomato, herbs, egg-yoghurt topping and a golden cheese crust.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 2 medium potatoes,
  • thinly sliced
  • 1 chicken breast,
  • cut into small pieces
  • 1 onion,
  • finely sliced
  • 1 tomato,
  • sliced or chopped
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp plain yoghurt
  • 100g grated cheese
  • A little olive oil
  • Dill,
  • oregano,
  • smoked paprika,
  • and salt,
  • to taste

Method

  1. Lightly oil two suitable small, air-fryer-safe dishes.
  2. Arrange a layer of sliced potato in each, then add the onion and chicken pieces.
  3. Season with salt, oregano and smoked paprika.
  4. Beat the eggs with the yoghurt and chopped dill, then divide the mixture between the dishes.
  5. Add the tomato and top with the grated cheese.
  6. Air fry at 160°C for about 30 minutes, until the potatoes are tender and the chicken is thoroughly cooked.
  7. Increase to 180°C for a further 5 minutes to brown the cheese.
  8. Check the centre of the chicken reaches 75°C; if the potatoes are not tender, continue cooking in short intervals.

Recipe notes
