Bacon Badger

A traditional British-style savoury baked dish with bacon and a simple suet-free batter-style topping.

Time: 1 hr · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 250 g smoked bacon, chopped
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 150 g self-raising flour
  • 1 medium egg
  • 150 ml milk
  • 1 tsp mustard powder, optional,
  • Black pepper
  • A little oil for greasing

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 190°C (fan 170°C).
  2. Grease a medium ovenproof dish.
  3. Cook the bacon and onion in a frying pan for 5–6 minutes until the onion softens and bacon begins to colour.
  4. Spread in the dish.
  5. Whisk the flour, egg, milk, mustard powder if using and black pepper into a smooth batter.
  6. Pour the batter over the bacon and onion.
  7. Bake for 30–40 minutes until risen, golden and cooked through in the centre.
  8. Rest for 5 minutes before serving with vegetables.

Recipe notes

A simplified home adaptation inspired by the saved Bacon Badger recipe; traditional versions vary, so this may differ from the source. Source: https://www.sainsburysmagazine.co.uk/recipes/mains/bacon-badger
