Mexican-Spiced Potatoes

Golden potato cubes tossed with smoky paprika, cumin and chilli for a spicy side dish.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 800 g potatoes,
  • cut into 2 cm cubes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • ½ tsp chilli powder,
  • or to taste
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Juice of ½ lime,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (fan 200°C), or air fryer to 200°C.
  2. Pat the potato cubes dry.
  3. Toss with oil, paprika, cumin, garlic granules, chilli powder, salt and pepper.
  4. For the oven, spread on a tray and roast for 30–35 minutes, turning halfway.
  5. For the air fryer, cook for 18–24 minutes, shaking the basket every 6–7 minutes.
  6. Cook until the potatoes are golden and tender inside.
  7. Finish with lime juice if desired and serve hot.

Recipe notes

Home adaptation inspired by the saved Mexican Potatoes recipe. Adjust chilli to taste. Source: http://pinchofnom.com/recipes/mexican-potatoes-slimming-world-weight-watchers-friendly
