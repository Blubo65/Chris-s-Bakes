Corned Beef Pasta Bake

A budget-friendly pasta bake with corned beef, tomato sauce and a cheesy golden topping.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g dried pasta
  • 1 x 340 g tin corned beef,
  • diced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 tsp dried mixed herbs
  • 100 g grated cheddar
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tsp oil

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Cook the pasta in boiling water until just under al dente, then drain.
  3. Heat the oil in a pan and cook the onion and pepper for 5 minutes until softened.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes, tomato purée and mixed herbs.
  5. Simmer for 8–10 minutes, then stir in the corned beef and warm through gently.
  6. Combine the sauce with the drained pasta and transfer to an ovenproof dish.
  7. Sprinkle with the grated cheese.
  8. Bake for 15–20 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  9. Season to taste and serve hot.
