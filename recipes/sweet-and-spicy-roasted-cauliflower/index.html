Sweet & Spicy Roasted Cauliflower

Roasted cauliflower tossed in a sweet, tangy and lightly spicy glaze.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower,
  • cut into florets
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp sriracha or hot sauce,
  • to taste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 garlic clove,
  • crushed
  • 1 tsp lime juice
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (fan 200°C).
  2. Toss the cauliflower with olive oil, salt and pepper.
  3. Spread on a baking tray and roast for 18–22 minutes, turning once, until browned and nearly tender.
  4. Mix the honey or maple syrup, hot sauce, soy sauce, garlic and lime juice.
  5. Toss the cauliflower with the glaze and return to the oven for 4–6 minutes until sticky and tender.
  6. Serve hot as a side dish or snack.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Sweet and Spicy Roasted Cauliflower bookmark. Adjust the hot sauce to taste. Source: https://thefitchen.com/sweet-spicy-baked-cauliflower
