Breaded Potato Cakes

Crisp golden potato cakes with a soft mashed-potato centre, ideal as a side or light lunch.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g potatoes,
  • peeled and chopped
  • 25 g butter
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 egg,
  • beaten
  • 50 g breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp grated Parmesan or mature cheddar (optional)
  • Oil spray

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, about 15 minutes.
  2. Drain well and allow the steam to escape, then mash with the butter, milk, salt and pepper.
  3. Cool until easy to handle.
  4. Shape into 8 small flat cakes.
  5. Chill for 10 minutes if the mash is soft.
  6. Mix the breadcrumbs with cheese if using.
  7. Dip each cake in beaten egg, then coat in breadcrumbs.
  8. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  9. Place the cakes in a single layer, spray lightly with oil and cook for 10–14 minutes, turning carefully halfway, until crisp and golden.
  10. Serve hot.
  11. If the cakes are delicate, use a liner with holes and turn gently.

Recipe notes

Home adaptation inspired by the saved air fryer potato cakes video. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cqFr8YYJ-vA
