Honey Mustard Chicken

Juicy chicken breasts coated in a sweet, tangy honey and Dijon mustard glaze.

Time: 15–20 mins plus marinating · Makes 2–3

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves,
  • minced
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt and black pepper,
  • to taste
  • 1 tsp lemon juice (optional)

Method

  1. Mix the honey, Dijon mustard, olive oil, garlic, paprika, salt, pepper and optional lemon juice in a bowl.
  2. Coat the chicken breasts thoroughly with the marinade.
  3. Leave for 15–30 minutes, or refrigerate for up to 2 hours.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C for 3–5 minutes.
  5. Place the chicken in the basket in a single layer.
  6. Air fry for 10–14 minutes, turning halfway through.
  7. Cooking time depends on the thickness of the chicken; check the thickest part reaches an internal temperature of 75°C.
  8. Rest for 5 minutes before slicing and serving.
