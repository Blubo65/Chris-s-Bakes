Lemon Biscuits

Buttery lemon biscuits with fresh lemon zest and juice, made for the Ninja AF410 air fryer.

Ingredients

  • 125g salted butter, softened
  • 100g caster sugar
  • Zest of 2 lemons
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 225g plain flour
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • Optional icing: 100g icing sugar and 2 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Rub the lemon zest into the caster sugar to release its oils.
  2. Cream the softened butter and lemon sugar together for 2–3 minutes.
  3. Mix in the egg, lemon juice and vanilla.
  4. Stir in the plain flour and baking powder to form a dough. Chill for 20–30 minutes.
  5. Roll into walnut-sized balls (about 30g each) and flatten slightly.
  6. Line the Ninja AF410 crisper plate with perforated parchment and cook about 6 biscuits at a time at 160°C for 7–8 minutes. Edges should be pale golden and centres may still feel soft.
  7. Cool to firm up. If using icing, mix the icing sugar with lemon juice and drizzle over cooled biscuits.

Recipe notes

Use perforated parchment, allow room around each biscuit, and let them cool before moving as they firm up.
