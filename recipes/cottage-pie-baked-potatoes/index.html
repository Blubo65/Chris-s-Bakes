Cottage Pie Baked Potatoes

Crispy jacket potatoes filled with savoury cottage-pie mince and topped with cheese.

Time: 1 hr · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 medium baking potatoes
  • 400 g lean beef mince
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 carrot,
  • finely diced
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 300 ml beef stock
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 75 g grated cheddar
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tsp oil

Method

  1. Bake the potatoes at 200°C (fan 180°C) for 50–60 minutes until crisp outside and tender inside, or use your preferred air fryer jacket potato method.
  2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a pan.
  3. Cook the onion and carrot for 5 minutes, then add the mince and brown thoroughly.
  4. Stir in the tomato purée, stock, Worcestershire sauce and thyme.
  5. Simmer for 15–20 minutes until the filling is thick and the mince fully cooked.
  6. Split the potatoes and fluff the centres with a fork.
  7. Spoon the mince mixture into each potato and top with grated cheese.
  8. Return to the oven or air fryer briefly until the cheese melts.
  9. Serve hot.

Recipe notes
