Peanut Butter Oat Cookies

Simple peanut butter oat cookies made with a few everyday ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp honey or golden syrup
  • ½ cup peanut butter
  • ½ cup oats

Method

  1. Whisk the egg, honey or golden syrup and peanut butter together.
  2. Stir in the oats. If the mixture is very wet, add 1 tsp extra oats.
  3. Shape into cookies and flatten to about 1cm thick.
  4. Place on parchment or a silicone liner in the air fryer basket. Preheat the Ninja air fryer at 170°C for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Air fry at 170°C for 6–8 minutes, checking from 5–6 minutes. Do not flip. If browning too quickly, reduce to 160°C.
  6. Leave to rest for 5–10 minutes to firm up.

Recipe notes

Cookies will be soft when hot and firm as they cool. Golden syrup can replace honey at the same quantity.
