Cheesy Cauliflower-Topped Pie

A comforting savoury mince and vegetable filling finished with a cheesy cauliflower mash topping.

Time: 1 hr · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower,
  • cut into florets
  • 500 g lean beef mince or turkey mince
  • 1 onion,
  • finely chopped
  • 1 carrot,
  • diced
  • 150 g mushrooms, sliced (optional)
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 300 ml beef or chicken stock
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp dried mixed herbs
  • 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp cold water,
  • if needed
  • 60 g grated mature cheddar
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper,
  • to taste

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Steam or boil the cauliflower until very tender, then drain thoroughly and allow it to steam-dry.
  3. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a pan and cook the onion and carrot for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the mince and cook until browned, breaking it up as it cooks.
  5. Stir in the mushrooms if using, tomato purée, stock, Worcestershire sauce and mixed herbs.
  6. Simmer for 15–20 minutes until the vegetables are tender and the filling has reduced.
  7. Thicken with the cornflour slurry if needed, then season.
  8. Mash or blend the drained cauliflower until smooth.
  9. Stir in half the grated cheese and season with pepper.
  10. Spoon the mince mixture into an ovenproof dish and spread the cauliflower mash over the top.
  11. Sprinkle with the remaining cheese.
  12. Bake for 20–25 minutes until bubbling and golden on top.
  13. Let it stand for 5 minutes before serving.
