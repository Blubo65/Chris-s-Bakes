Chicken Katsu

Golden, crunchy Japanese-style chicken cutlets coated in panko breadcrumbs and cooked in the air fryer with less oil.

Ingredients

  • 900 g chicken breast (2 lb), cut into approximately 4 cutlets
  • 1 tsp salt, for seasoning chicken
  • 1 tsp black pepper, for seasoning chicken
  • 2 large eggs
  • 65 g plain flour (½ cup)
  • 60 ml water (¼ cup), as needed
  • ½ tsp salt, for batter
  • ½ tsp black pepper, for batter
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 120 g panko breadcrumbs (about 2 cups)
  • Neutral cooking oil spray
  • Cooked rice, to serve (optional)
  • Katsu sauce, to serve (optional)
  • Red pickled ginger, to garnish (optional)

Method

  1. Slice the chicken breasts into cutlets if needed and pound them to approximately 1.25 cm (½ inch) thick. Season both sides with salt and pepper and set aside for 15 minutes while preparing the coating.
  2. In a wide bowl, whisk together the eggs, flour, water, remaining salt and pepper, and garlic powder until the batter is slightly thinner than pancake batter.
  3. Put the panko breadcrumbs into a separate shallow dish.
  4. Dip each chicken cutlet into the batter, allowing excess to drip off, then coat thoroughly in panko, pressing the crumbs on firmly so no bare patches remain.
  5. Arrange the coated cutlets in the air fryer basket without overlapping and lightly spray the tops with neutral oil.
  6. Air fry at 175°C (350°F) for 10 minutes. Lightly spray again, turn the cutlets over, spray the other side and cook for another 4–6 minutes, until golden and the thickest part reaches at least 74°C (165°F). Cook in batches if necessary.
  7. Serve with rice, katsu sauce and red pickled ginger if desired.
