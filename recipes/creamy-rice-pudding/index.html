Creamy Rice Pudding

Traditional slow-baked rice pudding with a soft creamy texture and a lightly golden skin.

Time: 1 hr 45 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 100 g pudding rice
  • 50 g caster sugar
  • 1 litre semi-skimmed milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract, optional,
  • A pinch of nutmeg

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 150°C (fan 130°C).
  2. Rinse the rice briefly and put it into a buttered ovenproof dish.
  3. Stir in the sugar, milk and vanilla if using.
  4. Sprinkle a little nutmeg over the top.
  5. Bake uncovered for 1 hour 30 minutes to 1 hour 45 minutes, stirring after the first 30 minutes and again after an hour if you prefer no skin.
  6. The pudding is ready when the rice is tender and the mixture is creamy; it thickens further as it cools.
  7. Add a little hot milk if it becomes too thick.
  8. Serve warm or chilled.

Recipe notes

Basic home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved rice pudding video. This version contains milk and sugar and is not fat-free. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T2XTKiMiNAQ
