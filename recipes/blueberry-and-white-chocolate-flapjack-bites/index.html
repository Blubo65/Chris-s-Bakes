Blueberry and white chocolate flapjack bites

Oaty flapjack-style squares with a springy cake texture, blueberries and white chocolate drizzle.

Time: 50 mins plus 10 mins cooling/chilling · Makes 16

Ingredients

  • Low-calorie cooking spray
  • 4 eggs,
  • separated
  • 4 level tbsp sweetener granules
  • 1–2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 1 level tsp baking powder
  • 50g plain jumbo porridge oats
  • 25g extra-light spread
  • 2 level tbsp light brown sugar
  • 2 level tsp honey or golden syrup
  • 16 blueberries,
  • plus extra to serve
  • 25g white chocolate,
  • to decorate

Method

  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C/fan 160°C/gas 4. Grease a 22cm x 22cm cake tin with low-calorie cooking spray and line with greaseproof paper.
  2. Put the egg yolks and sweetener in a large bowl.
  3. Add the vanilla and, using an electric whisk, beat for 5 minutes, until pale and slightly thickened.
  4. In a clean bowl, whisk the egg whites until soft peaks form.
  5. Gently fold into the egg yolk mixture.
  6. Sift the flour and baking powder into another bowl and gently fold into the eggs.
  7. Spoon the mixture into the prepared tin.
  8. Mix the oats, spread, sugar and honey or syrup together really well and gently spread or dollop over the sponge mixture.
  9. Push the blueberries in lightly, aiming for a blueberry in each square.
  10. Bake for 20 minutes or until golden and firm, then cool in the tin for 10 minutes.
  11. Melt the white chocolate in the microwave or in a bowl over simmering water.
  12. Turn out the cake, drizzle over the white chocolate and leave to cool completely.
  13. Cut into 16 squares and serve with extra blueberries and fat-free natural yogurt if liked.

Recipe notes

3½ Syns each is shown in the supplied image. The image notes that leaving out the white chocolate drizzle saves ½ Syn.
