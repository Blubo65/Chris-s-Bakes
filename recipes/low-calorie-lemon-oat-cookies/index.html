Low-Calorie Lemon Oat Cookies

Lemon oat cookies using low-calorie granulated sweetener.

Time: 7–9 mins · Makes About 10 cookies

Ingredients

  • 60g rolled oats
  • 40g self-raising flour
  • 25g Tesco low-calorie granulated sweetener
  • 40g softened salted butter
  • 1 medium egg
  • Zest of 1–1½ lemons
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp baking powder

Method

  1. Pulse oats until mostly fine but still slightly textured.
  2. Beat softened butter and sweetener for 30–60 seconds; it may remain grainy.
  3. Mix in egg, lemon zest, lemon juice and vanilla.
  4. Stir in oats, flour and baking powder to form a soft dough.
  5. Chill for 20 minutes.
  6. Shape into about 10 cookies and place on lined air-fryer parchment with space between them.
  7. Air fry at 160°C for 7–9 minutes, checking early; cool to firm up.

Recipe notes

Ninja AF410. The saved guides differ on butter quantity (25g or 40g); this entry uses the later 40g version requested. Do not expect low-calorie sweetener to cream like sugar.
