Peanut Butter Oatmeal Cookies

A deliciously chewy peanut butter oatmeal cookie, packed with creamy peanut butter and hearty rolled oats. Quick and easy to bake in the air fryer.

Ingredients

  • 115 g salted butter, softened
  • 200 g soft brown sugar
  • 1 medium egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 95 g plain flour
  • ¼ tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • Small pinch of salt
  • 30 g smooth or crunchy peanut butter — about 2 tbsp
  • 120 g rolled/old-fashioned oats
  • 40–45 g chocolate chips (optional)

Method

  1. Put the 115 g softened butter, 200 g brown sugar and 30 g peanut butter into a bowl. Beat until creamy and well combined.
  2. Beat in the medium egg and 1 tsp vanilla.
  3. In a separate bowl, mix together the 95 g plain flour, ¼ tsp bicarbonate of soda and small pinch of salt.
  4. Gradually stir the flour mixture into the wet mixture. Don't overmix.
  5. Stir in the 120 g rolled oats and chocolate chips, if using.
  6. Chill the dough for 45–60 minutes. This will help prevent the cookies spreading too quickly in the air fryer.
  7. Bake at 160c.
  8. Leave them sitting on the liner for 5 minutes after cooking before moving them. They'll firm up considerably as they cool.
