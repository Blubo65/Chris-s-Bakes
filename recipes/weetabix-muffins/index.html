Weetabix Muffins

Simple breakfast-style muffins made with softened Weetabix and fruit, lightly sweet and easy to customise.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 12

Ingredients

  • 2 Weetabix biscuits,
  • crushed
  • 200 ml milk
  • 150 g self-raising flour
  • 75 g light brown sugar
  • 1 medium egg
  • 50 ml vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp mixed spice or cinnamon
  • 75 g raisins or chopped apple,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C) and line a 12-hole muffin tin with paper cases.
  2. Mix the crushed Weetabix with the milk and leave for 5 minutes to soften.
  3. Stir in the egg, oil and sugar.
  4. Fold in the flour and spice until just combined, then add raisins or apple if using.
  5. Divide the mixture between the cases.
  6. Bake for 15–20 minutes until risen and a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean.
  7. Cool on a rack.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Weetabix Muffins video. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IhxyRU80Wjc
