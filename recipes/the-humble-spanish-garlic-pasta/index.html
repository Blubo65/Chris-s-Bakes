The Humble Spanish Garlic Pasta

A simple Spanish-inspired penne pasta tossed with gently sautéed garlic, smoked paprika, parsley and Manchego cheese. Ready in just 20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 225 g penne pasta (8 oz)
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil (45 ml)
  • 8 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • ¼ tsp crushed red pepper (0.65 g)
  • ½ tsp sweet smoked Spanish paprika (1.25 g)
  • 15 g fresh parsley, chopped (¼ cup)
  • 30 g Manchego cheese, grated (¼ cup)
  • Sea salt and black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Fill a stockpot a little over halfway with water, season generously with salt and bring to a boil over a high heat.
  2. Add the penne pasta and cook according to the packet instructions, stirring every 1–2 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a large frying pan over a medium heat.
  4. After about 2 minutes, add the sliced garlic, crushed red pepper and a pinch of salt. Stir continuously for 1–2 minutes until the garlic is lightly sautéed. Turn off the heat, add the smoked paprika and mix together.
  5. When the pasta is just al dente, drain it, reserving some of the pasta cooking water.
  6. Add the pasta to the frying pan with the garlic sauce, parsley and grated Manchego. Season with salt and black pepper. Add 2–3 tbsp (30–45 ml) of reserved pasta water and toss until everything is well combined.
  7. Serve immediately.
