Classic Cheddar Cheese Sauce

A smooth, creamy homemade Cheddar cheese sauce, perfect for cauliflower cheese, pasta bakes and vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 40 g butter
  • 40 g plain flour
  • 500 ml milk
  • 150 g mature Cheddar cheese, grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard (optional)
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a saucepan over a medium-low heat.
  2. Stir in the flour and cook for 1–2 minutes, stirring continuously, without allowing it to brown.
  3. Gradually add the milk, a little at a time, whisking well after each addition until smooth.
  4. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 3–5 minutes, stirring, until the sauce thickens and the flour is cooked through.
  5. Remove the pan from the heat and stir in the grated Cheddar until melted and smooth.
  6. Add the mustard if using, then season to taste with salt and black pepper. Serve warm.
