Lamb Kofta Curry with Mint Yoghurt

Spiced lamb meatballs simmered in a tomato curry sauce, served with cool mint yoghurt.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g lamb mince
  • 1 small onion,
  • finely grated or chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ground cumin,
  • divided
  • 2 tsp ground coriander,
  • divided
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp chilli powder,
  • or to taste
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion,
  • finely chopped (for sauce)
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 200 ml water or stock
  • 150 g plain yoghurt
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh mint
  • Salt and black pepper,
  • to taste

Method

  1. Mix the lamb mince with the grated onion, one garlic clove, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp ground coriander, garam masala, chilli powder and a little salt and pepper.
  2. Shape into 16 small koftas.
  3. Chill for 10 minutes if time allows so they hold their shape.
  4. Mix the yoghurt with the chopped mint and a pinch of salt.
  5. Refrigerate until serving.
  6. Heat the oil in a large frying pan or casserole.
  7. Brown the koftas in batches, turning carefully; they do not need to be fully cooked yet.
  8. Set aside.
  9. Add the chopped onion to the pan and cook for 5 minutes until soft.
  10. Add the remaining garlic, cumin and coriander and stir for 30 seconds.
  11. Add the chopped tomatoes and water or stock.
  12. Simmer for 5 minutes, then return the koftas to the sauce.
  13. Cover and simmer gently for 15–20 minutes, turning the koftas once, until cooked through.
  14. Add a splash of water if the sauce becomes too thick.
  15. Serve with the mint yoghurt and rice or flatbreads.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation based on the saved Lamb Kofta Curry with Mint Yoghurt entry; not a verbatim reproduction. Ensure the lamb koftas are cooked through before serving.
