Roasted Garlic & Mushroom Spaghetti

Spaghetti tossed with oven-roasted garlic, sautéed mushrooms, spinach, rosemary and Parmesan.

Ingredients

  • 400 g spaghetti
  • 15–20 garlic cloves, peeled
  • Olive oil, for roasting and frying
  • Salt, to taste
  • 1 tbsp chilli flakes (adjust to taste)
  • Rosemary or herb of choice
  • 4 shallots, sliced
  • 400 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 large handfuls spinach
  • 1 knob butter
  • 4 tbsp Parmesan (or vegetarian hard cheese)
  • Black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Roast the garlic: Peel the garlic cloves and put them in an ovenproof dish with a generous amount of olive oil, a pinch of salt and rosemary sprigs (or another chosen herb). Cover and roast at 180°C fan for 25–30 minutes until soft and fragrant.
  2. Sauté the shallots and mushrooms: While the garlic roasts, heat olive oil in a frying pan and cook the sliced shallots for a few minutes until translucent. Add the sliced mushrooms and a knob of butter
  3. cook until the mushrooms release their juices and begin to brown. Stir in the spinach and season with salt, pepper and chilli flakes to taste.
  4. Cook the spaghetti in salted boiling water according to the packet instructions.
  5. Combine with roasted garlic: Mash the roasted garlic cloves with a fork in their dish, add the mashed garlic to the mushroom and spinach mixture, and cook together for a few minutes so the flavours meld.
  6. Toss the pasta and serve: Using tongs, transfer the cooked spaghetti straight from the pot into the pan without fully draining, allowing a little pasta water to loosen the sauce. Toss thoroughly, sprinkle with 4 tbsp Parmesan, adjust the seasoning and serve immediately.
