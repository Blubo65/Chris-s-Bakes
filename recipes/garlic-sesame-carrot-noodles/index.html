Garlic Sesame Carrot Noodles

A quick noodle bowl with tender carrots, garlic, sesame and a savoury soy dressing.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 200 g dried noodles
  • 2 large carrots,
  • cut into thin matchsticks or ribbons
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 garlic cloves,
  • finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar or lime juice
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp honey or sugar,
  • optional
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • 2 spring onions,
  • sliced
  • Chilli flakes,
  • to taste (optional)

Method

  1. Cook the noodles according to the packet instructions.
  2. Drain, reserving a little cooking water.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil in a large frying pan or wok.
  4. Add the carrots and stir-fry for 3–5 minutes until just tender but still with some bite.
  5. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds, taking care not to let it burn.
  6. Stir in the soy sauce, rice vinegar or lime juice, sesame oil and honey or sugar if using.
  7. Add the drained noodles and toss thoroughly.
  8. Add a splash of reserved noodle water if needed to loosen the sauce.
  9. Serve topped with sesame seeds, spring onions and chilli flakes if desired.
