Cinnamon Apple Rings

Warm apple rings dusted with cinnamon and sugar, cooked until tender with lightly caramelised edges.

Time: 15 mins · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 2 firm apples
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1–2 tsp caster sugar, optional,
  • A little oil spray,
  • optional

Method

  1. Core the apples and cut into rings about 5–8 mm thick.
  2. Toss lightly with lemon juice.
  3. Mix the cinnamon with sugar if using and sprinkle over the apple rings.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  5. Arrange the rings in a single layer, lightly spraying with oil if desired.
  6. Air fry for 6–10 minutes, turning halfway, until tender and lightly browned.
  7. Thicker rings may need a little longer.
  8. Serve warm on their own or with yoghurt, custard or ice cream.
