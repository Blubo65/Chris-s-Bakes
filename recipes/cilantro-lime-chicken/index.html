Cilantro Lime Chicken

Juicy chicken marinated with lime, garlic and fresh coriander, suitable for the grill or frying pan.

Time: 30 mins plus marinating · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast or boneless thighs
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh coriander
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Mix the lime juice, coriander, garlic, olive oil, cumin, paprika, salt and pepper.
  2. Add the chicken and coat thoroughly.
  3. Marinate for 20–30 minutes in the refrigerator.
  4. Heat a grill or frying pan to medium-high.
  5. Cook the chicken for 5–7 minutes per side, depending on thickness, until browned and cooked through.
  6. Check the thickest part reaches 75°C internally.
  7. Rest for 3–5 minutes before slicing.
  8. Serve with rice, salad or warm tortillas.

Recipe notes
