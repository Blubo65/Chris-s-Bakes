Fruit Cake with Espresso Martini

A rich fruit cake made with Lidl Deluxe Espresso Martini, mixed dried fruit and warming spices, finished with an optional coffee glaze.

Ingredients

  • 375ml Lidl Deluxe Espresso Martini
  • 500g mixed dried fruit
  • 80–90g brown sugar
  • 125g butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 225g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp mixed spice
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Optional coffee glaze: 2 tsp instant coffee, 2 tbsp boiling water and 1 tbsp sugar

Method

  1. Put the mixed dried fruit, Espresso Martini, brown sugar and butter in a saucepan. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 5 minutes, stirring.
  2. Remove from the heat and leave to cool until lukewarm.
  3. Beat in the eggs and vanilla.
  4. Fold in the self-raising flour, mixed spice, cinnamon and nutmeg until just combined.
  5. Divide between two greased and lined 5-inch round tins.
  6. In the Ninja air fryer, bake at 150°C for 18–20 minutes, then reduce to 140°C and bake for a further 25–35 minutes. Check the centres with a skewer; cooking time varies with tin depth and air fryer.
  7. Cool in the tins before turning out. For the optional glaze, dissolve the instant coffee in boiling water, stir in the sugar and drizzle over the cooled cakes.

Recipe notes

Uses the Lidl Deluxe Espresso Martini drink as requested. The coffee glaze is optional. Air-fryer timings were recorded for two 5-inch round tins.
