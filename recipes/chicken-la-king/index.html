Chicken à la King

Tender chicken, peppers and mushrooms in a creamy sauce, served over rice, toast or potatoes.

Ingredients

  • 500 g cooked chicken, chopped,
  • 1 onion, finely chopped,
  • 1 red pepper, diced,
  • 150 g mushrooms, sliced,
  • 30 g butter,
  • 30 g plain flour,
  • 400 ml chicken stock,
  • 150 ml milk or single cream,
  • ½ tsp paprika, Salt and black pepper, Chopped parsley, optional

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a large saucepan.
  2. Add the onion, pepper and mushrooms and cook for 5–7 minutes until softened., Stir in the flour and paprika and cook for 1 minute., Gradually whisk in the chicken stock until smooth.
  3. Simmer for 4–5 minutes, stirring, until the sauce thickens., Stir in the milk or cream and cooked chicken.
  4. Simmer gently for 5 minutes until the chicken is piping hot
  5. do not boil hard., Season to taste and garnish with parsley if desired.
  6. Serve with rice, toast or mashed potatoes.
