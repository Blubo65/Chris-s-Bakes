Flourless Chocolate Cake

A rich, fudgy chocolate cake made without flour, with a delicate crust and soft centre.

Time: 45 mins plus cooling · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 200 g dark chocolate,
  • chopped
  • 150 g butter
  • 150 g caster sugar
  • 4 medium eggs
  • 25 g cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt
  • Icing sugar or berries,
  • to serve (optional)

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 170°C (fan 150°C).
  2. Grease and line a 20 cm round cake tin.
  3. Melt the chocolate and butter together gently, then allow to cool slightly.
  4. Whisk the eggs and sugar until thick and pale.
  5. Fold in the chocolate mixture, vanilla and salt.
  6. Sift in the cocoa powder and fold gently until combined.
  7. Pour into the tin and bake for 25–32 minutes until the edges are set and the centre still has a slight wobble.
  8. Cool in the tin; the cake will settle as it cools.
  9. Dust with icing sugar or serve with berries if desired.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Flourless Chocolate Cake bookmark. Check ingredients if baking for someone with allergies. Source: https://en.simplefoodrecipes.info/flourless-chocolate-cake/
