One-Pot Creamy Spinach Pasta

A creamy one-pot pasta with garlic, spinach and a smooth dairy-free or regular creamy sauce.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g dried pasta
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 750 ml vegetable stock,
  • hot
  • 150 g fresh spinach
  • 150 ml oat cream or single cream
  • 2 tbsp nutritional yeast or 40 g grated Parmesan
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Black pepper
  • Lemon zest,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large deep pan.
  2. Add the garlic and cook gently for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the pasta and hot stock.
  4. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered, stirring frequently, for 10–12 minutes or according to the pasta packet.
  5. Add a little hot water if needed.
  6. When the pasta is nearly tender, stir in the spinach and let it wilt.
  7. Lower the heat and stir in the cream and nutritional yeast or Parmesan.
  8. Heat gently until creamy, without boiling hard.
  9. Season with black pepper and add a little lemon zest if desired.
  10. Serve immediately.
