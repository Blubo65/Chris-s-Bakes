Jamaican Sweet Potato Pudding

A dense, warmly spiced Caribbean-style sweet potato pudding with coconut and cinnamon.

Time: 1 hr 30 mins · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 700 g sweet potato,
  • peeled and finely grated
  • 200 ml coconut milk
  • 100 g plain flour
  • 100 g light brown sugar
  • 50 g raisins
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ½ tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 tbsp melted butter or oil

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Grease and line a 20 cm square or round cake tin.
  3. Mix the grated sweet potato with the coconut milk, flour, sugar, raisins, cinnamon, nutmeg, vanilla, salt and melted butter or oil.
  4. Stir until evenly combined.
  5. The mixture should be thick and moist.
  6. Spoon into the prepared tin and level the surface.
  7. Bake for 60–75 minutes until firm in the centre and browned on top.
  8. Cover loosely if it darkens too quickly.
  9. Cool in the tin before slicing; the texture becomes firmer as it cools.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Vegan Jamaican Sweet Potato Pudding bookmark. Grated sweet potato moisture varies; bake until the centre is set. Source: https://healthiersteps.com/recipe/vegan-jamaican-sweet-potato-pudding
