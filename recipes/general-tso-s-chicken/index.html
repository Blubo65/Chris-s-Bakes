General Tso’s Chicken

Crispy air fryer chicken tossed in a tangy, sweet and mildly spicy General Tso-style sauce.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 egg,
  • beaten
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp oil spray
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp honey or brown sugar
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • ½ tsp chilli flakes, optional,
  • 100 ml water
  • 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp cold water

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with the beaten egg, then coat with 3 tbsp cornflour.
  2. Preheat the air fryer to 200°C.
  3. Arrange the chicken in a single layer, lightly spray with oil and air fry for 10–14 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and the centre reaches 75°C.
  4. Cook in batches if needed.
  5. Meanwhile, put the soy sauce, hoisin, vinegar, honey, garlic, ginger, chilli flakes and water into a small saucepan.
  6. Simmer for 2 minutes.
  7. Stir in the cornflour slurry and cook until the sauce thickens and becomes glossy.
  8. Toss the cooked chicken in the sauce just before serving.
  9. Serve with rice and vegetables.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer General Tso's Chicken bookmarks. Ensure chicken reaches 75°C internally. Sources: https://www.loveandotherspices.com/air-fryer-general-tsos-chicken and https://www.upstateramblings.com/air-fryer-general-tso-chicken
