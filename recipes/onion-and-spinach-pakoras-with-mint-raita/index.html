Onion & Spinach Pakoras with Mint Raita

Crispy onion and spinach fritters with warming spices, served with a cool mint yoghurt dip.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 large onion,
  • thinly sliced
  • 100 g spinach,
  • chopped
  • 120 g gram flour
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp chilli powder, optional,
  • 120–150 ml water
  • Salt,
  • to taste
  • Oil spray
  • For raita: 150 g natural yoghurt,
  • 1 tbsp chopped mint,
  • squeeze of lemon

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (fan 200°C), or air fryer to 190°C.
  2. Mix the gram flour, cumin, coriander, turmeric, chilli powder and salt in a bowl.
  3. Add the onion and spinach.
  4. Gradually mix in enough water to make a thick batter that clings to the vegetables.
  5. Spoon small mounds onto a lined tray or into the air fryer basket, leaving space between them.
  6. Spray lightly with oil.
  7. Bake for 18–25 minutes or air fry for 12–16 minutes, turning carefully halfway, until crisp and cooked through.
  8. Mix the yoghurt, mint and lemon juice for the raita.
  9. Serve with the hot pakoras.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked baked/air fryer adaptation inspired by the saved onion and spinach pakora bookmark. Source: https://www.theannoyedthyroid.com/2013/08/26/meatless-monday-onion-and-spinach-pakoras-with-mint-raita
