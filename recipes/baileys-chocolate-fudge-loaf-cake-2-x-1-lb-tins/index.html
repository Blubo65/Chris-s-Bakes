Baileys Chocolate Fudge Loaf Cake (2 x 1 lb tins)

A rich, moist chocolate loaf cake flavoured with Baileys, topped with chocolate Baileys buttercream and finished with sprinkles and truffles. Adapted for a Ninja air fryer and two 1 lb loaf tins.

Ingredients

  • For the cakes:
  • 175g dark chocolate
  • 175g unsalted butter
  • 2 tsp instant coffee
  • 100ml boiling water
  • 135g plain flour
  • 25g cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 300g light brown sugar or caster sugar
  • 3 medium eggs
  • 75ml Baileys
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • For the frosting:
  • 125g unsalted butter, softened (block butter, not spread)
  • 250g icing sugar
  • 25g cocoa powder
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract (optional)
  • 50–75ml Baileys
  • To decorate:
  • Chocolate sprinkles
  • Chocolate truffles

Method

  1. Prepare the tins: Grease and line two 1 lb loaf tins with baking paper. Preheat the Ninja air fryer to 150°C for 3 minutes. Make sure the tins fit with space around them
  2. cook one at a time if necessary.
  3. Melt the chocolate and butter together in a microwave-safe bowl, stirring until smooth.
  4. Dissolve the instant coffee in the boiling water and stir it into the chocolate mixture.
  5. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, bicarbonate of soda and sugar.
  6. In a separate bowl, mix the eggs, Baileys and lemon juice. Add the chocolate mixture and egg mixture to the dry ingredients and gently fold until smooth. Do not overmix.
  7. Divide the mixture evenly between the two tins. Do not fill either tin more than two-thirds full.
  8. Air fry at 150°C for approximately 35–50 minutes. Start checking at 30–35 minutes with a skewer
  9. it should come out without wet batter, though a few moist crumbs are fine. If the tops brown too quickly, loosely cover with foil, securely held so it cannot touch the heating element. Cook in 5-minute increments if needed. If the tins do not fit comfortably together, cook them separately.
  10. Leave the cakes in their tins for 10–15 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack and cool completely.
  11. Make the frosting:
  12. Beat the softened butter for 2–3 minutes until creamy. Sift together the icing sugar and cocoa, then gradually beat into the butter. Add vanilla if using, then beat in 50ml Baileys a little at a time. Add more only if needed for a smooth, spreadable consistency.
  13. Decorate:
  14. When the cakes are completely cool, spread or pipe the frosting over the tops and finish with chocolate sprinkles and truffles.
