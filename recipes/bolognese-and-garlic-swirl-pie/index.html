Bolognese & Garlic Swirl Pie

A savoury beef bolognese filling topped with puff pastry swirls flavoured with garlic and herbs.

Time: 1 hr · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g lean beef mince
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 carrot,
  • finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 400 g passata
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 sheet ready-rolled puff pastry
  • 1 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp garlic granules
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Cook the mince in a frying pan until browned, then add the onion and carrot and cook for 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in one crushed garlic clove, passata, tomato purée and oregano.
  4. Simmer for 15–20 minutes until thick.
  5. Season and cool slightly.
  6. Unroll the puff pastry.
  7. Brush with melted butter mixed with garlic granules, the remaining garlic and parsley.
  8. Roll up from the long edge and slice into 2 cm swirls.
  9. Spoon the thick bolognese filling into an ovenproof dish.
  10. Arrange the pastry swirls over the top, cut-side up.
  11. Bake for 20–25 minutes until the pastry is puffed, golden and cooked through.
  12. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Recipe notes
