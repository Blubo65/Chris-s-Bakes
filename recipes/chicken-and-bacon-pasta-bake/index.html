Chicken & Bacon Pasta Bake

A family pasta bake with chicken, smoky bacon and a creamy tomato sauce topped with melted cheese.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g dried pasta
  • 350 g chicken breast,
  • diced
  • 150 g smoked bacon, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 400 g passata
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried mixed herbs
  • 100 g grated cheddar
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Cook the pasta until just under al dente, then drain.
  3. Heat the oil in a frying pan.
  4. Cook the bacon for 3 minutes, then add the chicken and cook until browned and cooked through.
  5. Add the onion and cook for 4 minutes.
  6. Stir in the garlic, passata and herbs, then simmer for 8–10 minutes.
  7. Mix the sauce with the pasta and transfer to an ovenproof dish.
  8. Scatter with grated cheese.
  9. Bake for 15–20 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  10. Rest for a few minutes before serving.
