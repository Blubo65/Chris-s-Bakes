Kebab Shop Yoghurt Garlic Sauce

A cool creamy garlic sauce for kebabs, wraps, chips and grilled chicken.

Time: 5 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 150 g thick natural yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise, optional,
  • 1 garlic clove,
  • finely grated
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh mint or parsley
  • Pinch of salt
  • Black pepper
  • 1–2 tsp water,
  • if needed

Method

  1. Stir the yoghurt and mayonnaise if using in a small bowl until smooth.
  2. Mix in the grated garlic, lemon juice, herbs, salt and pepper.
  3. Add a teaspoon of water if you prefer a thinner sauce.
  4. Taste and adjust the garlic, lemon and seasoning.
  5. Chill for 10–15 minutes before serving if time allows.
