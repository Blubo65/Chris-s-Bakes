Steak Bites & Peppers

Juicy steak bites and sweet peppers seasoned with garlic and herbs, cooked quickly in the air fryer.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 3

Ingredients

  • 500 g steak,
  • cut into 2–3 cm cubes
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 1 green pepper,
  • diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic granules
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 200°C.
  2. Pat the steak cubes dry.
  3. Toss the steak and peppers with olive oil, garlic granules, paprika, salt and pepper.
  4. Arrange in a single layer in the basket, working in batches if needed.
  5. Air fry for 6–10 minutes, shaking halfway, until the steak is cooked to your preference and peppers are tender.
  6. Rest the steak briefly before serving.
  7. Cooking time depends on cube size and desired doneness.

Recipe notes
