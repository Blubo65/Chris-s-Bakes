Chickpea & Spinach Curry

A quick, hearty vegetarian curry of chickpeas and spinach in a spiced tomato sauce.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 x 400 g tins chickpeas,
  • drained and rinsed
  • 150 g fresh spinach
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 200 ml vegetable stock or water
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Lemon juice,
  • to taste

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a saucepan and cook the onion for 5 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder and cumin.
  3. Stir for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes, stock and chickpeas.
  5. Simmer for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Stir in the spinach a handful at a time until wilted.
  7. Add a splash of water if the curry is too thick.
  8. Season with salt, pepper and a squeeze of lemon juice.
  9. Serve with rice or flatbreads.
