Chicken Bhuna Curry

A rich, thick chicken curry with onions, tomatoes and fragrant spices, served with rice or naan.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken thighs or breast,
  • diced
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp chilli powder,
  • or to taste
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 100 ml chicken stock
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a heavy pan.
  2. Cook the onions for 8–10 minutes until soft and golden.
  3. Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder, cumin, coriander, turmeric and chilli.
  4. Stir for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the chicken and stir to coat in the spices.
  6. Cook for 4–5 minutes until the outside changes colour.
  7. Add the tomatoes and stock.
  8. Simmer uncovered for 25–30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce is thick.
  9. Season to taste.
  10. Serve with rice or flatbreads.
