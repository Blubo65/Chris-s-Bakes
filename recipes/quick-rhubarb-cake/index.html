Quick Rhubarb Cake

A simple one-bowl cake with fresh rhubarb pieces and a tender, lightly sweet crumb.

Time: 50 mins · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 200 g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 150 g caster sugar
  • 2 medium eggs
  • 100 g melted butter
  • 100 ml milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250 g rhubarb,
  • cut into 1 cm pieces
  • 1 tbsp sugar,
  • for topping

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Grease and line a 20 cm round cake tin.
  3. Mix the flour, baking powder and caster sugar in a large bowl.
  4. Whisk the eggs with the melted butter, milk and vanilla.
  5. Stir into the dry ingredients until just combined.
  6. Fold in the rhubarb and spoon the batter into the tin.
  7. Sprinkle the extra sugar over the top.
  8. Bake for 30–40 minutes until golden and a skewer inserted into the cake part comes out clean.
  9. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then transfer to a rack.

Recipe notes
