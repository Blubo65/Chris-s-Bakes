Cheesy Potato Gratin

Thinly sliced potatoes baked in a creamy garlic sauce with a golden cheese topping.

Time: 1 hr 10 mins · Makes 6

Ingredients

  • 1 kg potatoes,
  • peeled and thinly sliced
  • 300 ml milk
  • 200 ml single cream
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 100 g grated mature cheddar
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Butter,
  • for greasing

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Grease a shallow ovenproof dish with butter.
  3. Arrange the potato slices in overlapping layers in the dish, seasoning lightly with salt, pepper and thyme.
  4. Whisk the milk, cream and garlic together and pour over the potatoes.
  5. The liquid should come about two-thirds of the way up the layers.
  6. Cover with foil and bake for 40 minutes.
  7. Remove the foil, scatter with cheese and bake for another 20–25 minutes until the potatoes are tender and the top is golden.
  8. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Cheesy Potato Gratin bookmark. Check the potatoes are tender before removing from the oven. Source: https://www.slimmingeats.com/blog/potato-gratin
