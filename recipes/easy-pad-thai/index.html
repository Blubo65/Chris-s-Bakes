Easy Pad Thai

A quick Thai-inspired stir-fried rice noodle dish with egg, vegetables, peanuts and a tangy sauce.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 250 g flat rice noodles
  • 300 g chicken breast or peeled prawns (optional)
  • 2 eggs,
  • beaten
  • 2 garlic cloves,
  • chopped
  • 1 red pepper,
  • thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce, optional,
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 100 g beansprouts
  • 30 g roasted peanuts,
  • chopped
  • Sliced spring onions,
  • to garnish

Method

  1. Soak or cook the rice noodles according to the packet instructions, then drain.
  2. Mix the soy sauce, fish sauce if using, lime juice and brown sugar in a small bowl.
  3. Heat the oil in a wok.
  4. If using chicken or prawns, stir-fry until cooked through and set aside.
  5. Add the garlic and pepper and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
  6. Push to one side, pour in the eggs and scramble until just set.
  7. Add the noodles, sauce, cooked protein if using and beansprouts.
  8. Toss over high heat until hot and evenly coated.
  9. Serve with peanuts and spring onions, with extra lime wedges if desired.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Instant Pot Pad Thai bookmark; this is a stovetop version. Source: https://thebetteredblondie.com/instant-pot-pad-thai-gluten-free
