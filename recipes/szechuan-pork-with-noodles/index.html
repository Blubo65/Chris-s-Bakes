Szechuan pork with noodles

A hot and garlicky Szechuan-style pork stir-fry with noodles and crunchy vegetables.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 350g dried egg noodles
  • 500g lean pork mince (5% fat or less)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce or tamari
  • 2 tbsp sherry vinegar or rice wine vinegar
  • Low-calorie cooking spray
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3cm piece of root ginger,
  • peeled and grated
  • 2 chillies,
  • deseeded and finely sliced
  • 1 tsp Szechuan peppercorns,
  • crushed (or dried chilli flakes)
  • ½ bunch spring onions,
  • sliced diagonally
  • 1 red pepper,
  • deseeded and sliced
  • 100g baby sweetcorn,
  • sliced
  • 100g asparagus tips,
  • sliced
  • 150ml hot chicken stock

Method

  1. Cook the noodles according to the pack instructions.
  2. Drain and keep warm.
  3. Put the pork, soy sauce and vinegar in a bowl and mix well.
  4. Place a non-stick wok or deep frying pan over a high heat.
  5. Add the pork mixture and stir-fry for 8–10 minutes or until browned and cooked through.
  6. Drain off any fat and tip the pork into a bowl.
  7. Wipe the pan, spray with low-calorie cooking spray and turn the heat to medium.
  8. Add the garlic, ginger, chillies, Szechuan peppercorns, spring onions, red pepper, sweetcorn and asparagus and stir-fry for 3 minutes.
  9. Add the stock and bring to the boil over a high heat.
  10. Turn the heat down to medium and simmer for 5 minutes or until reduced and thickened.
  11. Return the pork to the pan and cook for 2–3 minutes, then stir in the noodles and serve hot.

Recipe notes

FREE, 45 minutes, serves 4. The supplied PDF says it is suitable for freezing without adding the noodles.
