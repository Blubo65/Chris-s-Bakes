Buffalo Roasted Cauliflower

Roasted cauliflower florets coated in a tangy, spicy buffalo-style sauce.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower,
  • cut into florets
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 3 tbsp hot sauce
  • 1 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp honey,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (fan 200°C), or air fryer to 190°C.
  2. Toss the cauliflower with olive oil, garlic granules, salt and pepper.
  3. Roast on a tray for 18–22 minutes, or air fry for 12–16 minutes, shaking halfway, until browned and nearly tender.
  4. Mix the hot sauce with melted butter and honey if using.
  5. Toss the cauliflower in the sauce and return to the oven or air fryer for 3–5 minutes until glazed and tender.
  6. Serve hot as a side or snack.

Recipe notes

Home adaptation inspired by the saved Buffalo Roasted Cauliflower bookmark. Adjust hot sauce to taste. Source: https://www.livinglocurto.com/buffalo-roasted-cauliflower-air-fryer
