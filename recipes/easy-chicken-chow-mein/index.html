Easy Chicken Chow Mein

A takeaway-inspired noodle stir-fry with chicken, cabbage, carrots and a savoury soy sauce.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 250 g egg noodles
  • 350 g chicken breast,
  • thinly sliced
  • 1 carrot,
  • cut into matchsticks
  • 150 g cabbage,
  • shredded
  • 2 spring onions,
  • sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce, optional,
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp water

Method

  1. Cook the noodles according to the packet, drain and set aside.
  2. Toss the chicken with a little soy sauce.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil in a wok and stir-fry the chicken until cooked through; set aside.
  4. Add the carrot and cabbage and stir-fry for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  6. Return the chicken to the wok and add the noodles, remaining soy sauce, oyster sauce if using and sesame oil.
  7. Toss over high heat until piping hot.
  8. Add the cornflour slurry and stir briefly if you prefer a slightly glossy sauce.
  9. Scatter with spring onions and serve immediately.

Recipe notes
