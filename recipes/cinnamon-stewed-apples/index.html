Cinnamon Stewed Apples

Soft apples gently cooked with cinnamon, perfect with porridge, yoghurt, pancakes or custard.

Time: 15 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 apples, peeled,
  • cored and diced
  • 2 tbsp water
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1–2 tsp sugar or sweetener,
  • optional

Method

  1. Put the diced apples, water, cinnamon and lemon juice into a saucepan.
  2. Cover and cook gently for 8–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the apples are soft but still hold some shape.
  3. Add a little more water if the pan becomes dry.
  4. Sweeten to taste if desired.
  5. Serve warm or cool and refrigerate in a covered container for up to 3 days.
