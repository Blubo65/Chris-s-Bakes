Cheesy Fajita Pasta

A creamy cheesy pasta with chicken, peppers and fajita spices.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 400 g chicken breast,
  • sliced
  • 300 g dried pasta
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 peppers,
  • sliced
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning
  • 600 ml chicken stock,
  • hot
  • 150 g cream cheese
  • 100 g grated cheddar
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large deep pan.
  2. Cook the chicken until lightly browned.
  3. Add the onion and peppers and cook for 4–5 minutes.
  4. Stir in the fajita seasoning.
  5. Add the pasta and hot stock.
  6. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered for 12–16 minutes, stirring often, until pasta is tender.
  7. Add a splash of water if needed.
  8. Lower the heat and stir in the cream cheese and cheddar until smooth and creamy.
  9. Check the chicken is cooked through and the dish is piping hot.
  10. Season to taste and serve.

Recipe notes
