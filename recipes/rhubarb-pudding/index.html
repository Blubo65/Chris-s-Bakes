Rhubarb Pudding

A comforting British rhubarb pudding with a hint of vanilla, delicious served warm with custard or vanilla ice cream.

Ingredients

  • 115 g self-raising flour
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 55 g butter or margarine, cut into pieces
  • 2 tsp vanilla sugar
  • 115 g granulated sugar
  • 450 g young rhubarb, cut into 2 cm pieces
  • 140 ml milk
  • Butter, for greasing the dish
  • Caster sugar, for dusting (optional)
  • Custard or vanilla ice cream, to serve (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C conventional / 180°C fan (Gas Mark 6).
  2. Sift the flour and salt into a mixing bowl.
  3. Add the butter or margarine and rub into the flour with your fingertips until the mixture resembles fine breadcrumbs.
  4. Stir in the granulated sugar, vanilla sugar and rhubarb.
  5. Beat in the milk to make a thick batter.
  6. Spoon into a buttered 1-litre baking dish and bake for 40 minutes, until golden.
  7. Serve warm, dusted with caster sugar if desired, alongside custard or vanilla ice cream.
