Corned Beef Pasties

Classic savoury pasties filled with corned beef, potato and onion in crisp golden pastry.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet ready-rolled shortcrust pastry,
  • about 320 g
  • 1 x 340 g tin corned beef,
  • diced
  • 1 medium potato,
  • peeled and diced small
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • Black pepper
  • 1 egg, beaten, for glazing

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Boil the diced potato for 8–10 minutes until just tender, then drain and cool.
  3. Mix the corned beef, potato, onion, Worcestershire sauce and black pepper gently in a bowl.
  4. Cut the pastry into 4 circles or squares.
  5. Divide the filling between them, leaving the edges clear.
  6. Brush the edges with beaten egg, fold over and crimp firmly to seal.
  7. Cut a small steam vent in each pasty and glaze the tops.
  8. Bake for 25–30 minutes until golden and crisp.
  9. Allow to cool slightly before serving.

Recipe notes
