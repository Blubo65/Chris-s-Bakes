Syn Free Chinese Chicken Curry

A lighter Chinese-style chicken curry with tender chicken, peas and green pepper in a smooth homemade vegetable and spice sauce.

Ingredients

  • For the sauce:
  • 100g onion, chopped
  • 150g courgette, chopped
  • 150g butternut squash, chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1½ tbsp curry powder (medium or hot)
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • ¼ tsp Chinese five-spice
  • 720ml chicken stock
  • Low-calorie cooking spray
  • For the curry:
  • 450g chicken breast, diced
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 green pepper, chopped
  • 80ml frozen peas

Method

  1. Spray a frying pan with low-calorie cooking spray and heat over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the sauce onion, courgette and butternut squash
  3. fry for 2–3 minutes until beginning to soften.
  4. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for another minute.
  5. Stir in the curry powder, Chinese five-spice, tomato purée and soy sauce to coat the vegetables.
  6. Pour in the chicken stock, bring to the boil, cover and simmer for 20 minutes.
  7. Blend the sauce until smooth and set aside.
  8. Spray the frying pan again, add the diced chicken and cook for about 5 minutes until lightly browned. Remove and set aside.
  9. Spray the pan again and fry the remaining onion and green pepper for 2 minutes.
  10. Return the chicken to the pan, pour over the blended sauce and stir in the peas. Bring to the boil, cover and simmer for about 5 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through (75°C in the centre).
  11. Serve with your choice of sides.
