Old-Fashioned Bread Pudding

A traditional, warmly spiced bread pudding made with dried fruit and leftover bread.

Time: 30–45 mins · Makes About 6

Ingredients

  • 500g white bread,
  • torn into pieces (crusts can be left on)
  • 600ml whole milk
  • 150g light brown sugar (or white sugar)
  • 400g mixed dried fruit
  • 2 tbsp mixed spice,
  • or more to taste
  • 2 large eggs,
  • beaten
  • 50–100g butter,
  • melted (optional)
  • Demerara sugar,
  • for sprinkling

Method

  1. Line a suitable air-fryer tin or tins with baking parchment.
  2. Put the torn bread in a large bowl and pour over the milk.
  3. Press the bread down and leave it to soak for a few minutes, longer if using crusts.
  4. Stir and mash until the bread breaks down into a thick, stodgy mixture.
  5. Melt the butter if using it and beat the eggs.
  6. Add the sugar, dried fruit, mixed spice, eggs and melted butter to the soaked bread.
  7. Mix thoroughly.
  8. Spoon into the prepared tin or tins and level the surface.
  9. Sprinkle with demerara sugar.
  10. Bake on the air-fryer BAKE setting at 160°C for about 30–45 minutes, depending on tin size and air fryer.
  11. Check that the centre is fully set; a skewer should come out without wet mixture, or the centre should reach about 98°C.
  12. If your air fryer heats mainly from above, carefully turn the pudding over near the end if the base needs more cooking.
  13. Cool before slicing.
  14. Serve with custard, cream or ice cream.

Recipe notes
