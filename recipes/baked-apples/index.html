Baked Apples

Warm baked apples filled with cinnamon, oats and a little brown sugar.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 2 firm eating apples
  • 2 tbsp rolled oats
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp raisins, optional,
  • 1 tsp butter,
  • divided
  • 2 tbsp water

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 170°C.
  2. Core the apples, leaving the base intact if possible.
  3. Mix the oats, brown sugar, cinnamon and raisins if using.
  4. Spoon the mixture into the apple centres and dot with a little butter.
  5. Place the apples in a small air-fryer-safe dish and add the water to the base.
  6. Air fry for 12–18 minutes, depending on apple size, until tender when pierced with a knife.
  7. Add a few more minutes if needed.
  8. Serve warm with custard, yoghurt or ice cream.

Recipe notes

Home adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Baked Apple recipe. Cooking time varies by apple variety and size. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kdo74NEWTxM
