Creamy Chicken and Potato Bake

Tender chicken and potatoes baked in a creamy seasoned egg sauce, finished with cherry tomatoes and melted mozzarella.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes (quantity not specified)
  • Chicken fillet (quantity not specified)
  • Onion (quantity not specified)
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g 33% cream
  • Cherry tomatoes (quantity not specified)
  • Mozzarella cheese (quantity not specified)
  • Dried garlic (3g for sauce, plus extra for potatoes)
  • Oregano (1g for chicken, 2g for sauce)
  • Coriander (3g for sauce)
  • Paprika (2g for chicken)
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Olive oil

Method

  1. Lightly oil individual air-fryer-safe baking dishes.
  2. Dice the onion and divide between the dishes.
  3. Cube the potatoes, season with salt, pepper and dried garlic, drizzle with olive oil and mix. Layer over the onions.
  4. Cut the chicken into pieces, season with salt, pepper, 1g oregano and 2g paprika, toss with a little oil and place over the potatoes.
  5. Whisk the 2 eggs with 200g cream, salt, pepper, 3g dried garlic, 2g oregano and 3g coriander.
  6. Pour the creamy egg mixture into the dishes.
  7. Air fry at 180°C for 35 minutes.
  8. Top with sliced cherry tomatoes and grated mozzarella.
  9. Air fry at 180°C for another 15 minutes, until golden and the chicken is fully cooked. Check the chicken reaches 75°C in the centre.
  10. Serve hot.
