Homemade Chicken Kievs

Chicken breasts filled with garlic-herb butter, coated in breadcrumbs and baked until golden.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 small chicken breasts
  • 60 g softened butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 100 g breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg,
  • beaten
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Oil spray

Method

  1. Mix the butter with garlic, parsley, salt and pepper.
  2. Shape into 4 small logs and chill until firm.
  3. Make a pocket in the side of each chicken breast and insert a piece of garlic butter.
  4. Secure with a cocktail stick if needed.
  5. Put flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs into separate shallow bowls.
  6. Coat each chicken breast in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs; repeat the egg and breadcrumb coating for a firmer crust.
  7. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  8. Place the Kievs on a lined tray, spray lightly with oil and bake for 25–30 minutes, depending on size, until golden and the centre reaches 75°C.
  9. Rest for 3 minutes before serving, taking care as the garlic butter inside will be hot.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Chicken Kievs recipe. Ensure chicken reaches 75°C internally and handle the hot butter carefully. Source: https://pinchofnom.com/recipes/chicken-kievs
