Easy All-Purpose Steak Marinade

A savoury garlic, soy and herb marinade for steak before grilling or pan-searing.

Time: 5 mins plus marinating · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice or red wine vinegar
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried herbs

Method

  1. Whisk all the ingredients together in a bowl or shake in a sealed jar.
  2. Place the steaks in a shallow non-metal dish or food-safe bag and pour over the marinade, coating both sides.
  3. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes to 4 hours; avoid excessively long marinating for thin steaks.
  4. Remove the steaks from the marinade and pat dry before cooking so they sear properly.
  5. Discard the used marinade, or boil it thoroughly before using as a sauce.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved steak marinade bookmark. Marinate refrigerated and discard used marinade unless boiled thoroughly. Source: https://www.withablast.net/best-marinade-in-existence
