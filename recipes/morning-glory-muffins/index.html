Morning Glory Muffins

Moist breakfast-style muffins packed with carrot, apple, raisins and a little warm spice.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 12

Ingredients

  • 200 g plain flour
  • 100 g light brown sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 medium carrots,
  • grated
  • 1 apple,
  • grated
  • 75 g raisins or sultanas
  • 2 medium eggs
  • 100 ml vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp milk,
  • if needed

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C) and line a 12-hole muffin tin with paper cases.
  2. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder and cinnamon in a large bowl.
  3. Stir in the grated carrot, apple and raisins.
  4. In a separate bowl, whisk the eggs with the oil and vanilla.
  5. Add to the dry ingredients and fold together just until combined.
  6. Add a little milk if the mixture is very stiff.
  7. Divide between the cases and bake for 18–23 minutes until risen and a skewer inserted into the centre comes out clean.
  8. Cool in the tin for 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Morning Glory Muffins recipe. Source: https://happyhooligans.ca/morning-glory-muffins-recipe
