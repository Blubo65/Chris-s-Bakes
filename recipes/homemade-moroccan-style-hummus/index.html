Homemade Moroccan-Style Hummus

Creamy homemade chickpea hummus with cumin, paprika, coriander and a little cayenne warmth.

Time: 10 mins · Makes 6

Ingredients

  • 1 x 400 g tin chickpeas,
  • drained and rinsed (reserve a little liquid)
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 small garlic clove,
  • crushed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil,
  • plus extra to serve
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ¼ tsp ground coriander
  • Pinch of cayenne pepper,
  • or to taste
  • 2–4 tbsp cold water or reserved chickpea liquid
  • Salt,
  • to taste

Method

  1. Put the chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, cumin, paprika, coriander and cayenne into a food processor.
  2. Blend until mostly smooth, scraping down the sides as needed.
  3. Add cold water or reserved chickpea liquid a tablespoon at a time until the hummus reaches your preferred creamy consistency.
  4. Season with salt and extra lemon juice to taste, then blend briefly again.
  5. Spoon into a bowl, drizzle with a little olive oil and sprinkle with paprika.
  6. Serve with vegetables, flatbread or crackers.
