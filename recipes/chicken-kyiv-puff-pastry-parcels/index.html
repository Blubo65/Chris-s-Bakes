Chicken Kyiv Puff Pastry Parcels

Puff pastry parcels filled with chicken and garlic-herb butter, inspired by Chicken Kyiv flavours.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet ready-rolled puff pastry
  • 300 g cooked chicken,
  • finely chopped
  • 50 g softened butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 50 g grated mozzarella, optional,
  • 1 egg,
  • beaten
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Mix the butter with garlic, parsley, black pepper and mozzarella if using.
  3. Cut the pastry into 4 squares.
  4. Divide the cooked chicken between the squares and add a little garlic butter to each.
  5. Brush the pastry edges with beaten egg, fold into parcels and press the edges firmly to seal.
  6. Place seam-side down on a lined tray.
  7. Brush the tops with beaten egg and cut a small steam vent in each parcel.
  8. Bake for 20–25 minutes until puffed and golden and the filling is piping hot.
  9. Rest for 3 minutes before serving.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Chicken Kyiv Puffs bookmark. This version uses cooked chicken for a reliable, fully heated filling. Source: https://vm.tiktok.com/ZGd9HWmG2/
