Lemon Bars

Bright, tangy lemon bars with a buttery shortbread-style base and soft lemon topping.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 6–8

Ingredients

  • 60g plain flour
  • 60g icing sugar,
  • plus extra for dusting
  • 60g unsalted butter,
  • softened
  • A pinch of salt
  • Spray oil
  • 2 free-range eggs
  • 150g granulated sugar
  • 60ml lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp finely grated lemon zest
  • ½ tsp baking powder

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 150°C.
  2. Mix the flour, icing sugar, butter and salt in a bowl until the mixture forms crumbs.
  3. Lightly spray a 15cm (6-inch) cake pan or oven-safe dish with oil, then press the crumb mixture firmly into the base.
  4. Mix the eggs, granulated sugar, lemon juice, lemon zest and baking powder in a bowl, then pour the mixture over the crumb base.
  5. Air fry at 150°C for about 20 minutes, or until the top is set and slightly golden.
  6. Remove the pan and allow the bars to cool before slicing.
  7. Dust with icing sugar before serving.

Recipe notes
