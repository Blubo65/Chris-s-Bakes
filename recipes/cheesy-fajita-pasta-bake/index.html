Cheesy Fajita Pasta Bake

Chicken fajita pasta baked under a golden cheese topping for a comforting family meal.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g dried pasta
  • 400 g chicken breast,
  • diced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 peppers,
  • sliced
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 150 ml chicken stock
  • 100 g cream cheese
  • 100 g grated cheddar
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Cook the pasta until just under al dente, then drain.
  3. Heat the oil in a pan and cook the chicken until browned.
  4. Add the onion and peppers and cook for 4 minutes.
  5. Stir in the fajita seasoning, chopped tomatoes and stock.
  6. Simmer for 8–10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
  7. Stir in the cream cheese, then mix with the pasta and transfer to an ovenproof dish.
  8. Scatter with cheddar and bake for 15–20 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  9. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Fajita Chicken Pasta bookmark. Source: http://www.slimmingeats.com/blog/fajita-chicken-pasta
