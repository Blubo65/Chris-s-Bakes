Fruity Slices

A buttery shortcake-style base topped with tea-soaked dried fruit, almonds and egg sponge.

Time: 30 mins plus fruit soaking and cooling · Makes 8–10 slices

Ingredients

  • Base:
  • 140g self-raising flour
  • 140g light brown sugar
  • 140g cold butter or margarine
  • Pinch of salt
  • Topping:
  • 175g mixed dried fruit
  • Tea,
  • for soaking the fruit
  • 55g flaked almonds
  • 2 large eggs (or 3 small eggs),
  • beaten
  • 100g light brown sugar
  • 85g self-raising flour
  • A little caster sugar,
  • to sprinkle

Method

  1. Soak the dried fruit in tea for at least 4 hours or overnight.
  2. Drain thoroughly before using.
  3. Line an 20cm square traybake tin that fits your air fryer.
  4. For the base, rub the cold butter into the flour and salt until crumbly, then rub in the 140g light brown sugar.
  5. Press the mixture evenly into the lined tin using the back of a damp spoon.
  6. Bake on the air-fryer BAKE setting at 160°C for about 10 minutes.
  7. Add a few minutes if needed, then allow the base to cool completely.
  8. Mix the drained fruit with the flaked almonds, beaten eggs, 100g light brown sugar and 85g self-raising flour until combined.
  9. Spread evenly over the cooled base and sprinkle lightly with caster sugar.
  10. Return to the air fryer at 160°C for about 20 minutes, until the topping is set and the centre reaches approximately 98°C.
  11. Timing depends on the tin and air fryer, so check near the end.
  12. Leave to cool before carefully removing and cutting into slices.

Recipe notes
