Sweet & Sour Vegetables

Crisp vegetables tossed in a bright sweet-and-sour sauce, served with rice or noodles.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 red pepper,
  • cut into chunks
  • 1 green pepper,
  • cut into chunks
  • 1 onion,
  • cut into wedges
  • 1 carrot,
  • thinly sliced
  • 150 g pineapple chunks, optional,
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp sugar or honey
  • 150 ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp cold water
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a wok or large frying pan.
  2. Stir-fry the peppers, onion and carrot for 4–5 minutes until just tender.
  3. Mix the soy sauce, vinegar, ketchup, sugar or honey and stock in a small bowl.
  4. Pour the sauce over the vegetables and add pineapple if using.
  5. Simmer for 2 minutes.
  6. Stir in the cornflour slurry and cook until the sauce thickens and turns glossy.
  7. Serve immediately with rice or noodles.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Sweet and Sour Vegetables recipe. Source: https://www.justaveragejen.com/sweet-and-sour.html
