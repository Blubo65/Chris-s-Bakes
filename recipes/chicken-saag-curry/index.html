Chicken Saag Curry

Chicken simmered with spinach, tomatoes and fragrant spices for a hearty green curry.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast or thighs,
  • diced
  • 200 g spinach,
  • fresh or frozen
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 150 ml chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 2 tbsp natural yoghurt,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onion for 5 minutes until softened.
  2. Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder and cumin and stir for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the chicken and cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring until the outside changes colour.
  4. Add the tomatoes and stock.
  5. Simmer for 18–22 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
  6. Stir in the spinach and cook until wilted.
  7. Blend a portion of the spinach sauce if you prefer a smoother curry.
  8. Stir in yoghurt off the heat if using, season to taste and serve with rice.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved chicken saag recipe. Source: https://www.slimmingworld.co.uk/recipes/slimming-world-chicken-dopiaza
