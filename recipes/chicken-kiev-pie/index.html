Chicken Kiev Pie

A comforting chicken and leek pie with a creamy garlic-parsley filling and buttery garlic crumb topping.

Time: 35–40 mins · Makes 6

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet ready-rolled shortcrust pastry,
  • enough to line and cover your pie dish
  • 800g cooked chicken breast,
  • shredded
  • 120g leek,
  • finely diced
  • 40g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves,
  • finely minced
  • For the sauce:
  • 25g butter
  • 8g plain flour
  • 150ml milk
  • 50ml chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped fresh parsley
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp white pepper
  • For the topping:
  • 50g panko breadcrumbs
  • 3–4 tbsp finely chopped parsley
  • ½ tsp garlic powder or granulated garlic
  • 20g butter,
  • melted
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Heat the air fryer or oven to 200°C.
  2. Make sure the chicken is fully cooked and shred it into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Melt 40g butter in a pan and soften the leek for 5–7 minutes.
  4. Add the minced garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  5. For the sauce, melt 25g butter in a separate pan, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  6. Gradually whisk in the milk and chicken stock until smooth and thickened.
  7. Stir in the parsley, salt and white pepper, then combine with the chicken and leek.
  8. Allow the filling to cool slightly.
  9. Line a suitable pie dish with shortcrust pastry, add the filling and cover with pastry.
  10. Seal the edges and make a small steam hole.
  11. Mix the panko, parsley, garlic powder, melted butter and pinch of salt, then scatter over the top or press onto the pastry surface.
  12. Bake at 200°C for 35–40 minutes, until the pastry is golden and the filling is piping hot.
  13. For an air fryer, use a dish that fits with space for air circulation and check the top early; loosely cover with foil if it browns too quickly.

Recipe notes

Source saved in Raindrop: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lA7qtQnNmuI . The source describes an oven bake at 200°C for 35–40 minutes; the air-fryer guidance is a cautious adaptation and has not been tested by Chris. Source credits the original pie inspiration to Baldy's Pies, Wigan.
