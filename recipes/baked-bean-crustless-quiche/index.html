Baked Bean Crustless Quiche

A budget-friendly egg bake with baked beans, cheese and vegetables, with no pastry crust.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 6 medium eggs
  • 1 x 400 g tin baked beans
  • 100 g grated cheddar
  • 1 small onion,
  • chopped
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 100 ml milk
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried mixed herbs

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Lightly grease an ovenproof dish.
  3. Cook the onion and pepper in a non-stick pan for 5 minutes until softened.
  4. Whisk the eggs with the milk, cheese, herbs, salt and pepper.
  5. Stir in the baked beans and softened vegetables.
  6. Pour into the dish and bake for 25–35 minutes until set in the centre and lightly golden.
  7. Rest for 5 minutes before slicing.
  8. Serve warm with salad.

Recipe notes
