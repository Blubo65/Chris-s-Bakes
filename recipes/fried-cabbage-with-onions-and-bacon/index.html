Fried Cabbage with Onions & Bacon

Sweet tender cabbage pan-fried with smoky bacon and onions for a simple savoury side.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 small white cabbage,
  • shredded
  • 150 g smoked bacon, chopped
  • 1 onion,
  • thinly sliced
  • 1 garlic clove,
  • crushed
  • 1 tbsp oil,
  • if needed
  • Black pepper
  • Splash of water or stock

Method

  1. Heat a large frying pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the bacon and cook until browned and some fat has rendered.
  3. Add the onion and cook for 4–5 minutes until softened, adding a little oil if the pan is dry.
  4. Add the garlic and stir for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the shredded cabbage and a splash of water or stock.
  6. Toss well, cover and cook for 8–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender to your liking.
  7. Season with black pepper and serve hot.
