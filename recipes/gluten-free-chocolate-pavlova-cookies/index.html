Gluten-Free Chocolate Pavlova Cookies

Crisp-on-the-outside chocolate meringue cookies with soft, brownie-like centres.

Time: 1 hr 15 mins · Makes 12

Ingredients

  • 3 large egg whites,
  • room temperature
  • 150 g caster sugar
  • 25 g cocoa powder,
  • sifted
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • 50 g dark chocolate chips, optional,
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 150°C (fan 130°C).
  2. Line a baking tray with parchment.
  3. Whisk the egg whites with the salt until soft peaks form.
  4. Add the sugar gradually, whisking until the meringue is thick and glossy.
  5. Sift in the cocoa and cornflour, add vanilla and fold gently.
  6. Fold in chocolate chips if using.
  7. Spoon 12 mounds onto the tray, leaving space between them.
  8. Bake for 25–30 minutes, then turn off the oven and leave the cookies inside with the door slightly ajar for 20 minutes.
  9. Cool completely before lifting from the parchment.

Recipe notes
