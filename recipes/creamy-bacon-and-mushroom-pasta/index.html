Creamy Bacon & Mushroom Pasta

A quick creamy pasta with smoky bacon, mushrooms and a simple garlic cream sauce.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g pasta
  • 150 g smoked bacon, chopped
  • 250 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 150 ml single cream
  • 100 ml pasta cooking water
  • 40 g grated Parmesan or mature cheddar
  • 1 tsp olive oil,
  • if needed
  • Black pepper
  • Chopped parsley,
  • optional

Method

  1. Cook the pasta in boiling salted water according to the packet.
  2. Reserve a mug of cooking water before draining.
  3. Meanwhile, cook the bacon in a large frying pan until golden.
  4. Add the mushrooms and cook for 5–6 minutes until browned, adding a little oil if needed.
  5. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  6. Lower the heat and stir in the cream and a splash of pasta water.
  7. Add the drained pasta and grated cheese and toss until coated.
  8. Add more pasta water if needed to loosen the sauce.
  9. Season with black pepper and garnish with parsley if desired.
