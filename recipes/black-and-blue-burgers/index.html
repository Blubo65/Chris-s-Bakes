Black & Blue Burgers

Cajun-spiced beef burgers topped with tangy blue cheese and a cooling yoghurt dressing.

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef mince,
  • 1 tsp Cajun seasoning,
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika, Salt and black pepper,
  • 4 burger buns,
  • 100 g blue cheese, crumbled,
  • 100 g Greek yoghurt,
  • 1 tsp lemon juice, Lettuce and tomato, to serve

Method

  1. Mix the beef mince with Cajun seasoning, paprika, salt and pepper.
  2. Shape into 4 patties and chill for 10 minutes if time allows., Mix the Greek yoghurt with lemon juice and a pinch of pepper to make a simple dressing., Heat a grill or frying pan to medium-high.
  3. Cook the burgers for about 4–6 minutes per side, depending on thickness, until cooked through., During the final minute, scatter blue cheese over each burger and allow it to soften., Serve in toasted buns with lettuce, tomato and the yoghurt dressing.
