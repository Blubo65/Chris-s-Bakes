Keema Cottage Pie

A comfort classic with a warming Indian twist, topped with delicately spiced sweet potato mash.

Time: 1 hour ·

Ingredients

  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 5cm piece fresh root ginger,
  • peeled and roughly chopped
  • ½ small pack fresh coriander
  • 2½ tbsp mild curry powder*
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tsp ground coriander
  • 2 large onions,
  • diced
  • 5 tbsp tomato purée
  • 750g lean beef mince (5% fat or less)
  • 1 beef stock cube,
  • crumbled
  • 200g passata
  • 160g frozen peas
  • For the mash:
  • 3 medium potatoes,
  • cut into chunks
  • 1 medium sweet potato,
  • cut into chunks
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp mild curry powder*

Method

  1. Put the garlic, ginger, fresh coriander, spices and 100ml water in a food processor and blitz to a smooth paste, or use a stick blender and a jug.
  2. Place a large non-stick saucepan over a medium heat and add the paste.
  3. Cover and cook for 5 minutes, adding a little water if it is looking dry.
  4. Add the onions and tomato purée and cook for 10 minutes, then stir in the beef and cook for a further 10 minutes, stirring often.
  5. Meanwhile, make the mash.
  6. Cook the potatoes, sweet potato, turmeric and curry powder in a saucepan of boiling water for 15 minutes or until tender, then drain, mash and season to taste.
  7. Preheat the oven to 200°C/fan 180°C/gas 6.
  8. Add the stock cube, passata and 300ml boiling water to the onions and cook for 5 minutes, then add the peas and cook for another 5 minutes.
  9. Spoon the beef mixture into an ovenproof dish.
  10. Top the pie with the mash, level with a fork and cook in the oven for 15 minutes.
  11. Serve with hot Speed vegetables.
