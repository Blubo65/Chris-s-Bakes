Healthy Berry Crumble Flapjacks

Oaty flapjack bars layered with a tangy mixed-berry filling and a crunchy nut crumble topping.

Prep: 10 mins · Cook: 30 mins · Total: 40 mins · Makes 12

Ingredients

Oat base and crumble

  • 225 g rolled oats
  • 100 g wholemeal flour
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp clear honey or 80 g soft brown sugar
  • 1 medium egg
  • 80 ml rapeseed or vegetable oil
  • 30 g walnuts or almonds, roughly chopped

Berry filling

  • 300 g frozen mixed berries
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp clear honey
  • 1 tbsp wholemeal flour

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C). Line a 20 cm square tin with non-stick baking parchment.
  2. Mix the oats, 100 g flour, baking powder and salt in a large bowl. In a separate bowl, whisk together the honey (or sugar), egg and oil. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and mix with a wooden spoon until combined.
  3. Press about two-thirds of the oat mixture firmly into the prepared tin to make an even base. Bake for 15 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, put the frozen berries, lemon juice, 2 tbsp honey and 1 tbsp flour into a small saucepan. Simmer over a medium-low heat for 3–5 minutes, stirring, until thick and jammy.
  5. Spoon the berry mixture over the baked oat base and spread evenly. Stir the chopped nuts through the remaining oat mixture, then crumble it over the berries.
  6. Return to the oven for 18–20 minutes, until the topping is golden.
  7. Leave to cool completely in the tin before cutting into 12 bars. They firm up as they cool.

Storage

Cool completely, then store in an airtight container. For the best texture, allow the bars to cool fully before cutting.
