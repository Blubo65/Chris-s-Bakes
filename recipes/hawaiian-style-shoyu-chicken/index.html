Hawaiian-Style Shoyu Chicken

Tender chicken simmered in a sweet-savoury soy, ginger and garlic sauce, served with rice.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 800 g boneless chicken thighs
  • 120 ml soy sauce
  • 250 ml water
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 spring onions,
  • sliced

Method

  1. In a saucepan or deep frying pan, combine the soy sauce, water, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, rice vinegar and sesame oil.
  2. Add the chicken thighs and bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. Turn the pieces to coat in the sauce.
  4. Cover and simmer gently for 20–25 minutes, turning once or twice, until the chicken is cooked through and tender.
  5. Remove the lid and simmer for a few minutes to reduce the sauce slightly, turning the chicken so it becomes glossy.
  6. Serve over rice with spring onions and some of the sauce.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Hawaiian Shoyu Chicken recipe. Ensure chicken is fully cooked through. Source: https://dinnerthendessert.com/hawaiian-shoyu-chicken/
