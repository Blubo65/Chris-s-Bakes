Cheesy Cauliflower Garlic Bread

A low-carb-inspired cauliflower base topped with garlic, herbs and melted cheese.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower,
  • grated or finely processed
  • 1 egg
  • 80 g grated mozzarella
  • 40 g grated Parmesan or mature cheddar
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp grated cheese,
  • extra for topping

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (fan 200°C).
  2. Line a baking tray with parchment.
  3. Microwave or steam the grated cauliflower until tender, then allow to cool enough to handle.
  4. Squeeze out as much moisture as possible using a clean tea towel.
  5. Mix the cauliflower with the egg, mozzarella, Parmesan, garlic, oregano and black pepper.
  6. Press into a rectangle about 1 cm thick on the tray.
  7. Bake for 18–22 minutes until firm and golden at the edges.
  8. Sprinkle with extra cheese and return to the oven for 3–5 minutes until melted.
  9. Slice into strips and serve warm.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved cheesy cauliflower garlic bread recipe. Squeezing the cauliflower very dry is important for the base to hold together. Source: http://www.slimmingeats.com/blog/syn-free-cheesy-cauliflower-garlic-bread/cookbook-print/15924
