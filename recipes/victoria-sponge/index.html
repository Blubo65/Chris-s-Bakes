Victoria Sponge

A classic Victoria sponge baked as two air fryer loaf cakes, layered with vanilla buttercream and strawberry jam.

Ingredients

  • For the sponge:
  • 200 g self-raising flour
  • 200 g granulated sugar
  • 200 g softened butter or margarine
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 medium eggs
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • For the filling:
  • 100 g softened butter
  • 140 g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 100 g strawberry jam
  • Icing sugar, for dusting (optional)

Method

  1. Cream the softened butter or margarine with the sugar until combined. Beat in the eggs one at a time, mixing between additions, then add the vanilla extract.
  2. Mix in the baking powder. Sift in the self-raising flour a little at a time and mix until fully combined. Add the milk and mix gently.
  3. Divide the mixture evenly between two 1 lb loaf tins lined with baking parchment and lightly greased.
  4. Preheat the air fryer. Bake at 140°C for approximately 45 minutes, checking from 30 minutes onwards because air fryers vary. The centre should reach about 98°C and a skewer should come out clean. Cook the tins separately if they do not fit together with good air circulation.
  5. Allow both sponges to cool completely before assembling.
  6. Beat the softened butter with the icing sugar and vanilla extract until smooth and creamy.
  7. Spread the buttercream over one cooled sponge and top with the strawberry jam. Place the second sponge on top. Dust with icing sugar if desired.
