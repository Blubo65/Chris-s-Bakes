Cheesy Cauliflower & Broccoli Bake

Cauliflower and broccoli baked in a creamy garlic cheese sauce until bubbling and golden.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower,
  • cut into florets
  • 1 medium broccoli,
  • cut into florets
  • 30 g butter
  • 30 g plain flour
  • 500 ml milk
  • 150 g mature cheddar,
  • grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • Salt and black pepper
  • ½ tsp paprika,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Boil or steam the cauliflower and broccoli for 5–7 minutes until just tender, then drain well.
  3. Melt the butter in a saucepan.
  4. Add the garlic and cook gently for 30 seconds.
  5. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  6. Gradually whisk in the milk and simmer for 4–6 minutes until thickened.
  7. Remove from the heat and stir in most of the cheese; season to taste.
  8. Put the vegetables in an ovenproof dish and pour over the cheese sauce.
  9. Sprinkle with remaining cheese and paprika if using.
  10. Bake for 20–25 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  11. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Recipe notes
