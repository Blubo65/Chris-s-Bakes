Cream Cheese Spaghetti

A quick creamy spaghetti sauce made with cream cheese, garlic and Parmesan.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g spaghetti
  • 150 g cream cheese
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 40 g grated Parmesan
  • 150 ml reserved pasta water
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Black pepper
  • Chopped parsley,
  • optional

Method

  1. Cook the spaghetti in boiling salted water according to the packet.
  2. Reserve a mug of pasta water before draining.
  3. Heat the olive oil in a pan over low heat.
  4. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds without browning.
  5. Stir in the cream cheese and 100 ml of pasta water until smooth.
  6. Add the spaghetti and Parmesan and toss until coated.
  7. Add more pasta water a little at a time to make a silky sauce.
  8. Season with black pepper and garnish with parsley if desired.
  9. Serve immediately.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Instant Pot Cream Cheese Spaghetti recipe; stovetop method. Source: https://12tomatoes.com/cream-cheese-spaghetti
