Chickpea and Spinach Curry

A hearty, meat-free and dairy-free curry with chickpeas, spinach, warming spices, tomatoes and creamy coconut milk.

Ingredients

  • 1 red pepper, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, finely chopped
  • 1 green chilli, deseeded and finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp sunflower oil
  • 2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  • 700g canned chickpeas, drained
  • 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 250ml vegetable stock
  • 250ml coconut milk
  • 150g baby spinach, chopped
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Pinch of fresh coriander, to garnish

Method

  1. Prepare the ingredients: Dice the red pepper. Peel and finely chop the garlic and ginger. Deseed and finely chop the green chilli.
  2. Heat the sunflower oil in a large saucepan over a medium heat until hot.
  3. Add the red pepper, chilli, garlic, ginger and a pinch of salt. Cook for about 5 minutes, until softened.
  4. Stir in the curry powder, paprika, ground coriander, cumin and cayenne pepper.
  5. Cook for 2 minutes, stirring, until fragrant.
  6. Drain the chickpeas and stir them into the pan with the chopped tomatoes, vegetable stock and coconut milk. Bring to the boil, then reduce the heat and simmer gently for 30–35 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chickpeas are tender and the sauce has thickened.
  7. Chop the spinach and stir it into the curry. Cook for a further 5 minutes until wilted.
  8. Season with lemon juice, salt and black pepper to taste.
  9. Serve with rice or vegan naan bread, garnished with fresh coriander.
