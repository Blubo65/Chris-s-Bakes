Hot Dog Wraps

Quick hot dogs wrapped in tortilla or pastry and air fried until crisp and golden.

Time: 15 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 frankfurter sausages
  • 4 small tortilla wraps
  • 4 slices cheddar, optional,
  • 1 tsp mustard, optional,
  • Oil spray

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  2. Pat the sausages dry.
  3. If using cheese, place a slice around each sausage.
  4. Add a little mustard if desired.
  5. Wrap each sausage tightly in a tortilla, securing the seam underneath.
  6. Lightly spray with oil.
  7. Air fry for 6–9 minutes, turning halfway, until the wraps are crisp and the sausages are piping hot.
  8. Serve immediately with your favourite sauces.

Recipe notes

Home adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Hot Dog Wraps bookmark. If using raw or uncooked sausages instead of frankfurters, cook them fully first according to packet instructions. Source: https://www.airfryeryum.com/air-fryer-hot-dog-wraps/
