One-Pot Creamy Cheesy Spicy Corn Orzo

Creamy orzo with sweetcorn, cheese and a little chilli, all cooked in one pan.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g orzo pasta
  • 1 small onion,
  • finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp chilli flakes,
  • or to taste
  • 700 ml vegetable stock,
  • hot
  • 200 g sweetcorn,
  • drained if tinned
  • 100 g cream cheese
  • 75 g grated cheddar
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Black pepper
  • Chopped parsley,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large deep pan.
  2. Cook the onion for 4 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the garlic, smoked paprika and chilli flakes and stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the orzo and hot stock.
  5. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered for 10–12 minutes, stirring frequently to prevent sticking.
  6. Stir in the sweetcorn and cook for another 2–3 minutes until the orzo is tender.
  7. Add a splash of hot water if needed.
  8. Lower the heat and stir in the cream cheese and cheddar until creamy and melted.
  9. Season with black pepper and garnish with parsley if desired.
