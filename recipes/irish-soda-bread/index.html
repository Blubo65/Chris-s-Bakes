Irish Soda Bread

A quick no-yeast soda bread with a rustic crust, made with flour and buttermilk.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 350 g plain flour,
  • plus extra for dusting
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 300 ml buttermilk,
  • approximately

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 160°C and line a small round tin that fits the basket.
  2. Mix the flour, bicarbonate of soda and salt in a bowl.
  3. Add most of the buttermilk and mix with a table knife until a soft, slightly sticky dough forms.
  4. Add the rest only if needed.
  5. Turn onto a lightly floured surface and shape gently into a round; do not knead heavily.
  6. Place in the tin and cut a deep cross on top.
  7. Air fry for 25–35 minutes, checking from 25 minutes.
  8. If the crust browns too quickly, reduce the temperature slightly.
  9. The loaf should sound hollow when tapped and be cooked through in the centre.
  10. Cool on a rack before slicing.

Recipe notes

Adapted air fryer method inspired by the saved Irish Soda Bread recipe. Tin size and air fryer model affect baking time; ensure the centre is cooked through. Source: https://nkechiajaeroh.com/recipes/air-fryer/homemade-irish-soda-bread-in-air-fryer-bread-soda
