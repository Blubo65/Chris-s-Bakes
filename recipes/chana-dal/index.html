Chana Dal

A comforting Indian-style split chickpea dhal with garlic, ginger and warming spices.

Time: 50 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 250 g chana dal,
  • rinsed and soaked for 30 minutes if possible
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tomato,
  • chopped
  • 750 ml water,
  • plus extra as needed
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt,
  • to taste
  • Fresh coriander,
  • optional

Method

  1. Drain the soaked dal and put it in a saucepan with the water and turmeric.
  2. Bring to a boil, skim if needed, then simmer for 35–45 minutes until tender, adding water if needed.
  3. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a small pan.
  4. Cook the onion until golden, then add the garlic, ginger, cumin and coriander and stir for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the chopped tomato and cook for 4–5 minutes until softened.
  6. Stir the spiced onion mixture into the cooked dal.
  7. Simmer for 5 minutes, mashing some dal to thicken if desired.
  8. Season with salt and garnish with coriander.
  9. Serve with rice or flatbread.

Recipe notes

Stovetop home adaptation inspired by the saved Instant Pot Chana Dal bookmark. Dried chana dal cooking time varies; cook until completely tender. Source: https://www.yellowthyme.com/instant-pot-chana-dal
