Hoisin Crispy Beef Noodles

Quick crispy beef mince and egg noodles tossed in a glossy hoisin, soy and Chinese five-spice sauce with spring onions.

Ingredients

  • 2 nests medium dried egg noodles
  • Sesame oil, for tossing the noodles
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp dark soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1/4 tsp Chinese five spice
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 1 tsp oil
  • 250 g beef mince
  • Sesame oil or chilli crisp oil, to serve (optional)

Method

  1. Cook the egg noodles according to the packet instructions. Drain, rinse in cold water and drizzle with sesame oil to stop them sticking. Set aside.
  2. Slice the spring onions. In a bowl, mix the hoisin sauce, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar, minced garlic and Chinese five spice.
  3. In a separate bowl, stir the cornflour and water together to make a slurry.
  4. Heat a wok over high heat with 1 tsp oil. Add the beef mince, break it up and cook for 5–6 minutes until browned and starting to crisp.
  5. Add the sauce mixture, reduce the heat and stir-fry for 1 minute. Add the cornflour slurry, bring to a low simmer and stir until thickened.
  6. Add the noodles and toss everything together for 2–3 minutes. Stir in the spring onions.
  7. Serve in bowls, with a drizzle of sesame oil or chilli crisp oil if you like.
