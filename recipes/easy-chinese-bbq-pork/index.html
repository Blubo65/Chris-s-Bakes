Easy Chinese BBQ Pork

Tender pork strips glazed with a sweet, savoury Chinese-style barbecue marinade.

Time: 40 mins plus marinating · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 700 g pork tenderloin or boneless pork shoulder,
  • cut into strips
  • 3 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 garlic clove,
  • crushed
  • 1 tsp five-spice powder
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Mix the hoisin, soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, garlic, five-spice, ginger and oil.
  2. Add the pork and coat thoroughly.
  3. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes.
  4. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  5. Arrange the pork on a lined roasting tray, reserving the marinade separately.
  6. Roast for 20–30 minutes depending on the thickness of the strips, turning and brushing with reserved marinade during cooking.
  7. Boil any leftover marinade thoroughly before using it as a glaze.
  8. Check the pork is cooked through, rest for 5 minutes and slice to serve with rice and vegetables.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Easy Chinese BBQ Pork bookmark. Never use marinade that has touched raw pork as a sauce unless it has been boiled thoroughly. Source: https://www.easypeasy-lemonsqueezy.co.uk/easy-chinese-bbq-pork
