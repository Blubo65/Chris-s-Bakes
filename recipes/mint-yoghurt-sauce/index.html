Mint Yoghurt Sauce

A cool mint yoghurt sauce for curries, kebabs, grilled meat or roasted vegetables.

Time: 5 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 150 g natural yoghurt
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped fresh mint
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • ¼ tsp ground cumin, optional,
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1–2 tsp water,
  • if needed

Method

  1. Put the yoghurt in a small bowl and stir until smooth.
  2. Mix in the chopped mint, lemon juice, cumin if using and salt.
  3. Add a teaspoon of water if you prefer a thinner sauce.
  4. Taste and adjust the lemon and seasoning.
  5. Chill until ready to serve.

Recipe notes

Simple home version inspired by the saved yoghurt mint sauce recipe. Source: https://www.seriouseats.com/recipes/2012/07/yogurt-mint-sauce-recipe.html
