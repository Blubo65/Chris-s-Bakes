Cheese & Onion Scones

These delicious air fryer cheese and onion scones are golden and crisp on the outside, soft and fluffy inside, and packed with mature cheddar and sweet, softened onion.

Ingredients

  • 225g self-raising flour
  • 50g salted butter, chilled
  • 100g mature cheddar cheese, grated
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 medium egg
  • 2–3 tbsp milk
  • ½ tsp mustard powder (optional)
  • A pinch of black pepper
  • A little extra grated cheese for the tops

Method

  1. Finely chop the onion and fry gently in a small pan with a little butter or oil for 4–5 minutes until softened. Do not brown it. Set aside to cool completely.
  2. Put the flour and optional mustard powder into a large mixing bowl. Add the chilled butter, cut into small cubes.
  3. Rub the butter into the flour using your fingertips until the mixture resembles fine breadcrumbs.
  4. Stir in the grated cheddar, cooled onion and a pinch of black pepper, keeping a little cheese aside for the tops.
  5. Beat the egg in a small bowl and add it to the mixture, reserving a little for brushing the tops if you wish.
  6. Add 2 tablespoons of milk and mix gently. Add a little more milk if needed, until you have a soft dough that is not sticky. Avoid overmixing.
  7. Lightly flour your worktop and gently press the dough out to about 2.5cm thick.
  8. Use a 6–7cm round cutter to cut out 6 scones. Press the cutter straight down without twisting to help them rise evenly. Gently reshape any leftover dough if necessary.
  9. Preheat your Air Fryer to 160°C for 3 minutes.
  10. Line the air fryer basket with a suitable piece of perforated baking parchment. Arrange the scones with space between them. Brush the tops with a little reserved beaten egg or milk and sprinkle over the remaining cheese.
  11. 160°C for 12–16 minutes
  12. Air fry until well risen and golden brown. Check after 12 minutes, as cooking times can vary depending on the size of your scones and which drawer you use.
  13. If the tops brown too quickly, loosely cover with foil, secured so it cannot blow around. Check that the centres are cooked before removing.
  14. Leave the scones to rest for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack. Serve warm with butter, or allow to cool completely before storing.
