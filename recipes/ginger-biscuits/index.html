Ginger Biscuits

Homemade ginger biscuits with warming ginger and a little golden syrup.

Ingredients

  • 125g plain flour
  • 1½ tsp ground ginger
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon (optional)
  • ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 75g sugar
  • 60g butter
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Mix the flour, ginger, optional cinnamon, bicarbonate of soda, sugar and salt.
  2. Melt the butter gently with the golden syrup, then stir into the dry ingredients to make a dough.
  3. Chill for 20–30 minutes.
  4. Roll into small balls and flatten to about 3–4mm thick.
  5. For the Ninja AF410, place on perforated parchment on the crisper plate with space between biscuits. Air fry at 150–155°C for 10–12 minutes, checking early. Do not flip; biscuits firm as they cool.

Recipe notes

For the Ninja AF410, use perforated parchment and leave room for spreading. If your model only allows 150°C or 160°C, use 150°C and check carefully.
