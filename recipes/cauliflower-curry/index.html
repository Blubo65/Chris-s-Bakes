Cauliflower Curry

A deliciously spiced cauliflower curry, A healthy, flavour-packed vegetarian dish that makes a perfect side or light meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon ginger purée
  • 1 large cauliflower
  • ½ teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1 fresh green chilli, thinly sliced (optional), or ¼ teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1 cup firmly packed chopped coriander (cilantro)
  • 1¼ teaspoons ground cumin
  • 2½ teaspoons ground coriander
  • 1½ teaspoons garam masala
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Salt or salt substitute (optional)

Method

  1. Cut the cauliflower into tiny florets.
  2. In a large skillet over medium heat, stir-fry the ginger purée and turmeric for 2 minutes, adding a few drops of water if the mixture begins to stick.
  3. Add the chilli and coriander leaves. Stir-fry for another 2 minutes.
  4. Add the cauliflower and cook for 5 minutes, adding a teaspoon of water if the mixture starts to dry.
  5. Add the remaining ingredients and 4 tablespoons of water.
  6. Stir for 3–4 minutes, then reduce the heat, cover and cook for approximately 40 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the cauliflower is fork-tender.
