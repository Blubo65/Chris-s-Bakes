Beef Nasi Goreng

Indonesian-inspired fried rice with beef mince, vegetables, soy and a little chilli.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g lean beef mince
  • 600 g cold cooked rice
  • 1 onion,
  • finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 100 g frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sweet chilli sauce or kecap manis
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 eggs (optional,
  • to serve)
  • 2 spring onions,
  • sliced
  • Lime wedges,
  • to serve (optional)

Method

  1. Use rice that has been cooked and cooled promptly; cold leftover rice gives the best fried-rice texture.
  2. Heat the oil in a large wok or frying pan.
  3. Add the onion and pepper and cook for 3–4 minutes.
  4. Add the beef mince and cook, breaking it up, until browned and thoroughly cooked.
  5. Stir in the garlic and curry powder for 30 seconds.
  6. Add the peas and cold rice, breaking up any clumps.
  7. Stir-fry over high heat for 3–5 minutes until piping hot throughout.
  8. Add the soy sauce and sweet chilli sauce or kecap manis and toss everything together.
  9. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
  10. If desired, fry the eggs in a separate pan and serve on top.
  11. Finish with spring onions and lime wedges.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Beef Nasi Goreng recipe link; not a verbatim reproduction. Cool and refrigerate cooked rice promptly, and reheat it only once until piping hot. Source: https://www.slimmingeats.com/blog/nasi-goreng
