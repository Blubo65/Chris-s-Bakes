Creamy Bacon and Mushroom Pasta

A quick, creamy fusilli pasta with crispy bacon, mushrooms, peas and garlic-and-herb soft cheese.

Ingredients

  • 300 g fusilli pasta
  • 1 tsp oil
  • 4 rashers back bacon, cut into strips
  • 75 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 100 g peas, defrosted
  • 120 g garlic-and-herb soft cheese
  • 5 tbsp milk

Method

  1. Cook the pasta according to the packet instructions.
  2. While the pasta cooks, heat the oil in a saucepan and fry the bacon until crisp. Remove the bacon to a plate, leaving any remaining cooking oil in the pan. Add the mushrooms and cook in the same pan.
  3. Reduce the heat to low and add the peas, soft cheese and milk. Stir until the soft cheese melts and forms a creamy sauce.
  4. Drain the pasta, toss through the sauce and divide between four serving bowls.
