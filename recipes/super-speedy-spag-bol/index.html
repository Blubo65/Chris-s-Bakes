Super speedy spag bol

A go-faster pasta dish that gets on the plate in just 20 minutes.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • Low-calorie cooking spray
  • 500g lean beef or pork mince (5% fat or less)
  • 4 lean back bacon rashers,
  • visible fat removed,
  • chopped
  • 1 beef stock cube,
  • crumbled
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 200g passata
  • 1 onion,
  • diced
  • 2 celery sticks,
  • diced
  • 2 large carrots,
  • diced
  • 400g dried spaghetti
  • 250g cherry tomatoes,
  • halved
  • Fresh basil leaves,
  • to serve

Method

  1. Spray a large non-stick frying pan with low-calorie cooking spray and put it over a high heat.
  2. Add the mince, bacon, crumbled stock cube and oregano.
  3. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring often and breaking up any lumps.
  4. Stir in the passata and cook for another 5 minutes.
  5. At the same time, bring a saucepan of water to the boil over a high heat.
  6. Tip in the onion, celery, carrots and spaghetti.
  7. Cook for 10 minutes, then drain and combine with the beef.
  8. Stir in the tomatoes, season to taste and scatter with the basil leaves to serve.
