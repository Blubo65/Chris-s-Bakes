The Texas Squealer Burger

Juicy smoky beef and bacon burgers seasoned with Worcestershire sauce, garlic and onion, perfect for the barbecue.

Ingredients

  • 680 g beef mince, about 80% lean (1½ lb)
  • 12 rashers streaky bacon, uncooked and very finely chopped
  • 1–1½ tbsp smoky grill seasoning (such as mesquite seasoning)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp dried minced onion
  • 60 ml Worcestershire sauce (¼ cup)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Burger buns, cheese and favourite toppings, to serve (optional)

Method

  1. Finely chop the uncooked bacon with a sharp knife into very small pieces. Avoid using a food processor, as it can make the bacon sticky.
  2. Put the beef mince into a large bowl with the bacon, grill seasoning, garlic powder, dried onion and Worcestershire sauce. Season with salt and black pepper, taking account of the salt in the bacon and seasoning. Mix gently until just combined.
  3. Shape into 6–8 even burger patties. For firmer patties, place in the freezer for about 30 minutes before grilling
  4. this is optional.
  5. Cook on a preheated barbecue or grill for approximately 5 minutes per side, adjusting for thickness, until fully cooked. Because these burgers contain minced beef and raw bacon, check the centre reaches at least 75°C.
  6. Rest for 5 minutes before serving in buns with cheese or your favourite toppings.
