No-Knead Bread

An easy no-knead crusty bread loaf made with a wet dough and baked in the air fryer, with an optional overnight rest for better flavour.

Ingredients

  • 450 g strong bread flour (3 cups)
  • 7 g instant dried yeast (1 sachet / 2 tsp)
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, optional
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 375 ml warm water (1½ cups)
  • Extra flour, for dusting

Method

  1. Put the flour, yeast, salt, optional pepper, sugar, olive oil and warm water into a large bowl. Mix thoroughly until combined into a wet dough
  2. no kneading is required.
  3. Cover the bowl and leave the dough to rest for at least 2 hours. For best results, refrigerate overnight or up to 3 days
  4. the original creator rested hers for 2 days.
  5. Preheat the air fryer for 10 minutes. For a crisper crust, place a small heatproof bowl of water in the air fryer if the appliance instructions allow it, and remove it before cooking if necessary.
  6. Heavily flour the work surface. Scrape the wet dough onto it, dust with flour and gently shape using a scraper without kneading.
  7. Transfer the dough onto baking parchment, then lift it into a 6-inch round cake tin suitable for your air fryer.
  8. Air fry at 190°C (375°F) for approximately 30 minutes.
  9. Remove the tin and parchment, turn the loaf over and cook for another 15 minutes.
  10. Turn the loaf over again and cover loosely with foil, secured safely so it cannot touch the heating element. Cook for a final 15 minutes, or until the loaf is cooked through. The original creator gives a centre temperature range of 88–99°C (190–210°F).
  11. Leave to cool completely before slicing. Best fresh on the first day
  12. also makes excellent toast the next day.
