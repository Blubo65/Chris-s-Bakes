Garlic & Parsley Chicken

Golden chicken pieces tossed in garlic butter and fresh parsley for a quick, flavourful dinner.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 30 g butter
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan.
  3. Cook the chicken in batches for 5–7 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  4. Lower the heat and add the butter and garlic.
  5. Stir for 30–45 seconds until fragrant, taking care not to burn the garlic.
  6. Add the lemon juice and parsley and toss the chicken in the garlic butter.
  7. Check the thickest pieces reach 75°C internally and serve immediately with potatoes or vegetables.

Recipe notes
