Baileys-Style Cheesecake

A creamy no-bake Irish cream cheesecake on a buttery biscuit base.

Time: 30 mins plus chilling · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 200 g digestive biscuits,
  • crushed
  • 75 g butter,
  • melted
  • 300 g full-fat cream cheese,
  • softened
  • 200 ml double cream
  • 75 g icing sugar
  • 3 tbsp Irish cream liqueur
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract, optional,
  • Cocoa powder or chocolate shavings,
  • to decorate

Method

  1. Mix the crushed biscuits with melted butter and press firmly into the base of a 20 cm springform tin.
  2. Chill for 20 minutes.
  3. Beat the cream cheese with the icing sugar until smooth.
  4. Mix in the Irish cream liqueur and vanilla if using.
  5. Whip the double cream in a separate bowl until it forms soft peaks, then fold gently into the cream cheese mixture.
  6. Spread the filling over the biscuit base and smooth the top.
  7. Cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours, preferably overnight, until firm.
  8. Dust with cocoa powder or decorate with chocolate shavings before serving.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Baileys cheesecake bookmarks; not a verbatim reproduction. Contains alcohol and dairy.
