Crispy Cornflake Chicken

Crunchy cornflake-coated chicken pieces cooked in the air fryer with less oil than deep-frying.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • cut into strips
  • 80 g cornflakes,
  • crushed
  • 1 egg,
  • beaten
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Oil spray

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  2. Crush the cornflakes into coarse crumbs and mix with paprika, garlic granules, salt and pepper.
  3. Put the flour in one bowl and the beaten egg in another.
  4. Coat each chicken strip in flour, dip in egg, then press into the cornflake crumbs.
  5. Arrange in a single layer in the air fryer basket and lightly spray with oil.
  6. Cook in batches if needed.
  7. Air fry for 10–15 minutes, turning halfway, until crisp and the thickest piece reaches 75°C internally.

Recipe notes

Adapted home version inspired by the saved air fryer cornflake chicken video. Cooking time depends on strip size and air fryer model. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XUdgfe7rNvA
