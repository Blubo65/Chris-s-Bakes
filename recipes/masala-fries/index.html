Masala Fries

Crispy homemade fries tossed with a punchy Indian-inspired blend of spices and tangy seasoning.

Time: 20–25 mins · Makes 3–4

Ingredients

  • 700g potatoes,
  • cut into thin chips
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • ½ tsp salt,
  • plus more to taste
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ¼ tsp chilli powder,
  • or to taste
  • ½ tsp amchur (dried mango powder), optional,
  • Lemon juice,
  • to finish (optional)

Method

  1. Rinse the cut potatoes under cold water to remove excess starch, then dry very thoroughly with a clean tea towel.
  2. Toss the chips with the oil and salt.
  3. Air fry at 190°C for 18–25 minutes, shaking the basket every 5–7 minutes, until golden, crisp and tender inside.
  4. Cook in batches if necessary so the chips are not piled too deeply.
  5. Mix the paprika, cumin, coriander, garam masala, turmeric, chilli powder and optional amchur in a small bowl.
  6. Toss the hot chips with the spice mix, taste and adjust the salt.
  7. Add a squeeze of lemon if liked and serve straight away.

Recipe notes
