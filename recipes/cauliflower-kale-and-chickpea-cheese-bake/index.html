Cauliflower, Kale & Chickpea Cheese Bake

A comforting vegetable bake with cauliflower, kale and chickpeas in a creamy cheese sauce.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower,
  • cut into florets
  • 150 g kale,
  • tough stems removed
  • 1 x 400 g tin chickpeas,
  • drained
  • 30 g butter
  • 30 g plain flour
  • 500 ml milk
  • 120 g mature cheddar,
  • grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard, optional,
  • Salt and black pepper
  • ½ tsp paprika

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Steam or boil the cauliflower for 5–6 minutes until just tender.
  3. Add the kale for the final 2 minutes, then drain well.
  4. Melt the butter in a saucepan.
  5. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute, then gradually whisk in the milk until smooth.
  6. Simmer for 4–6 minutes until thickened.
  7. Remove from the heat and stir in most of the cheese, mustard if using, salt and pepper.
  8. Combine the cauliflower, kale and chickpeas in an ovenproof dish.
  9. Pour over the cheese sauce and sprinkle with remaining cheese and paprika.
  10. Bake for 20–25 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  11. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.
