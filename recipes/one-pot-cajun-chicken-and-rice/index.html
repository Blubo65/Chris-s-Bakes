One-Pot Cajun Chicken & Rice

A one-pot Cajun-style rice dish with chicken, peppers and smoky spices.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast,
  • diced
  • 250 g long-grain rice,
  • rinsed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 400 ml chicken stock,
  • hot
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large lidded pan.
  2. Brown the chicken for 4–5 minutes, then remove to a plate.
  3. Add the onion and pepper and cook for 4 minutes.
  4. Stir in the garlic and Cajun seasoning for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the rinsed rice, chopped tomatoes and hot stock.
  6. Stir well, then return the chicken to the pan.
  7. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover and cook on low for 18–22 minutes until the rice is tender and chicken fully cooked.
  8. Add a splash of hot water if needed.
  9. Turn off the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes.
  10. Fluff with a fork, season to taste and serve.

Recipe notes
