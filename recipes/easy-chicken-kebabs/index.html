Easy Chicken Kebabs

Tender chicken pieces marinated with yoghurt, lemon, garlic and spices, cooked on skewers.

Time: 30 mins plus marinating · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast or thighs,
  • cut into chunks
  • 150 g natural yoghurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 red pepper and 1 red onion,
  • cut into chunks (optional)

Method

  1. Mix the yoghurt, lemon juice, garlic, paprika, cumin, oregano, olive oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Add the chicken and stir to coat.
  3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
  4. Thread the chicken onto skewers, alternating with pepper and onion if using.
  5. If using wooden skewers, soak them first.
  6. Grill or roast at 220°C (fan 200°C) for 15–20 minutes, turning halfway, until lightly charred and the chicken reaches 75°C internally.
  7. Rest for 3 minutes and serve with flatbreads, salad and yoghurt sauce.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved chicken kebab recipe. Ensure chicken reaches 75°C internally. Source: https://www.tamingtwins.com/chicken-kebab-recipe
