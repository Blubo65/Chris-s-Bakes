Easy Hamburger Potato Casserole

A comforting family casserole of savoury beef mince, thinly sliced potatoes, creamy mushroom sauce and melted Cheddar cheese.

Ingredients

  • 450 g lean beef mince (1 lb)
  • 1 can (about 300 g / 10.5 oz) condensed cream of mushroom soup
  • 180 ml milk (¾ cup)
  • 75 g chopped onion (½ cup)
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • About 450 g peeled potatoes, thinly sliced (3 cups)
  • 115 g grated Cheddar cheese (1 cup)

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F).
  2. In a large frying pan over medium-high heat, brown the beef mince until cooked through and crumbly, about 5–7 minutes. Drain off excess fat.
  3. In a bowl, mix the condensed cream of mushroom soup, milk, chopped onion, salt and black pepper.
  4. In a 2-quart (about 1.9-litre) baking dish, make alternating layers of thinly sliced potatoes, mushroom soup mixture and browned beef.
  5. Bake for 1–1½ hours, until the potatoes are tender throughout.
  6. Sprinkle the grated Cheddar over the top and return to the oven until melted and bubbling. Serve hot.
