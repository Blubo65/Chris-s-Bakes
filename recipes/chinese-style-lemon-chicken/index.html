Chinese-Style Lemon Chicken

Tender chicken pieces coated in a bright, tangy lemon sauce for a homemade Chinese-inspired favourite.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless skinless chicken breast,
  • cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 egg,
  • beaten
  • 2 tbsp cornflour,
  • plus 1 tsp to thicken sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 150 ml chicken stock
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey or sugar
  • 1 garlic clove,
  • crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • Sliced spring onions,
  • to garnish (optional)

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with the beaten egg and 2 tbsp cornflour until lightly coated.
  2. Heat the oil in a large non-stick frying pan.
  3. Cook the chicken in batches for 5–7 minutes, turning until golden and cooked through; set aside.
  4. In the same pan, add the stock, lemon juice and zest, soy sauce, honey, garlic and ginger.
  5. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  6. Mix the remaining 1 tsp cornflour with 1 tbsp cold water, then stir it into the sauce.
  7. Simmer for 1–2 minutes until glossy and lightly thickened.
  8. Return the cooked chicken to the pan and toss to coat.
  9. Heat until piping hot and serve with rice or vegetables, garnished with spring onions if desired.
