Melting Potatoes

Thick, golden potato rounds roasted in butter and rosemary, then finished in garlicky stock until meltingly tender.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp (about 28 g) unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 900 g Yukon Gold or floury potatoes (about 4–5 medium)
  • 2–3 sprigs fresh rosemary, coarsely chopped (about 1 tbsp)
  • 240 ml low-sodium vegetable stock (or chicken stock if not vegetarian)
  • 4 garlic cloves, peeled and smashed

Method

  1. Position a rack in the middle of the oven and preheat to 230°C (450°F). Mix the melted butter, olive oil, salt and black pepper in a small bowl.
  2. Peel the potatoes if desired. Trim the rounded ends slightly, then slice crosswise into 2.5 cm-thick rounds.
  3. Add the potato rounds and chopped rosemary to the butter mixture and toss to coat.
  4. Arrange the potatoes cut-side up in a single layer on a rimmed metal baking tray (approximately 23 x 33 cm). Avoid glass, which can shatter at this temperature.
  5. Drizzle any remaining butter mixture over the potatoes.
  6. Roast for about 15 minutes, until the bottoms begin turning golden and the rounds release easily. Flip and roast for about 15 minutes more, until both sides are deeply golden. Remove the tray from the oven.
  7. Carefully pour the stock into the tray and add the smashed garlic cloves.
  8. Return to the oven and roast for about 15 minutes, until the potatoes are very tender and most of the stock is absorbed.
