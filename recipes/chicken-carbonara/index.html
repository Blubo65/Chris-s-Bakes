Chicken carbonara

A lighter carbonara made without cheese, with shredded cooked chicken and smoky bacon.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500g dried spaghetti
  • Low-calorie cooking spray
  • 8 lean back bacon rashers or medallions,
  • visible fat removed,
  • cut into strips
  • 2 cooked skinless and boneless chicken breasts,
  • torn into chunky strips
  • 6 large fresh sage leaves,
  • finely chopped
  • 4 large eggs
  • Grated zest of 1 small unwaxed lemon
  • 3 tbsp chopped fresh parsley

Method

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the pack instructions, then drain well and tip back into the pan.
  2. At the same time, spray a large non-stick frying pan with low-calorie cooking spray and put it over a medium-high heat.
  3. Add the bacon and cook for 3–5 minutes or until lightly golden.
  4. Stir in the chicken and sage, reduce the heat to medium-low and cook for about 5 minutes or until the chicken has heated through.
  5. Beat the eggs in a bowl with half of the lemon zest and a little salt and pepper.
  6. Off the heat, add the bacon, chicken, eggs and most of the parsley to the spaghetti and toss well.
  7. The heat of the pasta should cook the eggs into a thick, creamy sauce; if necessary return the pan to a low heat and stir constantly for 1–2 minutes.
  8. Scatter over the remaining lemon zest and parsley and serve with salad.
