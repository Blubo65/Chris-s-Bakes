Slow-Braised Beef Stew

Tender beef simmered slowly with carrots, potatoes and herbs in a rich savoury gravy.

Time: 2 hrs 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 700 g stewing beef,
  • cubed
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots,
  • thickly sliced
  • 2 celery sticks,
  • sliced (optional)
  • 500 g potatoes,
  • cut into chunks
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 600 ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 bay leaves (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 160°C (fan 140°C).
  2. Pat the beef dry and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat the oil in a flameproof casserole and brown the beef in batches.
  4. Set aside.
  5. Add the onion, carrots and celery and cook for 4–5 minutes.
  6. Stir in the flour and tomato purée and cook for 1 minute.
  7. Gradually stir in the stock and Worcestershire sauce, scraping up any browned bits.
  8. Return the beef to the pot and add thyme and bay leaves if using.
  9. Cover and cook in the oven for 1 hour 30 minutes.
  10. Add the potatoes and cook for a further 45–60 minutes until the beef is tender and the potatoes are cooked.
  11. Remove bay leaves, adjust seasoning and serve.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved beef stew recipe. Cooking time depends on the cut; simmer longer if the beef is not yet tender. Source: https://tatyanaseverydayfood.com/beef-stew/
