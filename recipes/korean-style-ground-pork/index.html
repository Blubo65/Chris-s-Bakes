Korean-Style Ground Pork

Quick savoury-sweet minced pork with garlic, ginger and a soy-based Korean-inspired sauce, served over rice.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g lean pork mince
  • 1 tsp vegetable oil,
  • if needed
  • 3 garlic cloves,
  • finely chopped
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 4 tbsp reduced-salt soy sauce
  • 1½ tbsp brown sugar or honey
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • ½ tsp chilli flakes,
  • or to taste
  • 4 spring onions,
  • sliced
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds (optional)
  • Cooked rice,
  • to serve

Method

  1. Mix the soy sauce, brown sugar or honey, rice vinegar, sesame oil and chilli flakes in a small bowl.
  2. Heat a large frying pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Add the pork mince and cook for 6–8 minutes, breaking it up with a spoon, until browned and cooked through.
  4. Drain excess fat if necessary.
  5. Add the garlic and ginger and stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  6. Pour in the sauce and simmer for 2–4 minutes, stirring, until the pork is glossy and the sauce has reduced slightly.
  7. Stir through most of the spring onions.
  8. Sprinkle with the remaining spring onions and sesame seeds, if using.
  9. Serve hot over cooked rice.
