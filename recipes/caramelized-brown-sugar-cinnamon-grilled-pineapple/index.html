Caramelized Brown Sugar Cinnamon Grilled Pineapple

Juicy grilled pineapple spears coated in a buttery brown sugar and cinnamon glaze, caramelised until golden.

Ingredients

  • 1 pineapple, peeled and cut into spears
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup butter, melted
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon

Method

  1. Lay the pineapple spears on a tray and sprinkle lightly with cinnamon.
  2. Whisk together the melted butter, brown sugar and remaining cinnamon. If the glaze is too thick, warm it briefly so it can be poured or brushed over the pineapple.
  3. Brush or spread the glaze over the pineapple spears.
  4. Grill for about 7–10 minutes, turning as needed, until the pineapple starts to turn golden brown and caramelise. Brush with any remaining glaze before serving.
