Crispy Chilli Beef

Crispy strips of beef tossed in a sweet, sticky chilli-soy sauce with peppers and spring onions.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g lean beef steak,
  • thinly sliced across the grain
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 red pepper,
  • thinly sliced
  • 2 spring onions,
  • sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 3 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce,
  • extra for sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp honey or sugar
  • 1–2 tbsp water,
  • as needed

Method

  1. Toss the beef strips with 1 tbsp soy sauce and cornflour until lightly coated.
  2. Spray or toss with the oil.
  3. Arrange in a single layer in the air fryer basket and cook at 200°C for 8–12 minutes, shaking halfway, until browned and crisp.
  4. Cook in batches if needed.
  5. Meanwhile, mix the sweet chilli sauce, extra soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey or sugar and water in a small bowl.
  6. Stir-fry the pepper in a hot pan for 2–3 minutes.
  7. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 30 seconds.
  8. Pour in the sauce and simmer briefly.
  9. Add the crispy beef and toss quickly to coat.
  10. Garnish with spring onions and serve immediately with rice.

Recipe notes
