Korean Cauliflower with Sticky Gochujang Sauce

Crispy air-fried cauliflower tossed in a sticky, sweet-spicy Korean gochujang sauce, served with rice and fresh spring onions.

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower, cut into small florets
  • 180g plain flour
  • 1 tbsp cornflour/cornstarch, for the batter
  • ½ tbsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 210ml extra-cold sparkling water
  • Spray oil
  • For the chilli sauce:
  • 60ml maple syrup
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 large garlic clove, peeled and grated
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • ½ tbsp sesame oil
  • 2½ tbsp gochujang paste
  • 2 tbsp mirin
  • 2 tbsp cornflour/cornstarch, for thickening
  • To serve: rice, toasted sesame oil, chopped spring onions, toasted white sesame seeds and a squeeze of lime

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 200°C. Line the basket with air-fryer-safe parchment paper, following your appliance manufacturer's instructions.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the plain flour, 1 tbsp cornflour, baking powder, salt, garlic powder and black pepper. Stir in the extra-cold sparkling water until just combined
  3. the batter should be like thick pancake batter.
  4. Coat the cauliflower florets evenly in the batter and arrange in a single layer in the air fryer basket without overcrowding. You will likely need to cook in batches. Spray lightly with oil and air fry at 200°C for 12–17 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crisp.
  5. Meanwhile, blend the maple syrup, soy sauce, garlic, ginger, apple cider vinegar, sesame oil, gochujang and mirin until smooth. Pour about 3 tbsp of the sauce into a small bowl and mix with the 2 tbsp cornflour until smooth. Pour the remaining sauce into a small saucepan and heat over medium-low. Stir in the cornflour mixture and simmer gently for 3–5 minutes until thickened, then remove from the heat.
  6. Toss the crispy cauliflower in the sticky sauce. Serve over rice with chopped spring onions, toasted sesame seeds and a squeeze of lime, if desired.
