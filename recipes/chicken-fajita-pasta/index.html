Chicken Fajita Pasta

Creamy tomato pasta with chicken, peppers and fajita seasoning for a quick family meal.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 400 g chicken breast,
  • sliced
  • 300 g dried pasta
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 peppers,
  • sliced
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 500 ml chicken stock,
  • hot
  • 100 g grated cheddar
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large deep pan.
  2. Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  3. Add the onion and peppers and cook for 4–5 minutes.
  4. Stir in the fajita seasoning.
  5. Add the pasta, chopped tomatoes and hot stock.
  6. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered for 12–16 minutes, stirring frequently, until the pasta is tender.
  7. Add a splash of water if needed.
  8. Stir in the grated cheese until melted and creamy.
  9. Check the chicken is cooked through and the dish is piping hot.
  10. Season to taste and serve.
