Coconut Chickpea Curry

Chickpeas simmered in a creamy coconut tomato sauce with ginger and warming spices.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 x 400 g tins chickpeas,
  • drained and rinsed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 400 ml light coconut milk
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 150 ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Lime juice,
  • to taste

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a saucepan.
  2. Cook the onion for 5 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the garlic, ginger and curry powder and stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the chickpeas, coconut milk, chopped tomatoes and stock.
  5. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  6. Cook for 18–22 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens.
  7. Mash a few chickpeas to thicken further if desired.
  8. Season with salt, pepper and a squeeze of lime juice.
  9. Serve with rice or flatbreads.
