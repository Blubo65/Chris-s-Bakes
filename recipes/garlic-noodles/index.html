Garlic Noodles

Fast savoury noodles tossed with plenty of garlic, butter, soy and a little Parmesan.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 3

Ingredients

  • 250 g egg noodles or spaghetti
  • 5 garlic cloves,
  • finely chopped
  • 30 g butter
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce, optional,
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 30 g finely grated Parmesan
  • 2 spring onions,
  • sliced
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Cook the noodles according to the packet, drain and reserve a little cooking water.
  2. Heat the butter and oil in a large frying pan over medium-low heat.
  3. Add the garlic and cook gently for 30–60 seconds without browning.
  4. Stir in the soy sauce, oyster sauce if using and sugar.
  5. Add the noodles and toss thoroughly, adding a splash of noodle water if needed to coat them evenly.
  6. Turn off the heat and toss with the Parmesan and spring onions.
  7. Season with black pepper and serve immediately.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Garlic Noodles video. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=It4jb7KMoR0
