Slow-Roasted Garlic Lamb

Tender slow-roasted leg of lamb with whole roasted garlic and a fragrant coriander and cumin paste.

Time: 4 hrs 40 mins · Makes 6

Ingredients

  • 1 leg of lamb,
  • about 2 kg
  • 8 whole garlic bulbs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil,
  • plus 1–2 tbsp for the garlic paste
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 1½ tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp ground cumin

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 150°C (fan 140°C).
  2. Cut the tops off the garlic bulbs and arrange them in a roasting tin.
  3. Pat the lamb dry.
  4. Rub with 2 tbsp olive oil and season generously with salt and pepper.
  5. Sit it on top of the garlic bulbs and cover the tin tightly with foil.
  6. Roast for about 4 hours, until the lamb is very tender.
  7. Carefully remove the garlic bulbs and squeeze the softened cloves into a bowl.
  8. Mash the roasted garlic with the ground coriander, cumin, 1–2 tbsp olive oil and a little salt and pepper.
  9. Remove the foil, spread the garlic paste over the lamb and return it to the oven at 200°C (fan 180°C) for about 30 minutes to brown.
  10. Rest for 10–15 minutes before shredding or carving.
  11. Check the lamb is cooked to your preference and serve with the roasting juices.

Recipe notes

Adapted from the saved Raindrop recipe. Slow-roasting times vary with the shape of the joint and oven; use a suitable meat thermometer if available. Source: https://www.instagram.com/p/DWUbdlHAIs0/?igsh=Z3d5cG1lbWRzcjB4
