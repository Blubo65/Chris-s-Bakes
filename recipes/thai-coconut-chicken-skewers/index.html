Thai Coconut Chicken Skewers

Chicken skewers marinated in coconut milk, lime, garlic and Thai-inspired spices.

Time: 30 mins plus marinating · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken thighs,
  • cut into chunks
  • 150 ml coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 garlic clove,
  • crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp curry powder or Thai red curry paste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • Skewers

Method

  1. Mix the coconut milk, lime juice, garlic, ginger, curry powder or paste, soy sauce and sugar.
  2. Add the chicken and coat thoroughly.
  3. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes.
  4. Thread the chicken onto skewers.
  5. If using wooden skewers, soak them first.
  6. Grill or barbecue over medium-high heat for 10–15 minutes, turning regularly, until lightly charred and the thickest piece reaches 75°C internally.
  7. Rest for 2 minutes and serve with rice, salad or lime wedges.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Grilled Thai Coconut Chicken Skewers bookmark. Ensure chicken reaches 75°C internally. Source: https://pupswithchopsticks.com/grilled-chicken-skewers/
