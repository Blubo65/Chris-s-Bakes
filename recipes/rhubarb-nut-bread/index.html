Rhubarb Nut Bread

A lightly sweet quick bread with tart rhubarb and chopped nuts.

Time: 1 hr 10 mins · Makes 10

Ingredients

  • 250 g plain flour
  • 150 g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 medium egg
  • 120 ml milk
  • 60 ml vegetable oil
  • 200 g rhubarb,
  • finely chopped
  • 75 g chopped walnuts or pecans
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Grease and line a 2 lb loaf tin.
  3. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, bicarbonate of soda and salt.
  4. Whisk the egg, milk, oil and vanilla in another bowl.
  5. Add the wet ingredients to the dry and stir just until combined.
  6. Fold in the rhubarb and nuts.
  7. Spoon into the tin and bake for 45–55 minutes until a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean.
  8. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then transfer to a rack.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Rhubarb Nut Bread bookmark. Source: https://whatscookingamerica.net/Bread/Rhubarb-Bread.htm
