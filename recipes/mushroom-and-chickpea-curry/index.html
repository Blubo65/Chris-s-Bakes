Mushroom & Chickpea Curry

A hearty vegetarian curry combining mushrooms and chickpeas in a fragrant tomato and spice sauce.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 x 400 g tin chickpeas,
  • drained and rinsed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 150 ml vegetable stock or water
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh coriander,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan.
  2. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes until soft.
  3. Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder and cumin and stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the mushrooms and cook for 4–5 minutes until they begin to soften.
  5. Stir in the chickpeas, chopped tomatoes and stock.
  6. Bring to a simmer and cook for 15–20 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  7. Season to taste and garnish with coriander if desired.
  8. Serve with rice or flatbreads.
