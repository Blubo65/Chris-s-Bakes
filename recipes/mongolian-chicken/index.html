Mongolian Chicken

Crisp-edged chicken tossed in a glossy sweet soy, garlic and ginger sauce.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar or honey
  • 150 ml chicken stock or water
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp cold water
  • 2 spring onions,
  • sliced

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with 2 tbsp cornflour until lightly coated.
  2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan or wok.
  3. Cook the chicken in batches until golden and cooked through; set aside.
  4. Add the garlic and ginger to the pan and stir for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the soy sauce, sugar or honey, stock and rice vinegar.
  6. Simmer for 2 minutes.
  7. Stir in the cornflour slurry and cook until glossy.
  8. Return the chicken to the pan and toss to coat and heat through.
  9. Garnish with spring onions and serve with rice or noodles.

Recipe notes
