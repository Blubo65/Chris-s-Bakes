Hawaiian Style Couscous

Fluffy couscous tossed with juicy pineapple, diced ham, sweetcorn and colourful peppers for a quick sweet-and-savoury side.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (about 170 g dry) couscous
  • 1 cup (240 ml) chicken stock
  • ½ red pepper, finely chopped
  • ½ green pepper, finely chopped
  • 1 small red onion, finely chopped
  • 1 cup (about 150 g) cooked ham, cubed
  • 1 cup (about 165 g) fresh pineapple, cubed
  • ½ cup (about 80 g) canned sweetcorn, drained
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Low-calorie cooking spray

Method

  1. Spray a frying pan with low-calorie cooking spray and heat over medium-high heat. Add the red onion and both peppers and sauté for about 6 minutes, until softened. Remove from the heat and set aside.
  2. Bring the chicken stock to the boil in a small saucepan. Stir in the couscous, turn off the heat, cover and leave for about 5 minutes until the liquid has been absorbed. Fluff with a fork.
  3. Transfer the couscous to a large bowl. Add the softened onion and peppers, diced ham, pineapple and sweetcorn. Season with salt and black pepper and mix well.
  4. Serve warm or chilled.
