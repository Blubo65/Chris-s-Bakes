Chicken Singapore Noodles

Curry-seasoned stir-fried noodles with chicken, vegetables and a light savoury sauce.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 250 g dried rice vermicelli noodles
  • 300 g chicken breast,
  • thinly sliced
  • 1 red pepper,
  • thinly sliced
  • 1 carrot,
  • cut into matchsticks
  • 100 g peas or beansprouts
  • 2 eggs,
  • beaten (optional)
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 spring onions,
  • sliced

Method

  1. Prepare the noodles according to packet instructions, drain and set aside.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok.
  3. If using eggs, scramble them briefly and set aside.
  4. Add the remaining oil and stir-fry the chicken until cooked through.
  5. Add the pepper and carrot and cook for 3–4 minutes.
  6. Stir in the garlic and curry powder, then add the noodles, peas or beansprouts and soy sauce.
  7. Toss over high heat until piping hot.
  8. Return the egg to the pan if using, scatter with spring onions and serve.
