Crispy Baked Honey Garlic Chicken

Crispy oven-baked panko chicken bites coated in a sticky honey-garlic sauce with a little sriracha heat.

Ingredients

  • For the chicken:
  • ½ lb (225 g) boneless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 extra-large egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp white sesame seeds, optional garnish
  • 1 tbsp chopped spring onions, optional garnish
  • For the sauce:
  • 6 tbsp honey
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • ¼ cup (60 ml) water
  • ½ tbsp cornflour
  • 1½ tbsp reduced-salt soy sauce
  • 1½ tbsp sriracha sauce
  • 2 tbsp water

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan
  2. 400°F). Line a baking tray with baking parchment. Dip the chicken pieces into beaten egg, then coat thoroughly in panko breadcrumbs. Arrange on the tray and bake for 15–20 minutes, until crisp, deep golden and cooked through (75°C in the centre).
  3. Meanwhile, add the honey, garlic, onion powder, soy sauce, sriracha and 2 tbsp water to a small saucepan. Bring to a gentle boil. Mix the cornflour with the remaining ¼ cup (60 ml) water until smooth, then stir into the sauce. Simmer, stirring, until thick and glossy.
  4. Pour the sauce over the chicken just before serving to keep the coating crisp. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and chopped spring onions, if using.
