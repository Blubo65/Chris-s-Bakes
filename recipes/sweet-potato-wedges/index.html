Sweet Potato Wedges

Sweet potato wedges with crisp edges, seasoned with paprika and garlic.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes,
  • scrubbed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • ½ tsp cornflour,
  • optional for extra crispness
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  2. Cut the sweet potatoes lengthways into evenly sized wedges.
  3. Pat the wedges dry, then toss with olive oil, paprika, garlic granules, salt and pepper.
  4. Add cornflour if using and toss again.
  5. Arrange in a single layer in the basket; cook in batches if necessary.
  6. Air fry for 15–20 minutes, shaking or turning halfway, until browned outside and tender inside.
  7. Season to taste and serve hot.

Recipe notes

Home adaptation based on the saved air fryer sweet potato wedges bookmark. Source: https://allguiderecipes.info/air-fryer-sweet-potato-wedges/
