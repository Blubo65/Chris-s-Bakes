Potato Wedges

Crispy seasoned potato wedges with fluffy centres, an easy air fryer side.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 800 g potatoes,
  • scrubbed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • ½ tsp dried mixed herbs
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 200°C.
  2. Cut the potatoes into evenly sized wedges.
  3. Pat dry and toss with olive oil, paprika, garlic granules, herbs, salt and pepper.
  4. Arrange in a single layer in the basket, leaving space around each wedge.
  5. Cook in batches if necessary.
  6. Air fry for 18–24 minutes, shaking the basket every 6–7 minutes, until golden and tender.
  7. Season to taste and serve hot.
