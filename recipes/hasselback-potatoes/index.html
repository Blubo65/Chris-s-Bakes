Hasselback Potatoes

Potatoes sliced into thin fans, brushed with garlic butter and air fried until crisp at the edges.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil or melted butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried thyme or rosemary
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Grated Parmesan,
  • optional

Method

  1. Wash and dry the potatoes.
  2. Make thin slices across each potato, stopping before cutting all the way through so the base holds together.
  3. Mix the oil or melted butter with garlic, herbs, salt and pepper.
  4. Brush over the potatoes, working some mixture between the slices.
  5. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  6. Place the potatoes in the basket with space around them.
  7. Air fry for 25–35 minutes depending on size, brushing with more seasoned oil halfway, until the centres are tender and the edges crisp.
  8. Sprinkle with Parmesan if desired and serve hot.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Hasselback Potatoes bookmark. Cooking time depends on potato size and air fryer model. Source: https://www.kuchpakrahahai.in/hasselback-potatoes-in-air-fryer
