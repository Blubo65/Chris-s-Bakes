Ploughmans Scones

Savoury extra-strong cheese scones with mustard, pickled onions, sweet pickle and finely chopped apple.

Time: 15–20 mins · Makes About 10–12 scones

Ingredients

  • 450g self-raising flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 tbsp mustard powder
  • 100g cold butter,
  • cubed
  • 225g extra-strong cheese,
  • grated
  • 40g pickled onions, finely chopped
  • 160ml cold milk (you may not need all of it)
  • 1 medium egg,
  • beaten with a splash of milk (for glazing)
  • 40g sweet pickle
  • 1 small to medium apple,
  • finely chopped

Method

  1. In a large bowl, add the self-raising flour, salt, mustard powder and baking powder and mix thoroughly.
  2. Cut the cold butter into small pieces (rather than grating it, as it can start to melt).
  3. Add it to the flour mixture and rub in with your fingers or use a pastry blender until combined.
  4. Add the grated cheese, sweet pickle, finely chopped pickled onions and chopped apple.
  5. Mix to combine.
  6. Add the milk a little at a time until a dough forms.
  7. You may not need all the milk.
  8. Aim for a dough that is neither too dry nor too wet.
  9. Turn onto a lightly floured surface and flatten to about 3cm thick.
  10. Use a 6cm cutter to cut out around 11–12 scones.
  11. Line the air fryer crisper plates with parchment paper and place the scones in the basket, leaving space between them.
  12. Brush the tops with the beaten egg-and-milk mixture.
  13. Use the air fryer BAKE setting at 180°C (350°F) for 15–20 minutes.
  14. Check at 15 minutes; depending on your air fryer, you may need to flip them and bake for a further 5 minutes.
  15. Check the centre temperature is 93–98°C (200–210°F) when cooked through.

Recipe notes

Air fryer recipe. BAKE at 180°C/350°F for 15–20 minutes; check at 15 minutes and flip for a further 5 minutes if needed. Aim for a cooked centre temperature of 93–98°C (200–210°F). Line the crisper plates with parchment paper and ensure it is held down by the scones so it cannot lift into the heating element.
