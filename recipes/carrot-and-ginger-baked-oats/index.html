Carrot & Ginger Baked Oats

Warm baked oats flavoured with carrot, ginger and cinnamon for a carrot-cake-inspired breakfast.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 80 g rolled oats
  • 1 medium carrot,
  • finely grated
  • 1 medium egg
  • 120 ml milk
  • 1 tbsp honey or sweetener,
  • to taste
  • ½ tsp ground ginger
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp raisins,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C), or preheat the air fryer to 160°C for a small ovenproof dish.
  2. Mix the oats, grated carrot, ginger, cinnamon and baking powder in a bowl.
  3. Whisk the egg with the milk and honey or sweetener, then stir into the oat mixture.
  4. Add raisins if using.
  5. Spoon into a lightly greased small ovenproof dish.
  6. Bake for 20–25 minutes in the oven or about 15–20 minutes in the air fryer, until set in the centre.
  7. Allow to cool slightly before eating.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Carrot & Ginger Baked Oats video. Cooking time depends on dish size and appliance. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HVkXIUv8QPs
