Potato Flowers

Thin potato slices layered into flower shapes, seasoned and air fried until crisp at the edges.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 3 medium potatoes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil or melted butter
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Grated Parmesan,
  • optional

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  2. Wash the potatoes and slice very thinly, ideally with a mandoline; take care when handling the blade.
  3. Toss the slices with olive oil or melted butter, garlic granules, paprika, salt and pepper.
  4. Arrange overlapping slices in small flower shapes in silicone muffin cups or another air-fryer-safe holder, ensuring the holders allow air circulation.
  5. Air fry for 18–25 minutes, checking from 15 minutes, until the edges are golden and the potatoes are tender.
  6. Cooking time depends on slice thickness.
  7. Sprinkle with Parmesan if desired and serve hot.

Recipe notes

Practical home adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Potato Flower video. Thin slices cook quickly; check regularly and use suitable heat-safe holders. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xQJqQDYHMJg
