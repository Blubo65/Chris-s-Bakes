Chickpea Dhal Loaf

A savoury lentil and chickpea loaf flavoured with curry spices, served in slices with vegetables or chutney.

Time: 1 hr · Makes 6

Ingredients

  • 200 g red lentils,
  • rinsed
  • 1 x 400 g tin chickpeas,
  • drained and mashed roughly
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 carrot,
  • grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 2 medium eggs
  • 50 g breadcrumbs
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Grease and line a 2 lb loaf tin.
  3. Simmer the lentils in water for 12–15 minutes until soft, then drain thoroughly and cool slightly.
  4. Mix the lentils with the mashed chickpeas, onion, carrot, garlic, curry powder, eggs, breadcrumbs, salt and pepper.
  5. Press the mixture firmly into the loaf tin and smooth the top.
  6. Bake for 35–45 minutes until firm and cooked through.
  7. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes before turning out and slicing.
