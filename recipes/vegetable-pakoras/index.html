Vegetable Pakoras

Crispy spiced vegetable fritters made with chickpea flour and served with mint yoghurt.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 150 g gram flour
  • 1 small onion,
  • thinly sliced
  • 1 carrot,
  • grated
  • 100 g cauliflower,
  • finely chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp chilli powder, optional,
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • 120–150 ml water
  • Salt,
  • to taste
  • Oil spray

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (fan 200°C), or preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  2. Line a tray or basket with suitable parchment if needed.
  3. Mix the gram flour, cumin, coriander, turmeric, chilli powder, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
  4. Add the onion, carrot and cauliflower.
  5. Gradually add enough water to make a thick batter that clings to the vegetables.
  6. Spoon small mounds onto the tray or into the air fryer basket, leaving space between them.
  7. Spray lightly with oil.
  8. Bake for 18–25 minutes or air fry for 12–16 minutes, turning carefully halfway, until crisp and cooked through.
  9. Serve hot with mint yoghurt sauce.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved vegetable pakora bookmarks. This baked/air fryer version differs from traditional deep-fried pakoras.
