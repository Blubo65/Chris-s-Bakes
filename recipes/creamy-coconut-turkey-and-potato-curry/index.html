Creamy Coconut Turkey & Potato Curry

A mild, creamy curry of turkey mince and potatoes simmered with coconut milk and warming spices.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g turkey mince
  • 400 g potatoes,
  • peeled and diced
  • 1 onion,
  • finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • 400 ml light coconut milk
  • 200 ml chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper,
  • to taste
  • Fresh coriander,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Add the onion and cook for 4–5 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder and cumin.
  4. Stir for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  5. Add the turkey mince and cook for 5–7 minutes, breaking it up, until no longer pink.
  6. Stir in the diced potatoes, tomato purée, coconut milk and stock.
  7. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  8. Cover and cook for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the potatoes are tender and the turkey is cooked through.
  9. Remove the lid for the last few minutes if you would like a thicker sauce; add a little water if it becomes too thick.
  10. Season to taste and garnish with coriander if desired.
  11. Serve with rice or flatbreads.
