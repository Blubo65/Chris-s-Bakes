Date & Walnut Traybake Cake

A moist traybake with sweet dates and crunchy walnuts.

Time: 50 mins · Makes 12

Ingredients

  • 200 g chopped dates
  • 200 ml hot tea or water
  • 100 g butter,
  • softened
  • 150 g light brown sugar
  • 2 medium eggs
  • 200 g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp mixed spice
  • 75 g chopped walnuts

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Grease and line a 20 cm square tin.
  3. Pour the hot tea or water over the dates and leave for 10 minutes to soften, then cool slightly.
  4. Beat the butter and sugar together, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
  5. Fold in the flour and mixed spice, then stir in the dates and walnuts.
  6. Spoon into the tin and bake for 30–40 minutes until a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean.
  7. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then transfer to a rack.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Date and Walnut Traybake Cake bookmark. Source: https://poulef.com/date-and-walnut-traybake-cake/
