Honey Mustard Chicken — Grilled Version

Chicken coated in a sweet and tangy honey-mustard glaze, suitable for grilling or pan cooking.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast or boneless thighs
  • 2 tbsp Dijon or wholegrain mustard
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Whisk together the mustard, honey, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, salt and pepper.
  2. Coat the chicken in the marinade and leave for 15–30 minutes if time allows.
  3. Heat a grill or griddle pan to medium-high.
  4. Cook the chicken for 5–7 minutes per side depending on thickness, brushing with any reserved marinade only before it contacts raw chicken or after boiling it thoroughly.
  5. Check the thickest part reaches 75°C internally.
  6. Rest for 3–5 minutes before slicing.
  7. Serve with potatoes, salad or vegetables.

Recipe notes
