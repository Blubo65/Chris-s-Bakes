Creamy Chicken & Potato Bake

A creamy chicken and potato bake with garlic, herbs and melted cheese, cooked in a suitable air fryer-safe dish.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast,
  • cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 500 g potatoes,
  • diced small
  • 1 small onion,
  • chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 150 ml single cream
  • 150 ml chicken stock
  • 1 tsp dried mixed herbs
  • 100 g grated cheddar
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  2. Parboil the diced potatoes for 5–7 minutes, then drain well.
  3. Toss the chicken and potatoes with oil, onion, garlic, herbs, salt and pepper.
  4. Transfer to a suitably sized air-fryer-safe dish.
  5. Mix the cream and stock and pour over the ingredients.
  6. Cover the dish loosely with foil if your appliance permits safe use of foil, and cook for 20–25 minutes, stirring once halfway.
  7. Remove the foil, sprinkle with cheese and cook for another 8–12 minutes until bubbling, golden and the chicken reaches 75°C internally.
  8. Ensure the potatoes are tender.

Recipe notes

Practical adaptation inspired by the saved creamy air fryer chicken and potato dinner video. Use a dish that fits safely and check the chicken reaches 75°C. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=svvgInLrXX8
