'Never Fails' Mac 'n' Cheese

A comforting macaroni cheese with tomato, spinach and mascarpone, topped with mature Cheddar and crispy smoked bacon.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 240g macaroni
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 2 × 400g tins chopped tomatoes
  • 150g mascarpone
  • 250g fresh spinach or 250g frozen spinach, defrosted and drained
  • 75g medium-mature Cheddar, grated
  • 50g cooked smoked bacon strips

Method

  1. Preheat the grill to high. Heat the olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Once hot but not smoking, add the chopped onion and cook for 10 minutes until soft.
  2. Meanwhile, cook the macaroni in a pan of salted boiling water for 10 minutes, or until just tender according to the packet instructions.
  3. Add the tomato purée and chopped tomatoes to the onions and cook for 7–8 minutes until bubbling.
  4. Remove from the heat, then stir through the mascarpone and spinach.
  5. Season to taste.
  6. Drain the cooked macaroni, add it to the sauce and transfer everything to an ovenproof dish.
  7. Grate the Cheddar over the top.
  8. Cook under the hot grill for 3–4 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbling.
  9. Remove from the grill, crumble the cooked smoked bacon strips evenly over the top and serve.
