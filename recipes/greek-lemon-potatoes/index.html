Greek Lemon Potatoes

Golden roast potatoes infused with lemon, garlic, oregano and stock, tender inside with crisp edges.

Time: 1 hr · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 kg floury potatoes,
  • peeled and cut into wedges
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 250 ml vegetable or chicken stock
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Put the potato wedges in a roasting dish in a single layer.
  3. Whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, stock, salt and pepper.
  4. Pour over the potatoes and toss to coat.
  5. Roast for 45–60 minutes, turning once or twice, until the potatoes are tender and golden and most of the liquid has been absorbed.
  6. If the potatoes are tender but not browned enough, raise the heat slightly for the final few minutes.
  7. Serve hot.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Greek Lemon Potatoes recipe. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4drkNcIHmIs
