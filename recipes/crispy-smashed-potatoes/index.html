Crispy Smashed Potatoes

Baby potatoes boiled until tender, gently smashed and air fried until crisp with garlic and herbs.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 700 g baby potatoes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary or mixed herbs
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Grated Parmesan,
  • optional

Method

  1. Boil the baby potatoes in salted water for 12–15 minutes until just tender.
  2. Drain and allow to steam-dry.
  3. Gently press each potato with the bottom of a glass until cracked and flattened but still holding together.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 200°C.
  5. Brush or toss the potatoes with olive oil, garlic, herbs, salt and pepper.
  6. Arrange in a single layer and air fry for 12–18 minutes, turning carefully once, until crisp and golden.
  7. Sprinkle with Parmesan if desired and serve hot.
