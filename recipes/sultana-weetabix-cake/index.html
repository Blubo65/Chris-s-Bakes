Sultana Weetabix Cake

A simple, moist fruit cake made with Weetabix, tea and sultanas, with no creaming required.

Time: 1 hr 15 mins · Makes 10

Ingredients

  • 2 Weetabix biscuits
  • 200 ml strong tea,
  • cooled
  • 200 g sultanas
  • 150 g self-raising flour
  • 75 g light brown sugar
  • 1 medium egg,
  • beaten
  • 1 tsp mixed spice

Method

  1. Break the Weetabix into a bowl.
  2. Pour over the cooled tea and leave for 10 minutes to soften.
  3. Stir in the sultanas and leave to soak for at least 1 hour, or overnight in the refrigerator for a fuller flavour.
  4. Heat the oven to 170°C (fan 150°C).
  5. Grease and line a 2 lb loaf tin.
  6. Stir the flour, sugar, egg and mixed spice into the soaked mixture until evenly combined.
  7. Spoon into the tin and level the top.
  8. Bake for 50–60 minutes until a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean.
  9. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then transfer to a rack.
  10. Slice once completely cool.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Sultana Weetabix Cake bookmark. Source: https://www.slimmingeats.com/blog/sultana-weetabix-cake
