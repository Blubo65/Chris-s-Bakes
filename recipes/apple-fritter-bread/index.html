Apple Fritter Bread

A soft apple bread layered with cinnamon-sugar apples and finished with a simple sweet glaze.

Ingredients

  • 2 apples, peeled and chopped
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ⅓ cup brown sugar
  • 1½ cups plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ cup butter, softened
  • ⅔ cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ cup icing sugar
  • 1 tbsp milk

Method

  1. Mix the chopped apples with cinnamon and brown sugar. Set aside.
  2. Mix the flour, baking powder and salt.
  3. Cream the softened butter and sugar until light, then beat in the eggs and vanilla.
  4. Add the flour mixture in batches, alternating with the milk, mixing just until combined.
  5. Spoon half the batter into a greased and lined loaf tin. Add half the apple mixture, then repeat with the remaining batter and apples.
  6. Bake at 175°C (350°F) for 50–60 minutes, until a skewer inserted into the centre comes out clean.
  7. Cool completely. Mix the icing sugar with 1 tbsp milk and drizzle over the cooled loaf.

Recipe notes

Layer the cinnamon-sugar apples through the batter. Cool completely before adding the glaze.
