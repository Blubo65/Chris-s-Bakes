Rice Lasagna

This lasagna-style dish is made with cooked rice instead of noodles. It has a very simple ingredient list which makes this a great base recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 (26 oz) jar spaghetti sauce
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • 3 cups cooked rice, cooled
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • ¾ cup shredded Parmesan cheese, divided
  • 2 ¼ cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 2 cups cottage cheese

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. Cook and stir beef in hot skillet until browned and crumbly, 5 to 7 minutes
  4. drain and discard grease.
  5. Add spaghetti sauce and garlic powder.
  6. Mix rice, eggs, and 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese in a bowl.
  7. Mix 2 cups mozzarella cheese, cottage cheese, and 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese in another bowl.
  8. Spread 1/2 of the rice mixture in a baking dish, followed by 1/2 of the cheese mixture and 1/2 of the meat sauce. Repeat layers. Sprinkle remaining 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese and 1/4 cup mozzarella cheese over final layer of meat sauce.
  9. Bake in the preheated oven until cheese is melted and sauce is bubbly, 20 to 25 minutes.
