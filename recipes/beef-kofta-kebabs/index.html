Beef Kofta Kebabs

Juicy spiced beef koftas shaped around skewers and grilled, served with salad and flatbreads.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g lean beef mince
  • 1 small onion,
  • finely grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Flatbreads and salad,
  • to serve

Method

  1. Mix the beef mince with the grated onion, garlic, parsley, cumin, coriander, paprika, salt and pepper.
  2. Divide into 8 portions and shape each around a skewer or into small sausage shapes.
  3. Chill for 10 minutes if time allows.
  4. Heat a grill or griddle pan to medium-high.
  5. Cook the koftas for 10–14 minutes, turning regularly, until browned and cooked through.
  6. Check the centre is fully cooked, then rest for 2 minutes.
  7. Serve with flatbreads, salad and yoghurt mint sauce.
