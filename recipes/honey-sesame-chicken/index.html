Honey Sesame Chicken

Crispy air fryer chicken pieces coated in a sticky honey-soy glaze and finished with sesame seeds.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • diced
  • 1 egg,
  • beaten
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp oil spray
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • Sliced spring onions,
  • optional

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with the beaten egg, then coat with the cornflour.
  2. Preheat the air fryer to 200°C.
  3. Arrange the chicken in a single layer, lightly spray with oil and cook for 10–14 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and the thickest pieces reach 75°C.
  4. Cook in batches if needed.
  5. Meanwhile, simmer the honey, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic and ginger in a small pan for 2–3 minutes until slightly thickened.
  6. Stir in the sesame oil, then toss the cooked chicken in the glaze just before serving.
  7. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and spring onions.
  8. Serve with rice and vegetables.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Honey Sesame Chicken bookmark. Ensure chicken reaches 75°C internally. Source: https://fitfoodiefinds.com/air-fryer-honey-sesame-chicken/
