Crispy Shredded Chilli Chicken

Thin strips of chicken coated in a light crisp coating and tossed in a sweet chilli-soy sauce.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • cut into thin strips
  • 1 egg white
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 red pepper,
  • thinly sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 2 spring onions,
  • sliced

Method

  1. Toss the chicken strips with egg white and cornflour until evenly coated.
  2. Heat the oil in a large non-stick frying pan.
  3. Cook the chicken in batches until golden and cooked through, turning as needed; set aside.
  4. Add the pepper to the pan and stir-fry for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  6. Mix the sweet chilli sauce, soy sauce, vinegar and honey, then pour into the pan and simmer briefly until glossy.
  7. Return the chicken and toss quickly to coat.
  8. Garnish with spring onions and serve immediately with rice.
