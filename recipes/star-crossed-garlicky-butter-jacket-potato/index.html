Star-Crossed Garlicky Butter Jacket Potato

Crispy jacket potatoes cut into star-shaped crosses, stuffed with seasoned cream cheese and finished with roasted garlic parsley butter.

Ingredients

  • 2 large Maris Piper or Russet potatoes
  • 80 g cream cheese
  • 100 g salted butter
  • 1 whole bulb garlic
  • Small bunch parsley, roughly chopped
  • Salt, to taste
  • Ground white pepper, a pinch
  • Fine sugar, a pinch
  • Neutral oil, for coating potatoes
  • Olive oil, a drizzle for roasting garlic
  • Flaky sea salt, to finish

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C fan (220°C conventional) or the air fryer to 200°C. Prick the potatoes all over with a fork, rub with neutral oil and season generously with fine salt.
  2. Cut the top off the garlic bulb, drizzle with olive oil, season and wrap tightly in foil. Bake the potatoes and garlic together for 45–60 minutes, until the potatoes are tender inside with crisp skins.
  3. About 10 minutes before the potatoes finish, unwrap the roasted garlic and squeeze the softened cloves into the butter. Melt together briefly, stir in the chopped parsley and season with salt.
  4. Mix the cream cheese with a pinch each of salt, ground white pepper and fine sugar.
  5. Let the potatoes cool just enough to handle. Cut one long slit along the top and two diagonal slits crossing it to make a star. Gently open the cuts.
  6. Spoon or pipe the seasoned cream cheese into the centre and along each cut. Coat the tops generously with roasted garlic parsley butter.
  7. For the oven, increase the temperature to 210°C fan (230°C conventional)
  8. for the air fryer, keep at 200°C. Return the potatoes for 10–15 minutes until golden and bubbling. Finish with flaky sea salt.
