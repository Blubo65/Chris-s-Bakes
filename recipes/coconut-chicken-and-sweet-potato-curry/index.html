Coconut Chicken & Sweet Potato Curry

Chicken and sweet potato simmered in a gently spiced coconut curry sauce.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast or thighs,
  • diced
  • 1 large sweet potato,
  • peeled and cubed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 400 ml light coconut milk
  • 200 ml chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh coriander,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onion for 5 minutes until softened.
  2. Add the garlic, ginger and curry powder and stir for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the chicken and cook for 3–4 minutes, stirring to coat in the spices.
  4. Stir in the sweet potato, coconut milk, stock and tomato purée.
  5. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  6. Cover and cook for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sweet potato is tender and chicken reaches 75°C internally.
  7. Remove the lid briefly to thicken the sauce if needed.
  8. Season to taste and garnish with coriander if desired.
  9. Serve with rice.
