Slimming World Cowboy Hotpot

A hearty sausage, bean and vegetable hotpot topped with golden slices of potato.

Ingredients

  • 8 Slimming World Syn-free pork sausages (as specified in original recipe)
  • 4 large potatoes
  • Low-calorie cooking spray
  • 1 large onion, roughly chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 large carrots, peeled and diced
  • 150 ml hot vegetable stock
  • 400 g can chopped tomatoes
  • 415 g can baked beans
  • 400 g can mixed beans, drained and rinsed
  • 400 g chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper

Method

  1. Cook the sausages according to the packet instructions, cut each into three pieces and set aside.
  2. Cook the potatoes until tender, then drain and set aside.
  3. Spray a deep non-stick casserole pan with low-calorie cooking spray. Add the onion and garlic and cook over medium heat for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add a splash of water if the mixture starts to stick.
  4. Add the carrots, vegetable stock and chopped tomatoes. Bring to the boil, cover and simmer for 15 minutes.
  5. Stir in the baked beans, mixed beans and chickpeas, season with salt and pepper, and cook for another 5 minutes until heated through.
  6. Stir in the chopped sausages and spoon the mixture into individual dishes. Top with sliced potatoes.
  7. Grill for 2–3 minutes, or until the potatoes are browned.
