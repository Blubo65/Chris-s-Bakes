Chilli & Coriander Beef Burgers

Homemade beef burgers flavoured with fresh coriander, chilli and garlic.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g lean beef mince
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh coriander
  • 1 small garlic clove,
  • crushed
  • ½ tsp chilli flakes,
  • or to taste
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 4 burger buns and toppings,
  • to serve

Method

  1. Mix the beef mince with coriander, garlic, chilli flakes, cumin, salt and pepper.
  2. Shape into 4 even patties, pressing a shallow dimple in the centre of each.
  3. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  4. Place the burgers in a single layer, leaving space around them.
  5. Air fry for 10–14 minutes, turning halfway, until browned and cooked through.
  6. Cooking time depends on thickness.
  7. Check the centre is fully cooked; for minced beef, aim for 70°C for 2 minutes or follow current food-safety guidance.
  8. Rest briefly and serve in buns with salad and your favourite sauce.
