Mongolian-Style Meatballs

Tender beef meatballs glazed in a sticky soy, garlic and ginger sauce, served with rice.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef mince
  • 1 egg
  • 40 g breadcrumbs
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed,
  • divided
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar or honey
  • 150 ml water
  • 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp cold water
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 spring onions,
  • sliced
  • Sesame seeds,
  • optional

Method

  1. Mix the beef mince with the egg, breadcrumbs, half the crushed garlic, half the ginger and a little black pepper.
  2. Shape into about 16 meatballs.
  3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and brown the meatballs on all sides, working in batches if needed.
  4. Reduce the heat, cover and cook for 8–10 minutes until cooked through; transfer to a plate.
  5. Add the remaining garlic and ginger to the pan and stir for 30 seconds.
  6. Add the soy sauce, hoisin, sugar or honey and water.
  7. Bring to a simmer.
  8. Stir in the cornflour slurry and cook until the sauce thickens.
  9. Return the meatballs to the pan and toss gently until coated and piping hot.
  10. Garnish with spring onions and sesame seeds if desired.
  11. Serve with rice and vegetables.

Recipe notes
