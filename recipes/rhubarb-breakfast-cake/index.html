Rhubarb Breakfast Cake

A tender breakfast-style cake with tart rhubarb, vanilla and a lightly sugared top.

Time: 50 mins · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 220 g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 150 g caster sugar
  • 1 medium egg
  • 120 ml milk
  • 75 g melted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250 g rhubarb,
  • chopped
  • 1 tbsp sugar,
  • for topping

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Grease and line a 20 cm cake tin.
  3. Mix the flour, baking powder and sugar in a bowl.
  4. Whisk the egg with milk, melted butter and vanilla, then fold into the dry ingredients until just combined.
  5. Fold in the rhubarb and spoon into the tin.
  6. Sprinkle with the extra sugar.
  7. Bake for 30–40 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  8. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes before transferring to a rack.

Recipe notes
