Mushroom & Chickpea Curry

An easy Indian-style vegetarian curry of chickpeas and mushrooms with warming spices and tomato.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 onion, minced
  • 1 tin (400 g) chickpeas, drained (or 1¾ cups cooked chickpeas)
  • 700 g mushrooms, sliced
  • ½ tsp cayenne pepper (adjust to taste)
  • ½ tsp sea salt
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • Fresh coriander, to garnish (optional)

Method

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large shallow pan over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the cumin seeds, turmeric and garam masala. Cook for 30 seconds until the cumin seeds start to sizzle.
  3. Add the garlic and cook for another 30 seconds until lightly browned, taking care not to burn it.
  4. Add the onion and cook for a minute until browned.
  5. Add the chickpeas and stir thoroughly to coat in the spice mixture. Cook for a minute.
  6. Add the mushrooms and mix thoroughly. Sprinkle with sea salt and cayenne pepper, reduce the heat to medium-low and cover the pan.
  7. Cook gently, stirring occasionally, for 15 minutes until the mushrooms release their liquid.
  8. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for another 5 minutes.
  9. Spoon into bowls and garnish with fresh coriander, if using.
