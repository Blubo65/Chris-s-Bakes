Chicken Dopiaza

A fragrant chicken curry with plenty of onion, tomato and warming spices.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast or thighs,
  • diced
  • 2 large onions,
  • sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 150 ml chicken stock
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh coriander,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan.
  2. Add one sliced onion and cook for 6–8 minutes until soft and lightly golden.
  3. Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder, cumin and coriander.
  4. Stir for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the chicken and cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring to coat in the spices.
  6. Add the tomatoes and stock.
  7. Simmer gently for 18–22 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
  8. Meanwhile, soften the remaining onion in a separate pan until golden, then stir into the curry for the final 5 minutes.
  9. Season to taste, garnish with coriander if desired and serve with rice.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Chicken Dopiaza recipe. Source: https://www.slimmingworld.co.uk/recipes/slimming-world-chicken-dopiaza
