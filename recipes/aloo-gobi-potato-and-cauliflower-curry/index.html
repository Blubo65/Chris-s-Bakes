Aloo Gobi (Potato and Cauliflower Curry)

A comforting Indian-style dry curry of potatoes and cauliflower cooked with warming spices.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g potatoes,
  • peeled and cut into bite-sized cubes
  • 1 medium cauliflower,
  • cut into florets
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp chilli powder,
  • or to taste
  • 200 g chopped tomatoes
  • 100 ml water,
  • plus a little extra if needed
  • Salt,
  • to taste
  • Fresh coriander,
  • to garnish (optional)

Method

  1. Parboil the potato cubes in boiling water for 7–8 minutes, then drain well.
  2. Heat the oil in a large lidded pan.
  3. Cook the onion for 5–7 minutes until softened.
  4. Add the garlic, ginger, cumin, ground coriander, turmeric and chilli powder.
  5. Stir for 30–60 seconds until fragrant.
  6. Add the potatoes and cauliflower and stir to coat in the spices.
  7. Mix in the chopped tomatoes, water and salt.
  8. Cover and simmer gently for 18–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are tender.
  9. Add a splash of water if the pan becomes too dry; remove the lid for the final few minutes if you prefer it less saucy.
  10. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
  11. Scatter with fresh coriander if using and serve as a side dish or with rice or flatbreads.
