Chicken Wings

Crispy air fryer chicken wings with a simple garlic-paprika seasoning.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken wings
  • 1 tbsp baking powder,
  • not baking soda
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic granules
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Black pepper
  • Sauce of choice,
  • optional

Method

  1. Pat the chicken wings very dry with kitchen paper; this helps them crisp.
  2. Mix the baking powder, paprika, garlic granules, salt and pepper.
  3. Toss the wings in the seasoning until evenly coated.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  5. Arrange the wings in a single layer, leaving space for air to circulate.
  6. Air fry for 22–28 minutes, turning or shaking every 7–8 minutes, until crisp and the thickest parts reach at least 75°C internally.
  7. Toss with your favourite sauce if desired and serve hot.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved frozen chicken wings air fryer bookmark, using fresh wings. Do not overcrowd the basket; cooking time varies by wing size. Source: https://www.recipevibes.com/frozen-chicken-wings-in-air-fryer/
