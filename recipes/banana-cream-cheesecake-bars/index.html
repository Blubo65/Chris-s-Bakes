Banana Cream Cheesecake Bars

Creamy banana cheesecake bars on a biscuit base, finished with banana flavour and chilled until set.

Time: 1 hr plus chilling · Makes 9

Ingredients

  • 200 g digestive biscuits,
  • crushed
  • 75 g butter,
  • melted
  • 400 g full-fat cream cheese,
  • softened
  • 100 g caster sugar
  • 2 medium ripe bananas,
  • mashed
  • 2 medium eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 100 g Greek yoghurt or sour cream

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 160°C (fan 140°C).
  2. Line a 20 cm square tin with baking parchment.
  3. Mix the crushed biscuits with melted butter and press firmly into the base.
  4. Chill while preparing the filling.
  5. Beat the cream cheese and sugar until smooth.
  6. Mix in the mashed bananas, eggs, vanilla and yoghurt just until combined.
  7. Pour over the biscuit base and smooth the top.
  8. Bake for 35–45 minutes until the edges are set and the centre has a slight wobble.
  9. Turn off the oven and leave the door ajar for 15 minutes.
  10. Cool completely, then chill for at least 4 hours before cutting into bars.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Banana Cream Cheesecake Bars bookmark. Keep refrigerated and cut when fully chilled. Source: https://myincrediblerecipes.com/banana-cream-cheesecake-bars
