Chicken Tikka

Yoghurt-marinated chicken tikka pieces cooked in the air fryer until lightly charred and juicy.

Time: 25 mins plus marinating · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless chicken thighs or breast,
  • cubed
  • 150 g natural yoghurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp chilli powder, optional,
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Mix the yoghurt, lemon juice, garlic, ginger, paprika, cumin, coriander, turmeric, garam masala, chilli if using, salt and pepper.
  2. Add the chicken and coat thoroughly.
  3. Marinate in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes, ideally longer.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  5. Shake excess marinade from the chicken and arrange pieces in a single layer, leaving space around them.
  6. Air fry for 12–18 minutes, turning halfway, until lightly charred and the thickest pieces reach 75°C internally.
  7. Cook in batches if needed.
  8. Rest for 3 minutes and serve with rice, salad or flatbreads.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved chicken tikka masala/tandoori recipe collection, adjusted for air fryer chicken tikka pieces. Ensure chicken reaches 75°C internally.
