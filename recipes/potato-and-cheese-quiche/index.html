Potato & Cheese Quiche

A simple potato and cheese quiche with a savoury egg filling in crisp pastry.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet ready-rolled shortcrust pastry,
  • about 320 g
  • 250 g potatoes,
  • peeled and diced small
  • 4 medium eggs
  • 150 ml milk
  • 100 g grated cheddar
  • 1 small onion,
  • finely chopped
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried thyme

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 190°C (fan 170°C).
  2. Line a 20–22 cm quiche tin with pastry, trim the edges and prick the base.
  3. Blind bake for 10 minutes with baking beans, then remove the beans and bake for another 5 minutes.
  4. Boil the diced potatoes for 8–10 minutes until just tender, then drain and cool.
  5. Scatter the potatoes, onion and cheese over the pastry base.
  6. Whisk the eggs with milk, thyme, salt and pepper and pour over the filling.
  7. Bake for 25–30 minutes until the filling is set and golden.
  8. Rest for 10 minutes before slicing.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Potato Quiche video. Source: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/AHyvj3ZfPow
