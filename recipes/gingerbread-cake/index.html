Gingerbread Cake

A moist, warmly spiced gingerbread cake with ginger, cinnamon and golden syrup.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 200 g self-raising flour
  • 100 g light brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ½ tsp mixed spice
  • 75 g butter,
  • melted
  • 100 g golden syrup
  • 1 medium egg
  • 100 ml milk

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 150°C.
  2. Grease and line a small tin that fits comfortably in the basket.
  3. Mix the flour, sugar, ginger, cinnamon and mixed spice.
  4. Whisk the melted butter with the golden syrup, egg and milk.
  5. Fold into the dry ingredients until smooth.
  6. Spoon into the tin and level the top.
  7. Air fry for 25–35 minutes, checking from 20 minutes.
  8. Cover loosely if the top browns too quickly and your appliance permits it.
  9. Check a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean.
  10. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes before turning out.

Recipe notes

Practical air fryer adaptation inspired by the saved Ninja Air Fryer Gingerbread Cake video. Check the centre is cooked through. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IwZnFJEY6Ow
