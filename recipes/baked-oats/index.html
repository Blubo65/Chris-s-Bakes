Baked Oats

A warm, cake-like baked oat breakfast made with oats, banana, egg and milk.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 80 g rolled oats
  • 1 ripe banana,
  • mashed
  • 1 medium egg
  • 100 ml milk
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp honey or sweetener, optional,
  • A few berries or chocolate chips,
  • optional

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 160°C and lightly grease two small ovenproof ramekins or one small dish that fits the basket.
  2. Mix the oats, mashed banana, egg, milk, baking powder and cinnamon until combined.
  3. Add honey or sweetener if desired.
  4. Fold in berries or chocolate chips if using.
  5. Divide between the dishes and air fry for 12–18 minutes until set in the centre and lightly golden.
  6. Allow to cool for a few minutes before serving.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Baked Oatmeal bookmarks. Dish size affects timing; check the centre is set. Source: https://lifestyleofafoodie.com/air-fryer-baked-oats
