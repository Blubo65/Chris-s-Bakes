Chicken Tikka Masala

A popular creamy tomato curry with yoghurt-marinated chicken and warming Indian spices.

Time: 45 mins plus marinating · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast or thighs,
  • cubed
  • 150 g natural yoghurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 400 g passata or chopped tomatoes
  • 150 ml single cream or light coconut milk
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Mix the yoghurt, lemon juice, half the garlic, ginger, paprika, cumin and coriander.
  2. Add the chicken and marinate for at least 20 minutes.
  3. Heat half the oil in a frying pan and brown the chicken in batches until cooked through; set aside.
  4. Heat the remaining oil in a saucepan.
  5. Cook the onion for 5–6 minutes until soft, then add the remaining garlic and garam masala.
  6. Add the passata and simmer for 10 minutes.
  7. Stir in the cream or coconut milk and the cooked chicken.
  8. Simmer gently for 5 minutes until piping hot.
  9. Season to taste and serve with rice or naan.

Recipe notes
