Crispy Cauliflower Popcorn

Bite-sized cauliflower florets seasoned and roasted until golden, ideal as a snack or vegetable side.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower,
  • cut into small florets
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • ½ tsp ground cumin, optional,
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Lemon wedges,
  • to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (fan 200°C), or air fryer to 190°C.
  2. Pat the cauliflower florets dry and toss with olive oil, paprika, garlic granules, cumin if using, salt and pepper.
  3. For the oven, spread on a tray and roast for 20–25 minutes, turning halfway.
  4. For the air fryer, cook for 12–16 minutes, shaking the basket halfway.
  5. Cook until the edges are browned and the florets tender.
  6. Serve hot with lemon wedges.

Recipe notes
