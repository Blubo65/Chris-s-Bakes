Banana Bread

A moist banana loaf made with ripe bananas, ideal for a small loaf tin that fits in the air fryer.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 2 very ripe medium bananas,
  • mashed
  • 1 medium egg
  • 75 g caster sugar or light brown sugar
  • 60 ml vegetable oil or 60 g melted butter
  • 150 g self-raising flour
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon, optional,
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract,
  • optional

Method

  1. Mash the bananas in a bowl.
  2. Beat in the egg, sugar, oil or melted butter and vanilla if using.
  3. Fold in the flour and cinnamon until just combined; do not overmix.
  4. Spoon into a greased and lined small loaf tin that fits inside your air fryer, filling no more than about two-thirds full.
  5. Preheat the air fryer to 150°C.
  6. Bake for 30–40 minutes, checking from 25 minutes.
  7. If the top browns too quickly, cover loosely with foil if safe for your appliance.
  8. A skewer inserted into the centre should come out clean or with a few moist crumbs.
  9. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then transfer to a rack.

Recipe notes

Adapted air fryer method inspired by the saved BBC Good Food banana bread recipe. Tin size and cooking time vary; check the centre before removing. Source: https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/air-fryer-banana-bread
