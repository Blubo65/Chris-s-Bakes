One-Pot Cajun Chicken Pasta

Creamy Cajun-spiced pasta with chicken and peppers, cooked in one pan.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 400 g chicken breast,
  • sliced
  • 300 g dried pasta
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 red pepper, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 700 ml chicken stock,
  • hot
  • 150 ml single cream
  • 40 g grated Parmesan or cheddar
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large deep pan.
  2. Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  3. Add the onion and pepper and cook for 4 minutes.
  4. Stir in the garlic and Cajun seasoning.
  5. Add the pasta and hot stock.
  6. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered for 12–16 minutes, stirring frequently, until the pasta is tender.
  7. Add a splash of water if needed.
  8. Stir in the cream and grated cheese and heat gently until creamy and piping hot.
  9. Season to taste and serve immediately.
