Cheesy homity pie

Simple potatoes and vegetables turned into a comforting vegetarian pie.

Time: 1 hour · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • Low-calorie cooking spray
  • 600g floury potatoes,
  • such as Maris Piper or King Edward,
  • cut into cubes
  • 100ml hot vegetable stock
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 carrot,
  • finely diced
  • 2 celery sticks,
  • finely diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 6 spring onions,
  • sliced
  • 200g frozen peas
  • 200g baby spinach
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped fresh parsley
  • 2 large eggs,
  • lightly beaten
  • Mustard mixture,
  • as shown in the source recipe
  • Cheese,
  • for topping

Method

  1. Preheat your oven to 220°C/fan 200°C/gas 7.
  2. Spray a medium-size baking dish with low-calorie cooking spray.
  3. Boil the potatoes in a large saucepan for 12–15 minutes, or until tender.
  4. Drain, return to the pan and add the stock.
  5. Mash until smooth, then set aside.
  6. Spray a large non-stick frying pan with low-calorie cooking spray and put it over a medium heat.
  7. Cook the onion, carrot and celery for 8–10 minutes.
  8. Stir in the garlic, spring onions, peas and spinach, and cook for 2–3 minutes, or until the spinach has wilted.
  9. Add the parsley, eggs, mustard mixture, mashed potato and half the cheese.
  10. Season lightly and stir to combine.
  11. Spoon the mixture into the baking dish, smooth over the top and evenly scatter over the remaining cheese.
  12. Bake for 20 minutes, or until bubbling and golden.
