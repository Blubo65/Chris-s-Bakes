Peanut Butter Banana Rolls

Crisp pastry-style rolls filled with banana and peanut butter, served warm as a quick sweet treat.

Time: 15 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 small flour tortillas or spring-roll wrappers
  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 4 tbsp smooth peanut butter
  • 1 tsp honey, optional,
  • Oil spray
  • Icing sugar or cinnamon,
  • optional

Method

  1. Mash the bananas roughly and mix with the peanut butter and honey if using.
  2. Place a spoonful of filling near one edge of each tortilla or wrapper.
  3. Fold in the sides and roll tightly, sealing the edge with a little water if needed.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  5. Place the rolls seam-side down and lightly spray with oil.
  6. Air fry for 5–8 minutes, turning once if needed, until golden and crisp.
  7. Check early because thin wrappers brown quickly.
  8. Leave to cool for a few minutes before serving; the filling will be very hot.
  9. Dust with cinnamon or icing sugar if desired.
