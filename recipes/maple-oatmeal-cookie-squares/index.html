Maple Oatmeal Cookie Squares

Soft, sticky maple and oat squares baked in the air fryer.

Time: 12–14 mins · Makes 10 squares

Ingredients

  • 100g Tesco baking block,
  • melted
  • 90ml pancake syrup
  • 1 medium egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 150g rolled oats
  • 100g plain flour
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder

Method

  1. Line a 5 x 8 inch pan with parchment, leaving an overhang.
  2. Preheat air fryer to 160°C for 3 minutes.
  3. Melt baking block and cool for 1 minute.
  4. Whisk syrup, egg and vanilla into the melted block.
  5. Stir in oats, flour, brown sugar and baking powder until thick and sticky.
  6. If too wet add a tablespoon of oats; if too dry add a tablespoon of milk.
  7. Press evenly into the prepared pan.
  8. Air fry at 160°C for 12–14 minutes, until edges are set.
  9. Cool before cutting into squares.

Recipe notes

Ninja air fryer guide. Pan size 5 x 8 inches.
