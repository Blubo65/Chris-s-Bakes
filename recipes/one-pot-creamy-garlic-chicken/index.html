One-Pot Creamy Garlic Chicken

Chicken cooked in a creamy garlic and herb sauce in one pan, perfect with potatoes or vegetables.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast or boneless thighs
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 200 ml chicken stock
  • 150 ml single cream
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard, optional,
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp cold water,
  • if needed
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Chopped parsley,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan or casserole.
  2. Season the chicken and brown for 3–4 minutes on each side; remove to a plate.
  3. Add the onion and cook for 4 minutes until softened.
  4. Add the garlic and thyme and cook for 30 seconds.
  5. Pour in the stock and scrape up any browned bits.
  6. Return the chicken to the pan, cover and simmer gently for 10–15 minutes, depending on thickness, until cooked through.
  7. Stir in the cream and mustard if using.
  8. Simmer gently for 2–3 minutes; thicken with the cornflour slurry if desired.
  9. Season to taste, garnish with parsley and serve with vegetables, rice or potatoes.

Recipe notes
