Boneless Chicken Thighs

Juicy boneless chicken thighs with a simple smoky garlic seasoning and crisp edges.

Time: 22 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 700 g boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic granules
  • ½ tsp onion granules
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  2. Pat the chicken thighs dry.
  3. Mix the oil with paprika, garlic granules, onion granules, oregano, salt and pepper.
  4. Coat the thighs evenly.
  5. Arrange in a single layer in the basket.
  6. Air fry for 16–22 minutes, turning halfway, depending on thickness.
  7. Check the thickest piece reaches 75°C internally.
  8. Continue cooking in short increments if needed.
  9. Rest for 3 minutes before serving with vegetables or potatoes.

Recipe notes

Adapted home method inspired by the saved Air Fryer Boneless Chicken Thighs bookmark. Check doneness with a thermometer as sizes vary. Source: https://www.airfryeryum.com/air-fryer-boneless-chicken-thighs/
