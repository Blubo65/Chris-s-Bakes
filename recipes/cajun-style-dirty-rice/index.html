Cajun-Style Dirty Rice

A Cajun-style dirty rice dish with lean beef, bacon, peppers, mushrooms and spring onions.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 200g rice
  • 400g lean minced beef
  • 4 bacon medallions
  • ½ red pepper,
  • diced
  • ½ yellow pepper,
  • diced
  • ½ green pepper,
  • diced
  • ½ onion,
  • diced
  • 1 medium carrot,
  • diced
  • 6 mushrooms,
  • quartered
  • 1 bunch spring onions,
  • sliced
  • 1 beef stock pot
  • 1 chicken stock cube
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 200ml boiling water
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 dash Worcestershire sauce
  • Low calorie cooking spray

Method

  1. Cook the rice according to the packet instructions, adding a bay leaf and chicken stock cube to the water before cooking.
  2. Set the rice aside once cooked.
  3. Add the stock pot to 200ml of boiling water and set aside.
  4. This is separate from the water used to cook the rice.
  5. Spray a frying pan with low calorie cooking spray, then cook the mince, onions and bacon until brown.
  6. Add the Cajun seasoning and Worcestershire sauce and stir.
  7. Add the carrots, mushrooms, peppers and the made-up beef stock.
  8. Cook until the peppers start to soften.
  9. Add the cooked rice and spring onion and stir until all the rice is coated.
  10. Taste and add more Cajun seasoning if it is not spicy enough.

Recipe notes

Prep time: 10 minutes. Cook time: 30 minutes. 369 kcal and 45g carbohydrates per serving are stated on the supplied Pinch of Nom PDF. Source: Pinch of Nom.
