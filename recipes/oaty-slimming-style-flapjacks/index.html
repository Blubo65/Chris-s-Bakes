Oaty Slimming-Style Flapjacks

Simple oat squares with golden syrup and a soft, chewy centre.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 9

Ingredients

  • 200 g rolled oats
  • 60 g butter
  • 50 g light brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp golden syrup
  • Pinch of salt
  • ½ tsp cinnamon,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Line a small square baking tin with parchment.
  3. Warm the butter, sugar and golden syrup gently in a saucepan until melted and combined; do not boil.
  4. Stir in the oats, salt and cinnamon if using until evenly coated.
  5. Press the mixture firmly into the tin and smooth the surface.
  6. Bake for 15–20 minutes until lightly golden at the edges.
  7. The centre may still be soft.
  8. Cool completely in the tin before cutting into squares, as the flapjacks firm up as they cool.

Recipe notes

Practical home adaptation inspired by the saved Slimming World flapjack bookmark; this version contains butter, sugar and syrup and is not a syn-free recipe. Source: https://pebblesrecipes.wordpress.com/2013/01/12/tracys-slimming-world-flapjack
