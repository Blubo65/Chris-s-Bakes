Bacon, Kale and Sweet Potato Frittata

A quick single-serving frittata with lean bacon, kale, juicy tomatoes and leftover sweet potato mash, optionally topped with cheddar.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices cooked lean back bacon, chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, quartered
  • ⅓ cup (80 ml) leftover sweet potato mash
  • ½ cup (120 ml) kale
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • Grated cheddar cheese, optional
  • Low-calorie cooking spray
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Lightly spray a small ovenproof frying pan with low-calorie cooking spray and heat over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the quartered tomatoes and fry until softened.
  3. Add the kale and chopped cooked bacon, stirring well.
  4. Add the leftover sweet potato mash and stir to combine.
  5. Pour in the beaten eggs and season with salt and black pepper. Cook until the base is set.
  6. Sprinkle with grated cheddar, if using.
  7. Place the pan under a preheated grill until the top is lightly golden and the eggs are fully set.
  8. Serve immediately.
