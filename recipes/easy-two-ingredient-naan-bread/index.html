Easy Two-Ingredient Naan Bread

Quick soft flatbreads made from self-raising flour and natural yoghurt, perfect with curries.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 200 g self-raising flour,
  • plus extra for dusting
  • 200 g thick natural yoghurt
  • Pinch of salt, optional,
  • Melted butter or garlic butter,
  • optional

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yoghurt and salt if using in a bowl until a soft dough forms.
  2. Add a little extra flour if very sticky.
  3. Turn onto a lightly floured surface and knead gently for 1 minute until smooth.
  4. Divide into 4 balls.
  5. Roll each ball into an oval or round about 3–5 mm thick.
  6. Heat a dry frying pan over medium-high heat.
  7. Cook each naan for 1–2 minutes per side until puffed and browned in spots, adjusting the heat as needed.
  8. Brush with melted or garlic butter if desired and serve warm.

Recipe notes

Home method based on the saved two-ingredient naan bookmark. Flour and yoghurt moisture vary; add flour gradually to make a soft, manageable dough. Source: https://diethood.com/easy-naan-bread-recipe
