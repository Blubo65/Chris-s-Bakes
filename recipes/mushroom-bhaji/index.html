Mushroom Bhaji

A Free version of a tasty Indian starter, ideal with curry night or as a vegetarian main with rice and salad.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 1 large floury potato,
  • such as King Edward or Maris Piper,
  • cubed
  • 1 onion,
  • quartered and sliced
  • 1 garlic clove,
  • crushed
  • 110ml hot vegetable stock
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 225g mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 tomatoes,
  • deseeded and chopped
  • Plain boiled rice,
  • fat-free natural yogurt,
  • a tomato,
  • red onion and cucumber salad,
  • lime wedges and coriander sprigs,
  • to serve

Method

  1. Put the potato, onion, garlic, stock and garam masala in a large non-stick saucepan over a high heat and bring to the boil.
  2. Reduce the heat, cover and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Stir the mushrooms and tomatoes into the pan.
  4. Cover and simmer for a further 15 minutes or until the vegetables are tender.
  5. Serve with the rice, yogurt, salad and lime wedges on the side and with the coriander scattered over.
