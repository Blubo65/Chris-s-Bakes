Loaded Potato Fritters

Crispy potato fritters mixed with cheese and spring onions, cooked in the air fryer.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g potatoes,
  • peeled and grated
  • 1 egg
  • 60 g grated cheddar
  • 2 spring onions,
  • finely sliced
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Oil spray

Method

  1. Grate the potatoes and squeeze out as much moisture as possible in a clean tea towel.
  2. Mix the potato with the egg, cheddar, spring onions, flour, garlic granules, salt and pepper.
  3. Shape into 8 small flat patties and press firmly so they hold together.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  5. Place the fritters in a single layer and spray lightly with oil.
  6. Air fry for 12–16 minutes, turning carefully halfway, until crisp and golden.
  7. Cook in batches if needed.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Loaded Air Fryer Potato Fritters bookmark. Squeeze out excess moisture to help them crisp. Source: https://dailyyum.com/air-fryer-potato-fritters
