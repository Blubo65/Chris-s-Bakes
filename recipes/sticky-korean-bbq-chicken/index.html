Sticky Korean BBQ Chicken

Chicken pieces coated in a sticky Korean-inspired sauce with soy, garlic, ginger and a touch of sweetness.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless chicken thighs,
  • cut into chunks
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp gochujang paste
  • 2 tbsp honey or brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • 2 spring onions,
  • sliced

Method

  1. Mix the soy sauce, gochujang, honey, rice vinegar and sesame oil in a bowl.
  2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan.
  3. Cook the chicken in batches until browned and cooked through.
  4. Add the garlic and ginger and stir for 30 seconds.
  5. Pour in the sauce and simmer for 2–4 minutes, turning the chicken until coated in a glossy glaze.
  6. Add a splash of water if the sauce becomes too thick.
  7. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and spring onions.
  8. Serve with rice and vegetables.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Sticky Korean BBQ Chicken link. Gochujang varies in heat and saltiness, so adjust to taste. Source: https://julesthelazycook.co.uk/2019/11/14/sticky-korean-bbq-chicken
