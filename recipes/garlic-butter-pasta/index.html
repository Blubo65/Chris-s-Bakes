Garlic Butter Pasta

A simple pasta tossed with butter, garlic, Parmesan and parsley for a quick comforting meal.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 3

Ingredients

  • 250 g spaghetti or linguine
  • 40 g butter
  • 5 garlic cloves,
  • finely chopped
  • 30 g grated Parmesan
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 100 ml reserved pasta water
  • Black pepper
  • Salt,
  • to taste
  • Pinch of chilli flakes,
  • optional

Method

  1. Cook the pasta in boiling salted water according to the packet.
  2. Reserve a mug of cooking water before draining.
  3. Melt the butter in a large frying pan over low heat.
  4. Add the garlic and cook gently for 30–60 seconds without browning.
  5. Add the drained pasta and toss with the butter and garlic.
  6. Add the Parmesan and a splash of pasta water, tossing until a glossy sauce forms.
  7. Add more water a little at a time if needed.
  8. Stir through the parsley and black pepper.
  9. Add chilli flakes if desired and serve immediately.

Recipe notes
