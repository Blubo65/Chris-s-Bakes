Coffee Cake

A classic two-layer coffee sponge with coffee buttercream, made in the air fryer.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 8 slices

Ingredients

  • 170g self-raising flour
  • 170g caster sugar
  • 170g butter or margarine,
  • softened
  • 2 tsp strong coffee,
  • cooled
  • 3 large eggs (or 4 small eggs)
  • Walnuts,
  • to decorate (optional)
  • Coffee icing:
  • 200g icing sugar
  • 100g butter or margarine,
  • softened
  • 2 tsp strong coffee,
  • cooled

Method

  1. Grease and line two suitable cake tins that fit your air fryer.
  2. Beat the butter or margarine with the sugar until combined and lighter in texture.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, adding a spoonful of flour if the mixture starts to curdle.
  4. Fold in the remaining flour and the cooled coffee until evenly mixed.
  5. Divide the mixture between the prepared tins and level the tops.
  6. Bake in the air fryer using the BAKE setting at 140°C for about 45 minutes.
  7. Check with a skewer from around 40 minutes; it should come out clean.
  8. If your air fryer has two drawers, follow its instructions for cooking both tins together.
  9. Leave the cakes in their tins for a few minutes, then turn out onto a rack and cool completely.
  10. For the icing, beat the softened butter with the icing sugar and cooled coffee until smooth.
  11. Trim the cakes level if needed, spread icing over one layer, place the second layer on top and finish with the remaining icing.
  12. Decorate with walnuts if desired.

Recipe notes

Source saved in Raindrop: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wZJ6ajLE3_o . Original recipe specifies 6-inch tins for some air fryers and 140°C for 45 minutes; tin fit and timing vary. Not yet tested by Chris.
