Sticky Honey Garlic Chicken

Tender chicken bites coated in a sticky honey, garlic and soy glaze.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • diced
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar or lemon juice
  • 100 ml chicken stock
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds, optional,
  • Sliced spring onions,
  • optional

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with cornflour until lightly coated.
  2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and cook the chicken in batches until golden and cooked through.
  3. Add the garlic and stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Mix the honey, soy sauce, vinegar and stock in a small bowl and pour into the pan.
  5. Simmer for 2–4 minutes, stirring, until the sauce reduces to a glossy glaze and coats the chicken.
  6. Check the chicken reaches 75°C internally.
  7. Garnish with sesame seeds and spring onions if desired and serve with rice.
