Fanta-Style Sticky Chicken

Chicken simmered in a tangy orange-flavoured tomato sauce until glossy and sticky.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 red pepper, sliced
  • 330 ml orange fizzy drink
  • 200 g passata
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp garlic granules
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan.
  2. Cook the onion and pepper for 4–5 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the chicken and cook until the outside is no longer pink.
  4. Stir in the orange fizzy drink, passata, tomato purée, soy sauce, garlic granules and paprika.
  5. Simmer uncovered for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce reduces to a sticky glaze and the chicken is fully cooked.
  6. Season with black pepper and serve with rice or vegetables.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Fanta Chicken bookmark. This uses a sweetened orange fizzy drink; sauce reduction and sweetness vary by brand. Source: https://slimmingviolet.com/syn-free-fanta-chicken-slimming-world
