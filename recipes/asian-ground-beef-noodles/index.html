Asian Ground Beef Noodles

Quick noodles with savoury minced beef, garlic, ginger and a soy-based sauce.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g egg noodles
  • 400 g lean beef mince
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce, optional,
  • 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 1 red pepper,
  • thinly sliced
  • 100 ml beef stock or water
  • 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp cold water
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 spring onions,
  • sliced

Method

  1. Cook the noodles according to the packet, drain and set aside.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok or large frying pan.
  3. Add the beef mince and cook until browned and fully cooked.
  4. Add the garlic, ginger and pepper and stir-fry for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Stir in the soy sauce, oyster sauce if using, hoisin and stock.
  6. Simmer for 2 minutes.
  7. Add the cornflour slurry and stir until the sauce thickens.
  8. Toss in the noodles and heat until piping hot.
  9. Garnish with spring onions and serve.
