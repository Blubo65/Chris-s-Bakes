Banana and Chocolate Muffins

Easy, soft banana muffins made with ripe bananas and dark chocolate chips, with an optional crunchy walnut topping.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 230g self-raising flour
  • 3½ tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 150g softened butter
  • 75g caster sugar
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 90g dark chocolate chips
  • 30g walnuts, roughly chopped (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan / Gas 4).
  2. Line a 12-hole muffin tin with paper muffin cases.
  3. Peel two bananas, cut into pieces and mash finely in a bowl. Keep the remaining banana for the topping.
  4. In a separate bowl, mix the self-raising flour, baking powder and cinnamon.
  5. Beat the softened butter with the caster sugar, vanilla and salt until pale and creamy.
  6. Beat in the eggs, then stir in the mashed bananas.
  7. Add the flour mixture and fold until combined.
  8. Finally, fold in the chocolate chips.
  9. Divide the mixture between the 12 paper cases.
  10. Peel and slice the remaining banana and arrange slices on top of the muffins.
  11. Sprinkle with the chopped walnuts if using.
  12. Bake for 20–25 minutes, until risen and golden.
  13. Leave to cool briefly in the tin, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
