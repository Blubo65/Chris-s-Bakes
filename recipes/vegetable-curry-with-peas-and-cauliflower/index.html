Vegetable Curry with Peas & Cauliflower

A simple vegetable curry with cauliflower, peas and potatoes in a gently spiced tomato sauce.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower,
  • cut into florets
  • 250 g potatoes,
  • diced
  • 150 g frozen peas
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 200 ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onion for 5 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the garlic, ginger and curry powder and stir for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the potatoes, cauliflower, chopped tomatoes and stock.
  4. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  5. Cover and cook for 18–22 minutes until the potatoes and cauliflower are nearly tender.
  6. Stir in the peas and cook for another 4–5 minutes.
  7. Season to taste and serve with rice or flatbreads.

Recipe notes
