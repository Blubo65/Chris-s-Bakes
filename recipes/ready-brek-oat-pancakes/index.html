Ready Brek Oat Pancakes

Soft oat-based pancakes made with Ready Brek, egg and milk for a quick breakfast.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 60 g Ready Brek or finely ground oats
  • 2 medium eggs
  • 100 ml milk
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract, optional,
  • A little oil for the pan
  • Fruit or yoghurt,
  • to serve

Method

  1. Whisk the Ready Brek, eggs, milk and baking powder together until smooth.
  2. Add vanilla if using.
  3. Leave the batter for 3–5 minutes to thicken slightly.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled non-stick frying pan over medium-low heat.
  5. Spoon small rounds of batter into the pan.
  6. Cook for 2–3 minutes until bubbles appear and the edges look set, then flip and cook for another 1–2 minutes.
  7. Repeat with the remaining batter and serve with fruit or yoghurt.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Ready Brek pancake recipe. Source: https://www.whingewhingewine.co.uk/ready-brek-pancakes-recipe-gluten-free.html
