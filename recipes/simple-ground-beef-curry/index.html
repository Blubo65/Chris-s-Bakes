Simple Ground Beef Curry

A quick minced beef curry with onion, tomatoes and warming spices, served with rice.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g lean beef mince
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 150 ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh coriander,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan and cook the onion for 5 minutes until softened.
  2. Add the beef mince and cook until browned, breaking it up as it cooks.
  3. Stir in the garlic, ginger, curry powder and cumin and cook for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes and stock.
  5. Simmer for 20–25 minutes until the sauce thickens and the beef is fully cooked.
  6. Season to taste and garnish with coriander if desired.
  7. Serve with rice or flatbreads.

Recipe notes
