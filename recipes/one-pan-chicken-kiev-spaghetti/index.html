One-Pan Chicken Kiev Spaghetti

A one-pan pasta inspired by Chicken Kiev, with garlic, herbs, chicken and a buttery flavour.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g spaghetti,
  • broken in half if needed
  • 400 g chicken breast,
  • thinly sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves,
  • finely chopped
  • 700 ml chicken stock,
  • hot
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 25 g butter
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 40 g grated Parmesan
  • Black pepper,
  • to taste

Method

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large deep pan.
  2. Add the chicken and cook for 4–5 minutes until lightly golden.
  3. Add the garlic and butter and stir for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add the spaghetti and hot stock.
  5. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered, stirring frequently, for 10–12 minutes or until the pasta is tender.
  6. Add a splash of hot water if the pan gets dry too soon.
  7. Check the chicken is cooked through and the pasta is tender.
  8. Stir in the parsley, lemon juice and Parmesan.
  9. Season with black pepper and serve immediately.

Recipe notes
