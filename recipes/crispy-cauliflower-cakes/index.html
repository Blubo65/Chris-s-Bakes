Crispy Cauliflower Cakes

Golden cauliflower patties with cheese, herbs and a gentle curry spice, served with yoghurt sauce.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 400 g cauliflower florets
  • 1 medium egg
  • 75 g grated cheddar
  • 40 g breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Oil spray

Method

  1. Steam or boil the cauliflower until tender.
  2. Drain thoroughly, then chop or mash coarsely and cool slightly.
  3. Mix with the egg, cheddar, breadcrumbs, flour, curry powder, parsley, salt and pepper.
  4. Shape into 8 small patties and chill for 10 minutes if possible.
  5. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C), or air fryer to 190°C.
  6. Place the patties on a lined tray or in the air fryer basket and spray lightly with oil.
  7. Bake for 20–25 minutes or air fry for 12–16 minutes, turning carefully halfway, until golden and firm.
  8. Serve warm with yoghurt or mint sauce.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Crispy Cauliflower Cakes video. Drain the cauliflower well to help the patties hold together. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h0L7jAl7fOA
