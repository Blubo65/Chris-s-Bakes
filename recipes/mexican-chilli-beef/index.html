Mexican Chilli Beef

A warming beef stew with Mexican-inspired spices, tomatoes, beans and peppers.

Time: 45 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g lean beef mince
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 x 400 g tin kidney beans,
  • drained
  • 1 x 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 200 ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp mild chilli powder,
  • or to taste
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan.
  2. Add the onion and pepper and cook for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the mince and cook until browned, breaking it up as it cooks.
  4. Stir in the garlic, cumin, paprika, chilli powder and tomato purée.
  5. Cook for 30 seconds.
  6. Add the tomatoes, stock and kidney beans.
  7. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered for 25–30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until rich and thick.
  8. Season to taste and serve with rice, baked potatoes or a little grated cheese.
