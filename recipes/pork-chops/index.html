Pork Chops

Juicy pork chops with a simple garlic-paprika seasoning and a browned air fryer finish.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 2 pork chops,
  • about 175–225 g each
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • ½ tsp onion granules, optional,
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  2. Pat the pork chops dry.
  3. Mix the oil with paprika, garlic granules, onion granules if using, salt and pepper.
  4. Rub over both sides of the chops.
  5. Place in the basket in a single layer.
  6. Air fry for 10–14 minutes depending on thickness, turning halfway.
  7. Check the centre with a meat thermometer; pork should reach at least 63°C followed by a 3-minute rest.
  8. Cook longer if needed and follow local food-safety guidance.
  9. Rest for 3 minutes before serving.

Recipe notes
