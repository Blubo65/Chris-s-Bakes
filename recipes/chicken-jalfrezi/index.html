Chicken Jalfrezi

A punchy tomato-based chicken curry with peppers, onions and aromatic spices.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • diced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 red pepper, sliced
  • 1 green pepper, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp chilli powder,
  • or to taste
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan.
  2. Add the onion and peppers and cook for 5 minutes until beginning to soften.
  3. Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder, cumin, coriander and chilli powder.
  4. Stir for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the chicken and cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring to coat in the spices.
  6. Stir in the chopped tomatoes and simmer gently for 18–22 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce thickens.
  7. Season to taste and serve with rice or flatbreads.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Chicken Jalfrezi recipe. Source: https://tastefullyvikkie.com/syn-free-slimming-world-chicken-jalfrezi-indian-curry-recipe
