Roasted Garlic & Mushroom Spaghetti

Spaghetti tossed with sweet roasted garlic, mushrooms, shallots, spinach and Parmesan.

Time: 35–40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 15–20 garlic cloves,
  • peeled
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tbsp chilli flakes,
  • or less to taste
  • A few sprigs of rosemary or another herb
  • 4 shallots,
  • sliced
  • 400g mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 large handfuls of spinach
  • 1 knob of butter
  • 4 tbsp grated Parmesan

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C fan.
  2. Put the peeled garlic cloves in a small ovenproof dish with enough olive oil to coat, a little salt and the rosemary.
  3. Cover and roast for 25–30 minutes until very soft.
  4. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti in a large pan of salted boiling water according to the packet instructions.
  5. Heat a little olive oil in a large frying pan and soften the shallots for a few minutes.
  6. Add the sliced mushrooms and butter and cook until the mushrooms soften and start to brown.
  7. Stir in the spinach and season with salt and pepper.
  8. Mash the roasted garlic with a fork and stir it into the mushroom mixture with chilli flakes to taste.
  9. Use tongs to transfer the cooked spaghetti directly into the frying pan, allowing a little pasta water to come with it.
  10. Toss together, loosening with extra pasta water if needed.
  11. Serve with grated Parmesan.
