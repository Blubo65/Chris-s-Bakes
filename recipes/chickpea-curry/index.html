Chickpea Curry

A simple chickpea curry with onion, tomato and warming spices; made with tinned chickpeas for speed.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 x 400 g tins chickpeas,
  • drained and rinsed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 200 ml vegetable stock or water
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Lemon juice,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a saucepan and cook the onion for 5 minutes until softened.
  2. Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder and cumin and stir for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the chickpeas, chopped tomatoes and stock.
  4. Bring to a simmer.
  5. Cook gently for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Mash a few chickpeas against the side of the pan to thicken the sauce if desired.
  7. Season with salt, pepper and a squeeze of lemon juice.
  8. Serve with rice or flatbreads.

Recipe notes

Stovetop home adaptation inspired by the saved chickpea curry bookmark; uses tinned chickpeas rather than the dried chickpeas in the pressure-cooker source. Source: https://www.chefdehome.com/recipes/626/vegan-chickpea-curry-in-pressure-cooker
