Cauliflower, Paneer & Pea Curry

A creamy vegetarian curry with cauliflower, paneer cheese and peas in a warmly spiced tomato sauce.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower,
  • cut into florets
  • 225 g paneer,
  • cubed
  • 150 g frozen peas
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 150 ml vegetable stock
  • 100 ml natural yoghurt or light cream
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan.
  2. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder and cumin and stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the cauliflower, chopped tomatoes and stock.
  5. Cover and simmer for 15–18 minutes until the cauliflower is nearly tender.
  6. Add the paneer and peas and simmer for 5–7 minutes until heated through and the cauliflower is tender.
  7. Turn the heat low and stir in the yoghurt or cream.
  8. Do not boil hard after adding yoghurt.
  9. Season to taste and serve with rice.

Recipe notes
