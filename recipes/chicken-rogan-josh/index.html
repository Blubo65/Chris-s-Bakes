Chicken Rogan Josh

A warming tomato-based chicken curry with aromatic spices and a rich red sauce.

Time: 40 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast or thighs,
  • diced
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 150 ml chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp natural yoghurt, optional,
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan.
  2. Cook the onion for 5–7 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 30 seconds.
  4. Stir in the paprika, cumin, coriander, garam masala and turmeric.
  5. Add the chicken and stir to coat in the spices.
  6. Cook for 4–5 minutes until the outside changes colour.
  7. Add the tomatoes and stock.
  8. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 18–22 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce thickens.
  9. Stir in the yoghurt off the heat if using.
  10. Season to taste and serve with rice or flatbreads.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Chicken Rogan Josh recipe. Source: https://www.easypeasy-lemonsqueezy.co.uk/chicken-rogan-josh-slimming-style
