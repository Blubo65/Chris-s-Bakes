BARA Brith (WELSH Tea BREAD) 2 x 1b loafs

A homemade recipe from The Chidsey Chowdown: BARA Brith (WELSH Tea BREAD) 2 x 1b loafs.

Ingredients

  • 250g Self raising flour
  • 100g soft dark brown sugar
  • 400g dried mixed fruit
  • 1 medium egg
  • 1 tsp Mixed Spice
  • 300ml Hot Tea

Method

  1. Mix sifted flour with the *dried mixed fruit/hot tea/sugar mixture, add 1 tsp
  2. Mixed Spice and place into pre-prepared loaf tins.
  3. Preheat airfryer for 4mins on 200c/390f.
  4. Choose BAKE setting 150c/300f 30mins, you may need to adjust cooking time depending on your Airfryer - check at 25mins.
  5. Once cooked, leave to cool in tins for 15/20mins before leaving on cooling rack.
  6. Slice and enjoy buttered with a cup of tea
