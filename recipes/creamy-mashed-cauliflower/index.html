Creamy Mashed Cauliflower

A smooth, creamy cauliflower mash with garlic and a little cheese, a lighter alternative to mashed potato.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 large cauliflower,
  • cut into florets
  • 1 garlic clove,
  • crushed
  • 2 tbsp cream cheese or butter
  • 30 g grated Parmesan or mature cheddar
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Chopped chives,
  • optional

Method

  1. Steam or boil the cauliflower until very tender, about 10–15 minutes.
  2. Drain extremely well and leave in the hot pan for 2 minutes to allow excess moisture to evaporate.
  3. Blend or mash the cauliflower with garlic, cream cheese or butter and grated cheese until smooth.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. If the mash is too loose, warm gently in a pan for a few minutes, stirring, to evaporate extra moisture.
  6. Garnish with chives if desired and serve hot.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved creamy mashed cauliflower bookmark. Draining thoroughly is key to a thick mash. Source: https://www.loveandotherspices.com/best-mashed-cauliflower-recipe
