Slimming World Salt and Pepper Chips

Golden oven-baked chips with finely chopped red pepper and onion, inspired by Chinese takeaway salt and pepper chips.

Ingredients

  • 6 large Maris Piper potatoes, peeled and cut into 1 cm thick chips
  • ½ red pepper, deseeded and finely chopped
  • ½ white onion, peeled and finely sliced
  • Low-calorie cooking spray
  • Crushed sea salt, to taste
  • Malt vinegar, to serve

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 240°C (220°C fan/Gas 9). Bring a large saucepan of salted water to the boil. Add the chips and cook for 4 minutes. Drain and leave aside for 10 minutes to dry.
  2. Return the chips to the dry saucepan, cover with a lid and shake to roughen their edges
  3. this helps them crisp up.
  4. Spray a metal baking tray with low-calorie cooking spray. Transfer the chips to the tray, scatter over the chopped red pepper and sliced onion, and spray lightly again. Bake for 30 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden brown on all sides. Drain briefly on kitchen paper and serve with crushed sea salt and malt vinegar.
