Baked Spaghetti

A compact pasta bake with tomato sauce and melted cheese, cooked in an air fryer-safe dish.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 3

Ingredients

  • 250 g cooked spaghetti
  • 250 g passata
  • 1 garlic clove,
  • crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 100 g grated mozzarella or cheddar
  • 1 tbsp grated Parmesan, optional,
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tsp olive oil

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  2. Lightly grease a small ovenproof dish that fits safely in the basket.
  3. Mix the passata with garlic, oregano, salt and pepper.
  4. Toss the cooked spaghetti with the sauce.
  5. Spoon into the dish and scatter with mozzarella or cheddar and Parmesan if using.
  6. Air fry for 10–15 minutes until the sauce is bubbling and cheese golden.
  7. Check early as dish size and air fryer model vary.
  8. Rest for 3 minutes before serving.

Recipe notes

Practical adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Spaghetti bookmark; uses already-cooked pasta. Source: https://allguiderecipes.info/air-fryer-spaghetti/
