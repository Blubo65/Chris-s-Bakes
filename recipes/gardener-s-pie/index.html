Gardener’s Pie

A hearty vegetable and lentil filling topped with creamy mashed potato and baked until golden.

Time: 55 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 800 g potatoes,
  • peeled and chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots,
  • diced
  • 150 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 x 400 g tin lentils,
  • drained
  • 150 g frozen peas
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 400 ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 25 g butter
  • Splash of milk
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes until tender.
  3. Drain and mash with butter, milk, salt and pepper.
  4. Meanwhile, soften the onion and carrots in a saucepan with a splash of water or a little oil.
  5. Add the mushrooms and cook for 4 minutes.
  6. Stir in the tomato purée, thyme, lentils, peas and vegetable stock.
  7. Simmer for 10–15 minutes until the filling thickens; season to taste.
  8. Spoon the filling into an ovenproof dish and spread the mash over the top, roughing the surface with a fork.
  9. Bake for 20–25 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  10. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Recipe notes
