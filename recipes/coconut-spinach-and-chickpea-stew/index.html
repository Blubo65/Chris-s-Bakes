Coconut Spinach & Chickpea Stew

Chickpeas and spinach simmered in a lightly spiced coconut sauce with a bright lemon finish.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 x 400 g tins chickpeas,
  • drained and rinsed
  • 150 g fresh spinach
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp ground coriander
  • 400 ml light coconut milk
  • 200 ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a saucepan and cook the onion for 5 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the garlic, cumin and coriander and stir for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the chickpeas, coconut milk and vegetable stock.
  4. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 12–15 minutes.
  5. Stir in the spinach in handfuls until wilted.
  6. Mash a few chickpeas with the back of a spoon to thicken the stew if desired.
  7. Finish with lemon juice, season to taste and serve with rice or flatbread.
