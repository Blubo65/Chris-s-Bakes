Beef & Onion Pasties

Golden pastry pasties filled with savoury minced beef, onion and a little potato.

Time: 50 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet ready-rolled shortcrust pastry,
  • about 320 g
  • 300 g lean beef mince
  • 1 small onion,
  • finely chopped
  • 1 small potato,
  • peeled and diced very small
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 egg, beaten, for glazing

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Heat the oil in a frying pan and cook the onion for 3–4 minutes.
  3. Add the mince and brown thoroughly.
  4. Stir in the potato, Worcestershire sauce, salt, pepper and 2 tbsp water.
  5. Cover and cook for 8–10 minutes until the potato is nearly tender.
  6. Cool completely.
  7. Cut the pastry into 4 circles or squares.
  8. Divide the cooled filling between them, leaving a clear edge.
  9. Brush the edges with beaten egg, fold over and crimp firmly to seal.
  10. Cut a small steam vent and brush the tops with egg.
  11. Bake for 25–30 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  12. Allow to cool slightly before serving.
