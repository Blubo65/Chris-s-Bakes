Potatoes & Sausage

A simple all-in-one air fryer meal with seasoned potatoes, smoked sausage and peppers.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g potatoes,
  • cut into small cubes
  • 400 g smoked sausage,
  • sliced
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 1 onion,
  • cut into wedges
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  2. Pat the potato cubes dry and toss with olive oil, paprika, garlic granules, salt and pepper.
  3. Air fry the potatoes for 10 minutes, shaking halfway.
  4. Add the sausage, pepper and onion.
  5. Toss everything together and air fry for a further 12–16 minutes, shaking the basket every 5 minutes.
  6. Check the potatoes are tender and the sausage is piping hot.
  7. Cook in batches if the basket is crowded.
  8. Serve immediately, with mustard or a side salad if desired.

Recipe notes

Home adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Potatoes and Sausage bookmark. Adjust time to potato size and your appliance. Source: https://www.thecountrycook.net/air-fryer-potatoes-and-sausage-meal
