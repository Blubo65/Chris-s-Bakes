Steak Fajitas

Quick strips of steak, peppers and onion tossed with fajita spices and cooked in the air fryer.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g steak,
  • thinly sliced across the grain
  • 2 peppers,
  • sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp fajita seasoning
  • Juice of ½ lime
  • Tortillas and toppings,
  • to serve

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 200°C.
  2. Toss the steak, peppers and onion with olive oil and fajita seasoning.
  3. Arrange in a single layer in the basket; cook in batches if necessary.
  4. Air fry for 7–10 minutes, shaking or stirring halfway, until the vegetables are tender and steak is cooked to your preference.
  5. Squeeze over lime juice and serve in warm tortillas with your favourite toppings.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Steak Fajitas bookmark. Cooking time depends on steak thickness and preferred doneness. Source: https://recipeera.sherecipes.com/air-fryer-steak-fajitas
