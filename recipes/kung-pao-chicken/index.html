Kung Pao Chicken

A spicy-savoury Chinese-inspired stir-fry with chicken, peppers, peanuts and a glossy soy-vinegar sauce.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • diced
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce,
  • divided
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar or honey
  • 100 ml chicken stock or water
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 3 garlic cloves,
  • chopped
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • ½ tsp chilli flakes,
  • or to taste
  • 50 g unsalted roasted peanuts
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Sliced spring onions,
  • to garnish

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with the cornflour and 1 tbsp soy sauce.
  2. In a separate bowl, mix the remaining soy sauce, rice vinegar, hoisin, sugar and stock.
  3. Heat the oil in a wok or large frying pan.
  4. Stir-fry the chicken in batches until golden and cooked through, then set aside.
  5. Add the pepper and stir-fry for 2–3 minutes.
  6. Add the garlic, ginger and chilli flakes and cook for 30 seconds.
  7. Return the chicken to the pan and pour in the sauce.
  8. Simmer for 2–3 minutes until glossy and thickened.
  9. Stir through the peanuts and garnish with spring onions.
  10. Serve with rice.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Kung Pao Chicken recipe; not a verbatim reproduction. Adjust chilli to taste. Source: https://natashaskitchen.com/kung-pao-chicken
