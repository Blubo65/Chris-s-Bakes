One-Pot Cowboy Rice

A hearty one-pot meal with smoky sausage, rice, baked beans and barbecue sauce.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g smoked sausage,
  • sliced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 200 g long-grain rice,
  • rinsed
  • 1 x 400 g tin baked beans
  • 2 tbsp barbecue sauce
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 500 ml chicken stock,
  • hot
  • 1 tbsp oil,
  • if needed
  • Black pepper,
  • to taste

Method

  1. Heat a large lidded pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the sliced sausage and cook for 3–4 minutes until lightly browned, adding a little oil only if needed.
  3. Add the onion and pepper and cook for 4–5 minutes until starting to soften.
  4. Stir in the garlic and smoked paprika for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the rinsed rice, baked beans, barbecue sauce and hot stock.
  6. Stir well and bring to a gentle simmer.
  7. Cover and cook on low for 18–22 minutes, stirring once or twice to prevent sticking, until the rice is tender and most of the liquid has been absorbed.
  8. Add a splash of hot water if it becomes too dry before the rice is cooked.
  9. Turn off the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes.
  10. Fluff the rice, season with black pepper and serve hot.

Recipe notes
