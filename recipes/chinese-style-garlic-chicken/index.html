Chinese-Style Garlic Chicken

Tender chicken and vegetables in a glossy garlic-soy sauce, served with rice.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 red pepper, sliced
  • 150 g broccoli florets
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce, optional,
  • 150 ml chicken stock
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp cold water

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with 1 tbsp cornflour until lightly coated.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok or large frying pan.
  3. Stir-fry the chicken in batches until cooked through; set aside.
  4. Add the pepper and broccoli and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes, adding a splash of water if needed to soften the broccoli.
  5. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 30 seconds.
  6. Stir in soy sauce, oyster sauce if using, stock and honey.
  7. Bring to a simmer, then add the cornflour slurry and stir until glossy.
  8. Return the chicken to the pan and toss until piping hot.
  9. Serve with rice.

Recipe notes
