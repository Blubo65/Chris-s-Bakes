Bacon, Onion & Potato Bake

Layers of potatoes, onions and smoky bacon baked until tender with a golden cheesy top.

Time: 1 hr · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 800 g potatoes,
  • thinly sliced
  • 150 g smoked bacon, chopped
  • 2 onions,
  • thinly sliced
  • 250 ml chicken stock
  • 100 ml milk
  • 100 g grated cheddar
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 190°C (fan 170°C).
  2. Lightly grease an ovenproof dish.
  3. Cook the bacon in a frying pan until lightly browned.
  4. Add the onions and cook for 5 minutes until softened.
  5. Layer the sliced potatoes with the bacon and onion mixture in the dish, seasoning with pepper and thyme between layers.
  6. Pour over the stock and milk.
  7. Cover with foil and bake for 35–40 minutes until the potatoes are nearly tender.
  8. Remove the foil, scatter with cheese and bake for a further 15–20 minutes until golden and bubbling.
  9. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.
