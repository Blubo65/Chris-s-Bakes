Cashew Chicken

Chicken pieces in a savoury soy-garlic sauce with crunchy cashews and peppers.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • cut into chunks
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 red pepper,
  • diced
  • 75 g unsalted cashews
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 100 ml chicken stock
  • 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp cold water

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with 1 tbsp cornflour and half the oil.
  2. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  3. Arrange chicken in a single layer and air fry for 10–14 minutes, turning halfway, until cooked through and the thickest pieces reach 75°C.
  4. Meanwhile, heat the remaining oil in a pan.
  5. Cook the pepper for 3 minutes, then add garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.
  6. Stir in soy sauce, hoisin, honey and stock.
  7. Simmer for 2 minutes, then add the cornflour slurry and stir until thickened.
  8. Add the cooked chicken and cashews, tossing to coat and heat through.
  9. Serve with rice.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Cashew Chicken bookmark. Check chicken is fully cooked. Source: https://ketosisguide.us/air-fryer-cashew-chicken-1012mj
