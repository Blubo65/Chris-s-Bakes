Vegetable Singapore Noodles

Curry-seasoned rice vermicelli noodles stir-fried with colourful vegetables and soy.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 3

Ingredients

  • 250 g rice vermicelli noodles
  • 1 red pepper,
  • thinly sliced
  • 1 carrot,
  • cut into matchsticks
  • 100 g cabbage,
  • finely shredded
  • 100 g beansprouts
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 spring onions,
  • sliced

Method

  1. Prepare the noodles according to packet instructions, drain and set aside.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok or large frying pan.
  3. Add the carrot, pepper and cabbage and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes.
  4. Add the garlic and curry powder and stir for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the noodles, beansprouts and soy sauce.
  6. Toss over high heat until piping hot and evenly coated.
  7. Garnish with spring onions and serve immediately.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked vegetarian adaptation inspired by the saved Singapore Noodles bookmarks. Source: https://www.slimmingeats.com/blog/chicken-singapore-noodles
