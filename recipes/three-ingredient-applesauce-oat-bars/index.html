Three-Ingredient Applesauce Oat Bars

Simple soft oat bars made with applesauce, oats and cinnamon, with no added sugar in the basic version.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 8

Ingredients

  • 250 g unsweetened applesauce
  • 180 g rolled oats
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • A handful of raisins or chopped nuts,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C).
  2. Line a small square baking tin with parchment.
  3. Mix the applesauce, oats and cinnamon in a bowl.
  4. Stir in raisins or chopped nuts if using.
  5. Press the mixture evenly into the tin, smoothing the top.
  6. Bake for 18–22 minutes until set and lightly golden at the edges.
  7. Cool completely in the tin before cutting into bars; they firm up as they cool.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Applesauce Oatmeal Bars recipe. Choose unsweetened applesauce for the no-added-sugar version. Source: https://www.theconsciousplantkitchen.com/applesauce-oatmeal-bars/
