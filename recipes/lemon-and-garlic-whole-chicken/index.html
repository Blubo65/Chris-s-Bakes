Lemon & Garlic Whole Chicken

A whole roast chicken seasoned with lemon, garlic and herbs, cooked in a suitably sized air fryer.

Time: 1 hr 10 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken,
  • about 1.2–1.5 kg,
  • if it fits safely
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon,
  • halved
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Check the chicken fits comfortably in your air fryer with space around it and that your appliance instructions allow whole poultry.
  2. Pat the chicken dry.
  3. Mix the oil, crushed garlic, paprika, thyme, salt and pepper.
  4. Rub over the chicken and place the lemon halves in the cavity.
  5. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  6. Place the chicken breast-side down and cook for 30 minutes.
  7. Carefully turn breast-side up and cook for a further 25–35 minutes, depending on size.
  8. If the skin browns too quickly, reduce the temperature slightly.
  9. Check the thickest part of the breast and thigh reaches at least 75°C and juices run clear.
  10. Continue cooking in short increments if needed.
  11. Rest for 10–15 minutes before carving.

Recipe notes

Adapted home method inspired by the saved Air Fryer Lemon Garlic Whole Chicken video. Only cook a whole chicken if it fits safely in your model; confirm 75°C in the thickest parts. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T9k-16Y6SwI
