Garlic Butter Steak Bites

Tender, bite-sized steak pieces tossed in melted garlic butter and herbs.

Time: 10–15 mins · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 400g sirloin or rump steak,
  • cut into 2–3cm cubes
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 25g butter
  • 2 garlic cloves,
  • finely minced
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • A squeeze of lemon (optional)

Method

  1. Pat the steak cubes dry.
  2. Toss with the olive oil, paprika, salt and pepper.
  3. Preheat the air fryer to 200°C.
  4. Arrange the steak in a single layer and cook for about 5–8 minutes, shaking halfway through.
  5. Timing depends on cube size and desired doneness; check early and use a food thermometer if needed.
  6. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a small pan over low heat.
  7. Add the garlic and cook gently for 30–60 seconds without letting it brown.
  8. Stir in the parsley and optional lemon juice.
  9. Toss the cooked steak bites in the garlic butter and serve immediately.
  10. Allow the steak to rest briefly before serving.
