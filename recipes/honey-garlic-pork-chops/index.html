Honey Garlic Pork Chops

Pan-seared pork chops finished in a sticky honey, garlic and soy sauce.

Time: 25 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless pork chops
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 4 garlic cloves,
  • finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar or lemon juice
  • 100 ml chicken stock

Method

  1. Pat the pork chops dry and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan.
  3. Sear the chops for 3–5 minutes per side, depending on thickness, until browned.
  4. Transfer to a plate.
  5. Reduce the heat and add the garlic.
  6. Cook for 30 seconds, then stir in the honey, soy sauce, vinegar or lemon juice and stock.
  7. Simmer for 2 minutes, then return the chops and any juices to the pan.
  8. Turn them in the sauce and cook gently until the centre reaches at least 63°C, followed by a 3-minute rest.
  9. Spoon the glaze over the chops and serve with rice or vegetables.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Honey Garlic Pork Chops bookmark. Use a thermometer and rest pork for 3 minutes after reaching 63°C. Source: https://rasamalaysia.com/honey-garlic-pork-chops
