Diet Cola Chicken

A sweet-savoury chicken dish simmered in a tangy tomato and diet cola sauce.

Time: 35 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast,
  • cut into strips
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 red pepper, sliced
  • 330 ml diet cola
  • 200 g passata
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp garlic granules
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan.
  2. Cook the onion and pepper for 4–5 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the chicken and cook until the outside is no longer pink.
  4. Stir in the passata, tomato purée, diet cola, soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, garlic granules and paprika.
  5. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce reduces and becomes sticky and the chicken is cooked through.
  6. Season with black pepper and serve with rice or vegetables.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Diet Cola Chicken recipes; not a verbatim reproduction. Simmer until the sauce is reduced and chicken is fully cooked.
