Crispy Chickpeas

Crunchy roasted chickpeas seasoned with paprika and garlic, a simple savoury snack.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 x 400 g tin chickpeas,
  • drained and rinsed
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • ¼ tsp ground cumin, optional,
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Drain and rinse the chickpeas, then pat very dry with a clean tea towel.
  2. Remove any loose skins if convenient.
  3. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  4. Toss the chickpeas with olive oil, paprika, garlic granules, cumin if using, salt and pepper.
  5. Spread in a single layer in the basket and air fry for 12–18 minutes, shaking every 4–5 minutes, until crisp.
  6. Let them cool for a few minutes; they become crunchier as they cool.
  7. Eat the same day for the best texture.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved Air Fryer Crispy Chickpeas bookmark. Drying the chickpeas well is key to crispness. Source: https://sweetpeasandsaffron.com/crispy-air-fryer-chickpeas/
