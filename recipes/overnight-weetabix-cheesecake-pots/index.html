Overnight Weetabix Cheesecake Pots

Layered overnight Weetabix with yoghurt and a creamy cheesecake-style topping.

Time: 10 mins plus chilling · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 2 Weetabix biscuits
  • 100 ml milk
  • 200 g thick Greek yoghurt
  • 100 g light cream cheese
  • 1–2 tsp honey or sweetener,
  • to taste
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • 100 g berries or chopped fruit

Method

  1. Crumble one Weetabix into each of two jars or bowls.
  2. Pour half the milk over each portion and press into a compact base.
  3. Add a little more milk if needed to soften.
  4. Mix the yoghurt, cream cheese, honey or sweetener and vanilla until smooth.
  5. Spoon the cheesecake mixture over the softened Weetabix bases.
  6. Top with berries or chopped fruit, cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight.
  7. Serve chilled.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved overnight Weetabix cheesecake video. Keep refrigerated and consume within 2 days. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Qtyqsadh-E
