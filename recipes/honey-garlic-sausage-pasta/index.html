Honey Garlic Sausage Pasta

Sausage rounds in a sticky honey-garlic glaze tossed through creamy, cheesy pasta.

Time: 25–30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 6 lean pork sausages
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 3 garlic cloves,
  • finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • Pinch of chilli flakes
  • 300g bucatini or other pasta
  • 120g light cream cheese
  • 100g grated Red Leicester cheese
  • 2 tbsp water,
  • plus a little reserved pasta water
  • Black pepper,
  • to taste

Method

  1. Slice the sausages into rounds.
  2. Heat half the oil in a frying pan and brown the sausages on one side; add the remaining oil, turn and brown the other side.
  3. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in boiling water according to the packet instructions.
  4. Before draining, reserve a mug of pasta water.
  5. Add the garlic to the browned sausages and cook for 1 minute.
  6. Stir in the honey, soy sauce, chilli flakes and 2 tbsp water.
  7. Let the sauce bubble and reduce until glossy and the sausages are coated.
  8. Return the drained pasta to the pan.
  9. Stir through the cream cheese and grated Red Leicester, adding a splash of reserved pasta water at a time until creamy.
  10. Fold in the sticky sausages and serve hot.
