Baked pear and berry breakfast soufflé

Honey-roasted pears and summer berries baked in a lightly spiced, fluffy egg mixture.

Ingredients

  • Low-calorie cooking spray
  • 4 medium Conference pears, halved and cored
  • 1 level tbsp clear honey
  • 5 eggs, separated
  • 1 level tbsp sweetener granules
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ½ tsp ground ginger
  • 275g mixed berries, such as raspberries, blueberries and halved strawberries
  • ½ level tsp icing sugar, to serve
  • Fat-free natural yogurt or fromage frais, to serve

Method

  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C/fan 160°C/gas 4.
  2. Lightly spray a large non-stick baking tray with low-calorie cooking spray and add the pear halves, cut-side up.
  3. Drizzle with the honey and bake for 15 minutes.
  4. Put the egg whites in a large bowl and, using an electric whisk, whisk until thick, frothy and holding their shape.
  5. Add the sweetener and whisk again until thick and glossy, then whisk in the cinnamon and ginger. Lightly beat the egg yolks and fold them in.
  6. Remove the pears from the tin, then spoon the egg mixture into the tray and spread evenly.
  7. Push the pear halves into the egg mixture, scatter with the berries and bake for 20 minutes or until lightly risen and golden. Dust with icing sugar and serve with yogurt or fromage frais.
