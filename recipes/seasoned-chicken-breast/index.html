Seasoned Chicken Breast

Juicy air fryer chicken breast with a simple paprika, garlic and herb seasoning.

Time: 20 mins · Makes 2

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts,
  • similar size
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic granules
  • ½ tsp dried mixed herbs
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 190°C.
  2. Pat the chicken breasts dry.
  3. If very thick, gently flatten the thicker end for more even cooking.
  4. Mix the oil, paprika, garlic granules, herbs, salt and pepper.
  5. Rub over both sides of the chicken.
  6. Place in the air fryer basket in a single layer.
  7. Cook for 12–18 minutes depending on thickness, turning halfway.
  8. Check the thickest part reaches 75°C internally.
  9. Continue cooking in short increments if needed.
  10. Rest for 3–5 minutes before slicing.

Recipe notes

Home adaptation inspired by the saved air fryer chicken breast recipe. Thickness and appliance affect cooking time; use a thermometer to confirm doneness. Source: https://dinnerthendessert.com/air-fryer-chicken-breast/
