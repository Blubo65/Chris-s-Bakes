Ginger Soy Meatballs

Juicy beef meatballs glazed with ginger, soy and honey, served with crunchy slaw or rice.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g lean beef mince
  • 1 egg
  • 40 g breadcrumbs
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 150 ml water or stock
  • 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp cold water
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Sliced spring onions,
  • to garnish

Method

  1. Mix the mince with the egg, breadcrumbs, half the garlic, half the ginger and a little pepper.
  2. Shape into 16 meatballs.
  3. Heat the oil in a frying pan and brown the meatballs on all sides.
  4. Cover and cook gently for 8–10 minutes until cooked through, then set aside.
  5. Add the remaining garlic and ginger to the pan and cook for 30 seconds.
  6. Stir in the soy sauce, honey, vinegar and water or stock.
  7. Bring to a simmer, stir in the cornflour slurry and cook until the sauce thickens.
  8. Return the meatballs to the pan and toss until glazed and piping hot.
  9. Garnish with spring onions and serve with rice or slaw.

Recipe notes
