Oklahoma Onion Burgers

Thin smashed beef burgers with caramelised shaved onions, melted cheese and toasted brioche buns.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg boneless beef short ribs, minced
  • 4 small white onions, shaved very thinly
  • Salt, to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • Brioche burger buns, toasted
  • American-style cheese slices (or mild, very melty cheese)

Method

  1. Cut the short ribs into roughly 4 cm chunks and freeze for 15 minutes. Mince using a food processor or meat grinder, pulsing in batches until the beef has a loose mince texture.
  2. Divide the beef into approximately 70 g portions. Roll into balls and gently work until they hold together. Flatten between parchment to 12–15 cm rounds, thinner at the edges, and refrigerate until cooking.
  3. Peel the onions and shave into the thinnest possible rings, preferably using a mandoline.
  4. Preheat a large cast-iron or nonstick pan over medium-high heat.
  5. Add the patties, season with salt and pepper and pile approximately 1/2 cup shaved onions on each. Press each patty down firmly for at least 10 seconds.
  6. Once the undersides have browned and caramelised, flip the patties onion-side down and cook for about 20 seconds, then add a slice of cheese to half of the patties.
  7. Place the bottom buns on top of the patties, then the top buns on the bottom buns, allowing the buns to steam and warm as the patties finish cooking. Total burger cooking time is approximately 2–2½ minutes.
  8. Remove the buns, flip the patties so the onion sides face up, stack a plain patty on top of each cheese-topped patty and place into the buns.
