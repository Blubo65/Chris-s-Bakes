Moroccan Roasted Potatoes

Golden oven-roasted potatoes tossed with warming Moroccan-style spices and fresh coriander.

Ingredients

  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1–2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 lb (about 1.36 kg) potatoes, cut into 1-inch (2.5 cm) chunks
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh coriander

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 230°C (450°F).
  2. In a small bowl, mix the cumin, turmeric, cinnamon, cayenne pepper, paprika, garlic powder, salt, pepper and olive oil.
  3. Add the cubed potatoes and stir gently to coat with the oil and spices.
  4. Spread the potatoes in a single layer on a lightly greased baking sheet.
  5. Roast for 20–25 minutes, stirring once halfway through, until golden brown.
  6. Toss with the chopped fresh coriander before serving and adjust the seasoning if needed.
