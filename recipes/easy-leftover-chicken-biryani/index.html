Easy Leftover Chicken Biryani

A quick one-pot biryani-style rice dish using cooked chicken, spices and vegetables.

Time: 30 mins · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 300 g cooked chicken,
  • shredded
  • 300 g basmati rice,
  • rinsed
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp mild curry powder or biryani spice mix
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • 600 ml chicken stock,
  • hot
  • 100 g frozen peas
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh coriander,
  • optional

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large lidded pan.
  2. Cook the onion for 5 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder and cumin and stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the rinsed rice and stir to coat in the spices.
  5. Pour in the hot stock and bring to a simmer.
  6. Cover and cook on low for 10–12 minutes, adding the peas for the final 5 minutes.
  7. Avoid stirring frequently while the rice cooks.
  8. Once the rice is tender and the liquid absorbed, fold through the cooked chicken and heat until piping hot.
  9. If needed, add a splash of stock and cover for a few more minutes.
  10. Season to taste, rest covered for 5 minutes and garnish with coriander if desired.

Recipe notes

Home-cooked adaptation inspired by the saved easy leftover chicken biryani link. Use freshly cooked or safely chilled leftover chicken and reheat until piping hot. Source: https://www.easypeasy-lemonsqueezy.co.uk/chicken-biryani-easy
