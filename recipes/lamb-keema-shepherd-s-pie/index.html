Lamb Keema Shepherd’s Pie

A shepherd’s pie with a spiced lamb mince filling inspired by keema curry, topped with mashed potato.

Time: 1 hr · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 500 g lamb mince
  • 800 g potatoes,
  • peeled and chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 300 ml lamb or beef stock
  • 100 g frozen peas
  • 25 g butter
  • Splash of milk
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes until tender, then drain and mash with butter, milk, salt and pepper.
  3. Meanwhile, brown the lamb mince in a large pan.
  4. Add the onion and cook for 4–5 minutes.
  5. Stir in the garlic, ginger, curry powder, cumin and tomato purée.
  6. Cook for 30 seconds.
  7. Add the stock and simmer for 15–20 minutes until the filling thickens.
  8. Stir in the peas and season to taste.
  9. Spoon the filling into an ovenproof dish, top with mashed potato and rough the surface with a fork.
  10. Bake for 20–25 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  11. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Recipe notes
