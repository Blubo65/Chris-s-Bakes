Roasted Cauliflower Cheese Lasagne

A comforting vegetarian lasagne layered with roasted cauliflower, tomato sauce and creamy cheese sauce.

Time: 1 hr · Makes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower,
  • cut into small florets
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 x 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 garlic clove,
  • crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 6–8 lasagne sheets
  • 30 g butter
  • 30 g plain flour
  • 500 ml milk
  • 100 g grated cheddar
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (fan 180°C).
  2. Toss the cauliflower with olive oil, salt and pepper and roast for 20 minutes until lightly browned.
  3. Meanwhile, simmer the chopped tomatoes with garlic and oregano for 10 minutes to make a quick sauce.
  4. For the cheese sauce, melt the butter, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  5. Gradually whisk in the milk and simmer until thickened.
  6. Stir in half the cheese and season.
  7. Spread a little tomato sauce in an ovenproof dish.
  8. Layer lasagne sheets, roasted cauliflower, tomato sauce and cheese sauce, repeating until used up.
  9. Finish with cheese sauce and the remaining grated cheese.
  10. Bake for 25–30 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  11. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

Recipe notes
