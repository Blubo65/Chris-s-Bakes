Chinese-Style Gammon Joint

Quick sticky Chinese-style gammon with honey, soy sauce and Chinese five-spice. Delicious hot or cold.

Time: About 50–70 mins · Makes Serves 4–6

Ingredients

  • 750g–1kg boneless gammon joint
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp Chinese five-spice
  • 1 tsp Chinese rice vinegar (optional)
  • 1 garlic clove,
  • crushed (optional)

Method

  1. If the gammon is very salty, soak it in cold water first, following the packet advice.
  2. Pat dry.
  3. Mix the honey, soy sauce, Chinese five-spice, rice vinegar and garlic to make the glaze.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 160°C.
  5. Place the gammon in a suitable heatproof dish or foil tray and cover loosely with foil.
  6. Air fry for about 40–50 minutes for a 750g–1kg joint, depending on its shape and thickness.
  7. Remove the foil, brush with the glaze and cook uncovered for another 10–20 minutes, brushing again if desired.
  8. Keep an eye on the honey so it does not burn.
  9. Check the centre reaches 70°C for 2 minutes with a food thermometer, or follow the gammon packet instructions.
  10. Rest for 10 minutes before slicing.

Recipe notes

Serve with egg-fried rice, noodles or air-fried broccoli. Lovely sliced cold in sandwiches. Cooking times are approximate and depend on joint thickness and the air fryer; use a food thermometer to check the centre.
